## Autopilot world (CS)

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**The goal is physical superiority: sport instead of cinema and dates.**

- **How it develops:** Parents get children into sport from childhood. The new family is a running community. They said offline is the new luxury, and a new offline has appeared: sport. Sport, nutrition and “food from a tube” grow: eating to level up.
- **The price:** Overwork and competition: “higher, stronger, faster, but we come home exhausted”. Many children burn out and get injured by 13.
- **Signal:** A Spanish football club: around 50 children's schools, each with three sub-leagues, so every match is a celebration for any league (the host's observation).
- **Heard at the game:** “The old offline is gone; a new one has appeared: sport”
- **Step for this week:** Choose sport as a way of life, not as competition with others.

## Autonomy world (DS)

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Not automating in order to rest, but doing what you love.**

- **How it develops:** Freed time goes to fundamentals, not entertainment, so you can move freely from one activity to another. Activities alternate so that each both moves you forward and gives you rest. First you need to understand what you like; many people don't know.
- **The price:** People hand work to AI and then fill their free time, out of anxiety. Those who “worked until retirement” and stopped fade quickly.
- **Signal:** About 70% of people who expect new emotions from travel don't get them (statistics according to the host).
- **Heard at the game:** “Not how to automate so you do nothing, but how to start doing what you love”
- **Step for this week:** Write down what the last hour AI saved you was spent on.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**The silent majority decides** (CS + DE). The majority think they decide nothing, yet every product is made to be “swallowed” by the majority. If it wanted to understand AI, everyone would be building mass education. Right now it's tired and wants “sort it out for us”.

- **Paradox:** Silence is also a choice, and it becomes the world's norm.
- **Experiment for a week:** Say out loud what you want from technology, not only what you fear.
