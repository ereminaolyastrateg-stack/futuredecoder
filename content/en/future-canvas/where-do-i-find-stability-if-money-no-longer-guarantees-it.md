**What will give a sense of security, if not money?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

[Open in the interactive canvas](/static/polotno/en/#q-sc36oi) · [Challenge a friend to a duel of worlds](/static/polotno/en/#new-sc36oi)

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Belonging to the system: as long as you're connected, you're safe.**

- **How it develops:** Money is secondary: it distributes itself across accounts, taxes and loans are paid automatically. Security lies in being needed by the system and not standing out. There's plenty of comfort, and it takes away independence.
- **The price:** Get disconnected from the systems and you're not safe.
- **Signal:** Marketplaces split infrastructure into small pick-up points, but control stays with them.
- **Heard at the game:** “Disconnected from the systems means not safe”
- **Step for this week:** Look at which systems hold your life together (bank, job, services) and pick one you could leave without loss.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**Money is capital for building your own life kit.**

- **How it develops:** Money remains one of the main sources of security. Convenient automation exists, but it isn't imposed; you have to pay for it. I assemble my life like a construction kit, and money is my parts.
- **The price:** Nothing to pay with, and you'll quickly switch to subsistence farming.
- **Signal:** Assembling furniture yourself: “want it fast, assemble it yourself”.
- **Heard at the game:** “Nothing to pay with, and you'll quickly switch to subsistence farming”
- **Step for this week:** Work out how much the convenience you use each month costs and decide what you're ready to give up.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Your own capability: I can look after myself.**

- **How it develops:** Security lies in what I can do myself and in owning my tools. I own things, but the infrastructure is shared: “use mine, and I'll use yours”. Owning a flat isn't necessarily the goal.
- **The price:** It's all on you: if you can't build a system, autonomy turns into loneliness.
- **Signal:** Portable furniture for short-term renters: a whole life in a suitcase.
- **Heard at the game:** “Security is your own capability and independence”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one thing a service currently does for you and learn to do it yourself.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Belonging to a community: not a hundred roubles but a hundred friends.**

- **How it develops:** There's little money, but there's exchange and local resources. Security lies in strong small ties: if something happens, people are nearby. The community will tell you if something is wrong.
- **The price:** Without the community you're nobody, and it's hard to leave.
- **Signal:** The Five Elements festival: people gather around shared practices.
- **Heard at the game:** “Not a hundred roubles, but a hundred friends”
- **Step for this week:** Think of three people you could turn to in trouble, and do something for one of them this week.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Elders want to give** (Все). The experience of elders could speed up the young, but there's nowhere to pass it on.

- **Paradox:** Elders have the desire to give; the young don't yet have the desire to receive.
- **Experiment for a week:** Listen to your child's or a younger colleague's playlist and discuss it honestly.
