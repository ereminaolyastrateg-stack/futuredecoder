## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Through purchases and experiences.**

- **How it develops:** Here happiness is bought and displayed: experiences, things, status. The seller decides what counts as happiness.
- **The price:** If they stop selling, an empty life without holidays is left.
- **Signal:** The experience economy: people buy experiences rather than things.
- **Heard at the game:** “We explain to ourselves that we live well through attributes”
- **Step for this week:** Recall your last three purchases made to lift your mood. Which still makes you happy?

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**First by display, later by the right to be unnoticed.**

- **How it develops:** For now the norm is to display: tickets, runs, holidays. The new norm is a “world of the invisible”: you can stay unnoticed and choose your own norms.
- **The price:** To earn money you still have to be visible.
- **Signal:** Veblen describes conspicuous consumption: status is shown through visible spending.
- **Heard at the game:** “Do you climb the mountain to see the world, or so the world sees you?”
- **Step for this week:** For a week, post nothing about yourself and see how you feel.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Not at all: happiness loves quiet.**

- **How it develops:** Happiness loves quiet. It's in your body, and there's no need to prove it to others.
- **The price:** If you withdraw completely, ties with people weaken.
- **Signal:** Phones start helping people limit their own use.
- **Heard at the game:** “Happiness loves quiet”
- **Step for this week:** Find one activity you do only for yourself and never show anyone.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**By sharing it with others.**

- **How it develops:** Happiness isn't shown but shared: singing together at a stadium, eating at one table, helping neighbours.
- **The price:** You have to be part of something shared, and that takes time and energy.
- **Signal:** Bikers come to court to silently support children giving testimony.
- **Heard at the game:** “A state in process”
- **Step for this week:** Go where people do something together: a choir, a community clean-up, a shared dinner.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Quiet happiness or visibility** (DS + CE). Happiness loves quiet, but to earn money you have to be visible.

- **Paradox:** I want to quietly tend my garden, and have everyone know me and pay a lot
- **Experiment for a week:** For a week, separate where you must be visible and where you can disappear.

**Focus on yourself or on connections** (DS + DE). Focusing on your body and state brings happiness, but weakens the ties through which everything else comes.

- **Paradox:** How do you stay part of the world while focusing on yourself?
- **Experiment for a week:** Do one thing for yourself and one for someone else on the same day, and compare how it feels.

**State or process** (DE + DS). Happiness is a human quality that shows itself through processes and environment.

- **Paradox:** Is happiness a state or a process? Internal or external?
