**What will people turn to when nothing brings joy?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Quick fixes from outside.**

- **How it develops:** The fastest way is from outside: a mood pill, a purchase, a TV series. It works immediately.
- **The price:** This is maximum dependence: stop taking it and everything collapses.
- **Signal:** Products appear that noticeably change emotional state.
- **Heard at the game:** “Pills mean raising dependence to the maximum”
- **Step for this week:** If something external helps you, decide in advance on the dose and what your next step without it will be.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**Collective goals: “endure it for the common good”.**

- **How it develops:** The centre offers ready-made sources: collective goals, clubs, movements. “Everyone has it hard now; endure it for the common good.”
- **The price:** Imposed suffering: you're asked to endure instead of looking for your own thing.
- **Signal:** Korean management: set unattainable goals and praise effort; people live at work and the economy grows.
- **Heard at the game:** “For a bright future we have to endure a little longer”
- **Step for this week:** Ask yourself: which of your duties rests only on “just endure it”?

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**The body and unexpected sources.**

- **How it develops:** Look in unexpected places and through the body: sculpt your stress out of plasticine, spend money on someone else rather than yourself. The body already knows how to make happiness; it just needs you not to get in the way.
- **The price:** You'll have to learn to hear yourself again.
- **Signal:** People are invited to build their stress out of objects: a person makes a visible model of their stress and discusses it with others.
- **Heard at the game:** “The body is an artefact of happiness”
- **Step for this week:** Take plasticine or any objects and in 10 minutes build a model of what is weighing on you now.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Doing things for others.**

- **How it develops:** Acting for others helps: neighbourly mutual aid, a shared project. Spending on others brings more joy than spending on yourself.
- **The price:** It works only if you do it for real, not to tick a box.
- **Signal:** In an experiment, spending on others produced more positive emotions than spending on oneself.
- **Heard at the game:** “You decide: sit and suffer at home, or go and help a neighbour”
- **Step for this week:** Do one small thing for a neighbour or a stranger this week.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Quiet happiness or visibility** (ДС + ЦЗ). Happiness loves quiet, but to earn money you have to be visible.

- **Paradox:** I want to quietly tend my garden, and have everyone know me and pay a lot
- **Experiment for a week:** For a week, separate where you must be visible and where you can disappear.

**Focus on yourself or on connections** (ДС + ДЗ). Focusing on your body and state brings happiness, but weakens the ties through which everything else comes.

- **Paradox:** How do you stay part of the world while focusing on yourself?
- **Experiment for a week:** Do one thing for yourself and one for someone else on the same day, and compare how it feels.
