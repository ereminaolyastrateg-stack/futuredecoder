**How will people tell a real specialist when everything is beautifully packaged?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

[Open in the interactive canvas](/static/polotno/en/#q-eg4owr) · [Challenge a friend to a duel of worlds](/static/polotno/en/#new-eg4owr)

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**An algorithm judges, and the key skill becomes pleasing it.**

- **How it develops:** You used to have to please your boss; now it's the algorithm, and it's unclear how it judges. Systems dismiss people by efficiency and predict who will leave. I have five digital copies in different tones: for the boss, the client, the family.
- **The price:** Nobody is responsible; it's all on the algorithm, and people are disoriented. Shadow services appear: hack the system, get removed from a list.
- **Signal:** Predictive HR analytics: algorithms try to predict whether an employee will quit (a recruiter participant's experience).
- **Heard at the game:** “It used to be pleasing the boss; now it's pleasing the algorithm”
- **Step for this week:** Think about which metrics you're actually judged by, and compare them with what you consider important yourself.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**People trust results: whoever did in two weeks what the team couldn't in two months.**

- **How it develops:** Big companies hire a solo practitioner for what their staff can't do. Then the staff come to learn: “How did you do that?” Those with their own product and methodology survive, not just a service.
- **The price:** The entry threshold keeps falling and rising: big AI companies are already teaming up with consulting giants.
- **Signal:** Big AI companies are already teaming up with consulting giants (observation of a consultant participant).
- **Heard at the game:** “They hire me for what their own staff can't do”
- **Step for this week:** Describe one of your results in numbers: how much time and money it saved.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**What's checked is genuine experience, not the packaging: explain it without slides.**

- **How it develops:** Everyone speaks beautifully; CVs and presentations no longer mean anything. A good specialist can explain their system for three hours without a slide, and the answers to questions immediately show their level. Accumulated, confirmed experience is valued: the client publishes what work was done, without ratings, for others to judge.
- **The price:** Confirmation is already an assessment: someone has to vouch. It's hard to deceive for long, but possible at first.
- **Signal:** At a three-day training all the speakers had identical AI-made slides; those who didn't need slides stood out (a participant's experience).
- **Heard at the game:** “Word of mouth is moving to blockchain radio”
- **Step for this week:** Tell the story of one of your projects out loud, without slides, so it's clear what you yourself did.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**People trust the living and their own: word of mouth, taste, community.**

- **How it develops:** Live contact and human services become more expensive and more valuable. Entrepreneurs are talking about taste again: AI doesn't understand what it's doing, so it can't speak about taste. A startup does something local rather than global, for its own community whose taste it shares.
- **The price:** Trust is a skill almost nobody has; a world built on trust in people requires work on yourself.
- **Signal:** Entrepreneurs are talking about taste again.
- **Heard at the game:** “Trust is a skill”
- **Step for this week:** Find one person you trust in your field and ask what that trust rests on.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**There's no universal skill; there's a “why”** (Все). Only one thing is universal: understanding what you want to do and forming your own image of the future. It works like a flag: “I'm going there; whoever's with me, I'll help.” AI will draft any plan, but implementing it and finding the time is up to you.

- **Paradox:** We look for a skill when what's missing is direction.
- **Experiment for a week:** Describe in one paragraph the future you're building, and show it to one person.

**Automating in order to rest is a trap** (Все). Many want to automate everything so they can start resting. But “save up and get out” doesn't work: after six months you're bored, and whoever stops fades. The question isn't “how to do nothing” but “how to do what you love”.

- **Paradox:** Freedom from tasks without work of your own turns into emptiness.
- **Experiment for a week:** Instead of “what else could I automate”, ask “what would I do if money weren't an issue”.

**Not everything fashionable is something we need** (ЦС + ДС). Companies roll out AI because “everyone is”, without understanding why; travel is the same. You can't be autonomous in everything: you need to understand where autonomy is really needed and where you can rely on others.

- **Paradox:** Freedom of choice without understanding your own desires leads you to follow trends.
- **Experiment for a week:** Pick one fashionable decision in your life and ask: why is it for me specifically?
