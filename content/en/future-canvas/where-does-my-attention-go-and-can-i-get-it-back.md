**How will human attention work in ten years, and who will control it?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Attention is the platforms' fuel, and the person is a battery.**

- **How it develops:** Platforms have already filled all the space, and advertising has stopped working. Next they'll make people create: you film yourself, watch yourself, pay yourself, with your life and attention. Streaming will become a mandatory payment, like rent.
- **The price:** Fatigue, disappointment, shame: “reels until 2 a.m. again”, “bought a load of junk”. If you can't manage information, it manages you.
- **Signal:** Companies bring back dopamine as the hormone of anticipation: teasers in advance, limits, “the condensed milk arrives in two weeks” (the host's example).
- **Heard at the game:** “Attention is the resource, and we give it away”
- **Step for this week:** Over one evening, count how many times you opened a feed without meaning to.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**Attention is directed towards a shared past and a shared duty.**

- **How it develops:** Values and traditions are broadcast through content and art; nostalgia becomes a tool. The personal gives way to the shared. The routine is taking part in public affairs: volunteering, community service, “being on some register”, belonging to an approved club.
- **The price:** Attention goes into the past, with nothing new or rethought there. If you don't help anywhere, it's “not great”.
- **Signal:** Nostalgia for the music of your twenties: people return to what they listened to while their mental model was still updating (the host's thought).
- **Heard at the game:** “Pay your debt to society”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one shared effort you take part in because you want to, not because you “have to”.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Attention goes to emotions and worlds you build yourself.**

- **How it develops:** Surprise me, carnival, emotional swings, virtual worlds and new devices. The host sees Minecraft here: creating together with people, but inside your own world. Subscribing to authors is partial autonomy: you chose whom to trust and then read their focus.
- **The price:** It has to be brighter every time. Choice is delegated: “surprise me” means you're not the one choosing.
- **Signal:** Trips “to an unknown destination” and blind-date books: the “surprise me” trend as delegated choice (the host's observation).
- **Heard at the game:** “I delegated my mindfulness to authors”
- **Step for this week:** Notice where you're waiting to be surprised instead of choosing yourself.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Attention as a skill: you notice where it goes and choose yourself.**

- **How it develops:** People keep account of what they spend attention on and what they get. A “life digitisation indicator” is possible: 86% means it's time to get back down to earth. Attention is spent not on emotions but on understanding: first-year students read new laws themselves because “this is now their life”. Some people go off the radar: their own hobby, their own circle.
- **The price:** It takes effort and honesty with yourself. It's easy to go to the extreme; giving up digital entirely gets tiresome too.
- **Signal:** Some people are already protecting themselves from overload: paying to remove ads, turning off notifications; the host sees this as the first sign the problem will pass.
- **Heard at the game:** “Either I'm on my phone or in my head”
- **Step for this week:** At the end of the day, write down what your attention went to and what of it stayed with you.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Don't rewrite the scenario; play inside it** (Все). If we dislike something in a quadrant and remake it to suit ourselves, we assemble a single scenario again. Better to find the actor who decides there and think from their position, for example as a streaming company's board of directors.

- **Paradox:** We try to defend ourselves everywhere, and see only one version of the future.
- **Experiment for a week:** Take a trend you dislike and describe it from the position of whoever benefits from it.
