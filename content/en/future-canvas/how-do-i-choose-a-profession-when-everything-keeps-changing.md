## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**The system, based on test results.**

- **How it develops:** The system chose for me: tests showed where I do best and sent me there. Fast and convenient.
- **The price:** A step aside is hard, and the gap with other tracks grows. And some try to outrun the system and guess which professions AI will eat.
- **Signal:** A mother won't let her 14-year-old son study medicine: she fears AI will replace doctors by the time he graduates.
- **Heard at the game:** “An automated choice made for you: you were told where you do best, so go there”
- **Step for this week:** Take one career-guidance test and argue with it: what did it miss about you?

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**The state, according to its needs.**

- **How it develops:** The profession is set by need: from above you can see how many doctors, engineers and fitters are needed, and places open accordingly. A mentor guides you along the track.
- **The price:** You're taught to work for the salary on offer.
- **Signal:** Asimov's story “Profession”: people have professions loaded into them by assignment, and the “unfit” are quietly set aside to create something new.
- **Heard at the game:** “You must train them so they work for the salary we offer”
- **Step for this week:** Find out which specialists your city or country is officially looking for now and what they pay.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Nobody: you choose a foundation, not a profession.**

- **How it develops:** I choose a foundation, not a profession: a language, a subject, logic. With it you can move both sideways and up.
- **The price:** Nobody insures you; it's all on me.
- **Signal:** Colombia introduces a shared course on war and peace: a common language so people can discuss problems.
- **Heard at the game:** “Narrow specialisation is outdated”
- **Step for this week:** Write down three skills useful in any profession and choose which to build first.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**What the people and region around you need.**

- **How it develops:** Guessing the job of the future is pointless. I look at who needs whom now: I read the regional agenda, find a shortage, connect it with my interest and look for a mentor. Buckwheat loves hands, not combine harvesters.
- **The price:** The plan is built as you go.
- **Signal:** Wheat was brought where it never grew, and now people are rediscovering and planting local crops.
- **Heard at the game:** “Buckwheat loves hands; it doesn't grow with combine harvesters”
- **Step for this week:** Read your city's news for a month and write down three jobs that lack people.
