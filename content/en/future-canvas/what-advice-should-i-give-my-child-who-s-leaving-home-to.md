**How will advice to children work if parents don't know their world?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

[Open in the interactive canvas](/static/polotno/en/#q-60n6lr) · [Challenge a friend to a duel of worlds](/static/polotno/en/#new-60n6lr)

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**A world of convenient digital services: teach digital hygiene.**

- **How it develops:** All services, including social ones, move into a single window, like a marketplace: chose a school, applied, left a review. The interaction between person and system is automated.
- **The price:** The price is live contact and privacy. You can't get through the answering machine to a human; a choice may turn out to be a choice without a choice.
- **Signal:** The idea of placing state and municipal services on a marketplace (news discussed at the game).
- **Heard at the game:** “Anything that gets online is public”
- **Step for this week:** Discuss with your child which parts of their life are already online and who can see them.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**People look for support outside: teach them to check and stand their ground.**

- **How it develops:** When it's unclear what lies ahead, people go to astrologers, tarot readers and info-gurus for an image of the future. The host: they're the only ones offering ordinary people any image of the future at all, but they give no tools to get there.
- **The price:** The support is flimsy, and decisions are postponed until the “forecast”. A live consultation becomes expensive; everyone else gets an automated one.
- **Signal:** A streaming service launched film collections by horoscope (according to the host).
- **Heard at the game:** “Don't follow the herd, even if you're alone”
- **Step for this week:** Suggest your child check one popular claim against three different sources.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Look for meaning in work and for your people, and get to know yourself along the way.**

- **How it develops:** Work becomes a way to express yourself: people won't work where values diverge. Trust shifts from institutions to people. The routine is getting to know yourself bit by bit through tasks, learning new things, finding people, communities and companies close in spirit, and changing them when you've outgrown them.
- **The price:** It takes a long time, there's no right answer, and the responsibility for the choice is yours.
- **Signal:** The new generation leaves jobs that “aren't a buzz”; work is seen as a contribution, not just survival (discussion at the game).
- **Heard at the game:** “A person wakes up new every morning”
- **Step for this week:** Ask your child not “what will you be” but “what do you want to support”.
