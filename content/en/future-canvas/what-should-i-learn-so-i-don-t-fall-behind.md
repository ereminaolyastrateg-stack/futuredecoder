## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**An AI assessor: it tests you and draws up your plan.**

- **How it develops:** I have an AI assessor. Once a quarter it checks what I can do and draws up my plan itself. I learn not for the company but for my own productivity. The main thing is to tune it so it neither flatters nor scolds.
- **The price:** I sprout skills like a cabbage sprouts leaves, and sometimes I feel like a permanent half-learner.
- **Signal:** The OECD measures adults' ability to solve problems where no method is given and the conditions change along the way.
- **Heard at the game:** “You're like a cabbage, covered all over with different skills”
- **Step for this week:** Ask an AI to test you on three skills once a month and grade you honestly. No compliments.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**A mentor and a track you are guided along.**

- **How it develops:** I don't have to guess: I have a track and a mentor who moves me in time to where I'm needed. The main exam now is to explain in your own words what isn't in the template. And to be smarter than your AI.
- **The price:** I'm not the one who decides what to learn.
- **Signal:** The European Union has set out eight key competences for lifelong learning.
- **Heard at the game:** “Say what isn't written in the template”
- **Step for this week:** Find a programme or a mentor with a clear one-year track and sign up.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**You yourself, on a foundation that works anywhere.**

- **How it develops:** My minimum kit: reading, writing, numeracy and solving problems where conditions change. Plus logic, rhetoric and the ability to balance money and time. I learn a language and a subject, not a profession, so I can ride any elevator.
- **The price:** None of this works without discipline.
- **Signal:** Harvard promotes the idea of a versatile worker instead of narrow specialisation.
- **Heard at the game:** “Be your own carrot”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one basic skill and practise it 20 minutes a day for a whole month. Count the sessions, not the result.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**The people and place around you: you learn what they need right now.**

- **How it develops:** I no longer try to guess the job of the future. I look at who needs whom right now: what my neighbours and my district need. I learn along the way, on live tasks, together with the people around me.
- **The price:** There's no ready-made plan, and everything goes slower.
- **Signal:** Barely literate rural women become engineers through practice: they assemble and repair solar systems in their own homes.
- **Heard at the game:** “Guessing the job of the future is pointless; listen for who needs whom right now”
- **Step for this week:** Ask three people nearby what they're missing, and pick one thing you can learn right on that task.
