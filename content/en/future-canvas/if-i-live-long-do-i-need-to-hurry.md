## Autopilot world (CS)

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**There'll be no overpopulation: the problem isn't long life but that few children are born.**

- **How it develops:** The host: even if the whole current population lives long, there'll be no overpopulation; for that people would have to keep having children, and few want to. A separate question is what people will do until 150: without occupation they'll get on each other's nerves.
- **The price:** Society will have to invent new ways of living for older ages.
- **Signal:** Longevity won't cause overpopulation
- **Step for this week:** Think what you want to be doing at 80, and what you're starting now for it.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**A long life is several cycles: you can take your time and try things.**

- **How it develops:** A participant's example: if you live long, you can calmly start learning a new language, without anxiety that you won't get to use it. Life splits into several cycles, and in each you can be completely different: work, skills, competences.
- **The price:** The host's counterpoint: a limited resource directs attention and forces choice. Without a ticking clock the external motivator disappears.
- **Signal:** A long life as several cycles
- **Heard at the game:** You have four life cycles in which you can be completely different.
- **Step for this week:** Pick one thing you put off “because it's too late”, and start it this week.

## Community world (DE)

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**The pill and the anxiety** (CS + CE). There's a drug against ageing, yet health anxiety only grows.

- **Paradox:** More health data, more anxiety.
- **Experiment for a week:** Don't check your tracker metrics for a week, and see how you feel.

**The health paradox** (CE). The more you invest in health, the more you need to invest; health becomes a burden.

- **Paradox:** The goal of “more health” takes resources from the meaning it was wanted for.
- **Experiment for a week:** Lay out your desired future by time, money, attention and meaning, and check whether the day has enough hours.

**Limited or free time** (CS / DS). A short life forces you to choose; a long one lets you try things, but removes the external motivator.

- **Paradox:** Without a ticking clock, the reason to stop disappears.
- **Experiment for a week:** Start one thing you put off “because it's too late”.
