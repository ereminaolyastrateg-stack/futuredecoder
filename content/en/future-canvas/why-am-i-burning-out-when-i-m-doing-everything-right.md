## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Systems will fix it so you keep working.**

- **How it develops:** The system realised a person is more complex than a machine and added anti-burnout: pools, therapy, clubs without productivity. But the goal is the same: don't run the horse into the ground.
- **The price:** The paradigm hasn't changed: you're repaired so you keep working.
- **Signal:** Financial services start working with anxiety about money.
- **Heard at the game:** “A person is a machine, just one that turned out to be more complex”
- **Step for this week:** Take the perks your job offers and honestly look at what they go to: you, or recovering for the sake of work.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**Cruising speed instead of 110% will become the new norm.**

- **How it develops:** The norm of “giving 110%” is crumbling. The new norm is cruising speed: 75–90% effort, a four-day week, working from “I want to” rather than “I have to”.
- **The price:** The new norm may become a fashion: “What, you're not taking a sabbatical?”
- **Signal:** The four-day work week improved a number of indicators.
- **Heard at the game:** “If you give 100–110%, the system is overstretched and any sneeze will break it”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one task and do it at 80%, then see what changes.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**It will go away when happiness stops being the goal.**

- **How it develops:** Burnout is a sign you're chasing happiness as a goal. Here happiness is a side effect: when you devote yourself to something of your own, it comes by itself.
- **The price:** You need to find something to devote yourself to, and sometimes grit your teeth.
- **Signal:** A strong drive to be happy sometimes backfires.
- **Heard at the game:** “Chasing happiness leads to a dead end: you turn into a car standing in a garage”
- **Step for this week:** Ask yourself: what are you doing this for, apart from money and metrics?

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**People will deal with it together, agreeing on a rhythm.**

- **How it develops:** People burn out alone. It helps to agree on your rhythm with the people around you: a team that decided for itself how to work often works better.
- **The price:** The freedom rests on trust and agreements, and it's easy to lose.
- **Signal:** One branch let people work how and as much as suited them, and they started working better to keep that freedom.
- **Heard at the game:** “It has to come through ‘I want to’”
- **Step for this week:** Agree with one colleague on a rule that saves energy for you both.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Quiet happiness or visibility** (DS + CE). Happiness loves quiet, but to earn money you have to be visible.

- **Paradox:** I want to quietly tend my garden, and have everyone know me and pay a lot
- **Experiment for a week:** For a week, separate where you must be visible and where you can disappear.

**Focus on yourself or on connections** (DS + DE). Focusing on your body and state brings happiness, but weakens the ties through which everything else comes.

- **Paradox:** How do you stay part of the world while focusing on yourself?
- **Experiment for a week:** Do one thing for yourself and one for someone else on the same day, and compare how it feels.
