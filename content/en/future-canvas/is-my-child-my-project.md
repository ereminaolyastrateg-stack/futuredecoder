**Who will be responsible for how a child turns out?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

[Open in the interactive canvas](/static/polotno/en/#q-1v6z0jr) · [Challenge a friend to a duel of worlds](/static/polotno/en/#new-1v6z0jr)

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**The child is a product of big systems.**

- **How it develops:** Systems compete for minds from infancy: a children's bank card, a series, a game. Parents decide little: by choosing a bank or a game they choose which system the child will live in. You can't avoid a system nearby, but you can choose consciously.
- **The price:** The child is assembled from skills the system needs today, and tomorrow they may not be needed.
- **Signal:** Children are a top audience for corporations: banks and platforms reach them before their parents do.
- **Heard at the game:** “Which system will you settle your child in”
- **Step for this week:** Look at the services your child uses and decide which of them you'd consciously choose yourself.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**The child is the parents' project along a ready-made roadmap.**

- **How it develops:** The world sells roadmaps: ten clubs before lunch, five after. The system provides the map, and parents are responsible for delivering it. Only the world changes so fast that the project is out of date before it's built.
- **The price:** A parents' contest of “who has more clubs”, and a project that can't keep up with the world.
- **Signal:** An acquaintance wants his son to study AI, and in five years it may not be in demand.
- **Heard at the game:** “A marketing undertaking”
- **Step for this week:** Drop one club that you need rather than your child, and see what they choose themselves.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**The child is a project of the place they ended up in.**

- **How it develops:** The child belongs to themselves, to society or to the system, not only to the parents. They're raised by the community and the group they happen to end up in. Mentors and tutors teach them.
- **The price:** Parents have less influence, and the community may turn out to be random.
- **Signal:** Children as creative directors: they are invited to invent products.
- **Heard at the game:** “A project of the place they ended up in”
- **Step for this week:** Recall which adults besides your parents influenced you in childhood, and find such a person for your child.

## Community world (DE)

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Elders want to give** (Все). The experience of elders could speed up the young, but there's nowhere to pass it on.

- **Paradox:** Elders have the desire to give; the young don't yet have the desire to receive.
- **Experiment for a week:** Listen to your child's or a younger colleague's playlist and discuss it honestly.
