**What will happen to those who don't want to learn?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**They'll be led by metrics and reminders.**

- **How it develops:** The system doesn't ask whether you feel like it: you're led by metrics and reminders, and AI picks a format so it isn't boring.
- **The price:** You learn because you have to, not because it's interesting.
- **Signal:** In China, kindergartens work with AR scenarios: a ready lesson plan for the teacher and weekly tracking of every child.
- **Heard at the game:** “It's supposedly optimisation: more information in less time”
- **Step for this week:** Sign up for a service with small 10-minute portions and reminders; let it keep count for you.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**A mentor will hold them until they get into it.**

- **How it develops:** Not feeling like it is normal; that's what the mentor and shared rules are for. They hold you until you get into it.
- **The price:** Freedom is postponed until later.
- **Signal:** The owner of a learning space insisted the group sit two classes with a teacher nobody wanted. Afterwards everyone was grateful.
- **Heard at the game:** “The external limiter must be proactive, not wait until it hurts”
- **Step for this week:** Find an external “limiter”: a course with deadlines or a person who will check on you.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**They'll simply live in a different world.**

- **How it develops:** Without inner drive there's no autonomy: those who want nothing simply live in another world. But interest is often born from discipline, not the other way round. And from the skill of imagining, so you're interesting to yourself.
- **The price:** Everything rests on you.
- **Signal:** As a child, one participant spent weeks in the library reading everything: inner drive instead of clubs.
- **Heard at the game:** “And they say: I don't want to. What do you do about that? They simply live in another quadrant”
- **Step for this week:** For a week, do one basic thing for 10 minutes without asking yourself whether you feel like it.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**The desire will come when their work is needed by someone.**

- **How it develops:** Wanting is born in the process, when you see your work is needed by someone nearby. You learn not “in general” but for the live task of a neighbour or your district.
- **The price:** You have to go out to people and negotiate.
- **Signal:** Young people don't just master technology; through their own critical research they change the rules.
- **Heard at the game:** “If something doesn't exist, it has to be created”
- **Step for this week:** Find one task of a person nearby and learn exactly what it requires.
