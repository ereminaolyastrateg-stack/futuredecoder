## Autopilot world (CS)

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**The health paradox: the more you invest, the more you need to invest.**

- **How it develops:** The host: when every line of your desired future gets more time, money and attention for health, there's no resource left for meaning. Health becomes the goal itself and adds load rather than removing it. Health is linked to finances, time and motivation; you can't design for one area alone.
- **The price:** A vicious circle: more money for health means more work means less health.
- **Signal:** A desired future where most goes on health
- **Heard at the game:** We didn't lighten her load; we increased it.
- **Step for this week:** Cover your current state with a sheet, write your desired state next to it for time, money, attention and meaning, and check whether the sum adds up.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Energy is the new currency: energy income must exceed spending.**

- **How it develops:** Start with an energy audit: what gives energy and what takes it. Most energy goes into fighting problems; their sources need to be found and closed. Choose a goal that replenishes your resources as you move towards it. Small daily steps without forcing yourself.
- **The price:** It takes regular feedback and readiness for setbacks.
- **Signal:** Energy is the new currency
- **Heard at the game:** Income must be greater than spending.
- **Step for this week:** Every day, ask: what level is my energy at, and what can I do right now?

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**If growth in money is built into the goal, the system starts to fall apart.**

- **How it develops:** The host: in every game since October, as soon as more money was put into the desired future, the first step became “earn more”, and degrading feedback kicked in. So it's better to reduce the focus on money and increase the system's autonomy. Overwork is technical debt: you pay later with health and years.
- **The price:** Money “isn't noticed”, while the interest on the debt grows.
- **Signal:** Degrading feedback when betting on growth in money
- **Heard at the game:** As soon as the balance shifts to money, everything slides.
- **Step for this week:** Look at what technical debt you're accumulating now for money, and what you'll pay it with.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**The pill and the anxiety** (CS + CE). There's a drug against ageing, yet health anxiety only grows.

- **Paradox:** More health data, more anxiety.
- **Experiment for a week:** Don't check your tracker metrics for a week, and see how you feel.

**The health paradox** (CE). The more you invest in health, the more you need to invest; health becomes a burden.

- **Paradox:** The goal of “more health” takes resources from the meaning it was wanted for.
- **Experiment for a week:** Lay out your desired future by time, money, attention and meaning, and check whether the day has enough hours.

**Limited or free time** (CS / DS). A short life forces you to choose; a long one lets you try things, but removes the external motivator.

- **Paradox:** Without a ticking clock, the reason to stop disappears.
- **Experiment for a week:** Start one thing you put off “because it's too late”.
