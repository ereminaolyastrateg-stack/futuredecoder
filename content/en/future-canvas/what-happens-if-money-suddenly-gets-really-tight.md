## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Balance across all four quadrants and a stress scenario with minimal money.**

- **How it develops:** The host: check your financial system in all four logics: what's on autopilot, what follows the rules, what you own, what's in a shared cause. Write a stress scenario: how to live on the minimum. Autonomy starts with the basics, food for example.
- **The price:** It takes time and an honest look at your fears.
- **Signal:** A stress scenario with minimal money
- **Step for this week:** Write on one page what would carry you for three months if your income halved.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Finances in four quadrants** (Все). Part on autopilot, part by the rules, part owned, part in a shared cause. Plus a stress scenario at the minimum.

- **Paradox:** Leaning into one quadrant is convenient until a crisis hits.
- **Experiment for a week:** Lay out your money across the four quadrants and see which is empty.

**Money: a means or a product** (ЦС / ДЗ). Finance has become a product, though it should be a service. Rational decisions produce an irrational result.

- **Paradox:** More money, less wellbeing (according to the host).
- **Experiment for a week:** Over a week, mark which expenses served your life and which served someone's product.
