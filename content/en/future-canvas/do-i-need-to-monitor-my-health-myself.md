**Who will look after health?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**The centre controls health: how much to drink and how many squats will be set for you.**

- **How it develops:** Gadgets and corporations measure everything for me, and health is needed in order to work. I decide little myself, but everything is suggested to me.
- **The price:** You don't understand what's happening to you; you just follow the prompts.
- **Signal:** A huge market of rings, bracelets and smart earrings that measure everything.
- **Heard at the game:** “How much water to drink, how many squats to do will be set for you”
- **Step for this week:** Turn off one tracker for a week and write down how you feel without numbers.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**The tools are provided; after that, you manage on your own.**

- **How it develops:** There are gadgets and sensors, and capital flows into biotech and health. But the limit is the person: the body can't keep up with the speed of the world. Results take time and effort.
- **The price:** You can't speed up the body: “nine women can't make a baby in a month”.
- **Signal:** Punk wellness promises quick traditions, but at the game people noticed it takes the body a long time to get to even twenty pull-ups.
- **Heard at the game:** “Nine women can't make a baby in a month”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one healthy habit and keep it for six months without expecting results in a week.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**All the tools are yours: both biohacking and discipline.**

- **How it develops:** I build my own health system: apps, biohacking, restrictions. For example, a fridge that won't open until morning.
- **The price:** It's easy to overdo it: control for the sake of control.
- **Signal:** Brain organoids: lab-grown mini-models of the brain.
- **Heard at the game:** “A fridge that won't open until morning”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one tool that will help with your weakest habit and try it for a week.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**The environment holds it up: people just don't wreck it.**

- **How it develops:** No need to monitor: your wife or neighbours will tell you if something's wrong. I watch myself and compare with others. The main thing is to surround yourself with decent people.
- **The price:** If your surroundings are unhealthy, you'll repeat their habits.
- **Signal:** Mediterranean long-livers: it's not only the climate but families where generations live together (according to a participant).
- **Heard at the game:** “We just don't wreck it”
- **Step for this week:** Start something good for your body together with someone: a walk, swimming, morning exercises.
