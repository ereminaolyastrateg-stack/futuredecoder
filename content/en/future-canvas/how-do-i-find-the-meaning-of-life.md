## Community world (DE)

**We roughly know our meaning; we don't believe we can realise it.**

- **How it develops:** A participant's example: “I don't want anything”, yet offer ten million and the Maldives and you want everything. So the issue isn't a lack of meaning but disbelief that it can be realised. Instead of a global meaning, a current need: identified it, met it with a small step, rebuilt again. Sometimes it all starts with self-identification, deciding what kind of person you no longer want to see yourself as.
- **The price:** The advice “go find the meaning of life” turns into spending on coaches. You need a formula for where to start looking.
- **Signal:** We know our meaning but don't believe in realising it
- **Heard at the game:** The point isn't that I don't want; it's that you don't believe it can be realised.
- **Step for this week:** Ask yourself: what do I need right now, and what one step will meet it?

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**The pill and the anxiety** (ЦС + ЦЗ). There's a drug against ageing, yet health anxiety only grows.

- **Paradox:** More health data, more anxiety.
- **Experiment for a week:** Don't check your tracker metrics for a week, and see how you feel.

**The health paradox** (ЦЗ). The more you invest in health, the more you need to invest; health becomes a burden.

- **Paradox:** The goal of “more health” takes resources from the meaning it was wanted for.
- **Experiment for a week:** Lay out your desired future by time, money, attention and meaning, and check whether the day has enough hours.

**Limited or free time** (ЦС / ДС). A short life forces you to choose; a long one lets you try things, but removes the external motivator.

- **Paradox:** Without a ticking clock, the reason to stop disappears.
- **Experiment for a week:** Start one thing you put off “because it's too late”.
