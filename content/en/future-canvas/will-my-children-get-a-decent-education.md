## Autopilot world (CS)

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**Practical education: survival and self-sufficiency skills.**

- **How it develops:** Those who left the system learn what's needed by hand: generating energy, growing food, building. The village hardly needs the internet: the post and the neighbours. What's valued is what you can do yourself.
- **The price:** Outside the system there's no access to its knowledge and benefits.
- **Signal:** People switch to paper books and wired headphones: a demand for “something simpler”.
- **Heard at the game:** “Freedom has a price”
- **Step for this week:** Learn one practical thing that a service currently does for you.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Education is a privilege, and for the rest the ability to obey will do.**

- **How it develops:** Those who can't provide for themselves move next to the plant, which gives housing and electricity. Quality education is for the few; workers need to perform functions. Like ants: 80% are reserves who are given nothing but kept ready.
- **The price:** Stratification becomes fixed: the starting position decides everything.
- **Signal:** About 80% of ants are reserves: they step in when the first 20% die (from a book the host mentioned).
- **Heard at the game:** “An anthill around industry”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one skill that gives you or your child more freedom than just carrying out tasks.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Knowledge in your head: everyone is responsible for their own “hard drive”.**

- **How it develops:** The internet is built into the person; everyone becomes a server. To keep it working, people have to develop memory and connections in the brain, so they are very well educated. But norms are set by school and medicine, and selection is by the brain's starting parameters.
- **The price:** Those born “different” are filtered out; the prompter easily replaces your own thoughts.
- **Signal:** Neural interfaces are already in early human trials (according to the host).
- **Heard at the game:** “An internet of things where the thing is a person”
- **Step for this week:** Before you ask AI, formulate your own answer, and only then compare.
