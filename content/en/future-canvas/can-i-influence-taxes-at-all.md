## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**You influence by withdrawing the base: business with people instead of computation.**

- **How it develops:** If digital operations are taxed, live labour and real business without AI get cheaper: they're harder to count. The entrepreneur keeps autonomy and some data to themselves. The state sees the base shrinking, and that's the signal.
- **The price:** This is influence by exit, not dialogue. Tax authorities respond with new rules: registering computers, a tax “for optimisation”.
- **Signal:** After complaints, a region cut the cost of a business patent by 20%, and even more in poor districts (a participant's example).
- **Heard at the game:** “Singe their base a little”
- **Step for this week:** Think which of your processes could work without a digital trail and what that would cost.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**You can have influence, but not alone, and starting at the local level.**

- **How it develops:** At first participants discussed only how to protect themselves and didn't believe the state could be influenced. The host reminded them: the state is the expression of the people's will. What works is not the street but the local level: an association of entrepreneurs, an ally in government, a forum where decisions are made together with officials.
- **The price:** It takes critical mass and brave people. Out of twenty associations, maybe one will stick to the cause.
- **Signal:** A regional forum was organised by about five small entrepreneurs (one has three plumbing shops); they brought in a senator and a deputy governor and discussed taxes (according to the host).
- **Heard at the game:** “We can raise your KPI”
- **Step for this week:** Find an association of entrepreneurs in your region and go to one of its events.

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**You influence through mutual help and knowledge.**

- **How it develops:** Many businesses close not because of taxes but because of an outdated business model and lack of knowledge. Entrepreneurs team up: shared premises, advice, tax training. They offer the municipality one specific small thing that benefits both sides. “Vasya alone won't survive, but Vasya and Petya together will.”
- **The price:** It's slow and needs someone to take on the organising.
- **Signal:** A free three-week programme for business in the region: lectures, speakers, premises; “just registered and came” (a participant's experience).
- **Heard at the game:** “Taxes are a collective story too”
- **Step for this week:** Find one entrepreneur nearby and come up with one thing that's cheaper to do together.
