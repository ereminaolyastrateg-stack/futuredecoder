**Will it be possible to succeed without an education?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Yes, if you're useful to the system.**

- **How it develops:** Skills are set by the system and algorithms based on tests, and information arrives at any moment, but for the system's goals. You can be promoted without education if you're loyal and needed. There is a set of options, but it benefits the system.
- **The price:** The less you think for yourself, the more convenient for the system, and the easier you are to control.
- **Signal:** In the UK about 500 private colleges closed in two years (according to the host).
- **Heard at the game:** “The less education, the better for the system”
- **Step for this week:** Find one course or small college that teaches you to think, not just gives you a skill for a vacancy.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**No: education is a pass, but it quickly goes out of date.**

- **How it develops:** You need a diploma to get in, but it can lose its value in five years. What matters more is whether you're useful. So I learn continuously and take responsibility for the result myself: the instructions are given, and it's up to me what grade I do them to.
- **The price:** A race for diplomas: you grab the surface, while deep education takes time you don't have.
- **Signal:** Young people are moving into crafts, bookbinding for example.
- **Heard at the game:** “Education as a pass, but it can lose its value in five years”
- **Step for this week:** Before you go to study, check whether the specialism will be needed in five years: ask two people who work in it.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Yes: all the information is out there; you need perseverance and discipline.**

- **How it develops:** I learn without a university, collecting what I need from open sources. Big platforms give knowledge away for free, and I use that in my own interest. My security is in what I can do myself.
- **The price:** Nobody will check on you or push you: you have to keep the discipline yourself.
- **Signal:** Big systems give resources away for free: open courses, video platforms, free tiers.
- **Heard at the game:** “Ride the giant while it's let its guard down”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one free course from a big platform and complete it within a month.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Yes: being handy, resourceful and able to learn matters more.**

- **How it develops:** Diplomas mean little here. I learn from the people around me, through mentors and practice. What's valued is what I can do with my hands and how fast I adapt.
- **The price:** Mentoring is education too, just without a certificate: there's nothing to prove your knowledge with.
- **Signal:** An IT specialist with a master's degree took a furniture restoration course, in an area where locals do poor repairs.
- **Heard at the game:** “You can do without education: practical skills, being handy, resourcefulness”
- **Step for this week:** Find someone who does what you want to learn and ask to help them for a day.
