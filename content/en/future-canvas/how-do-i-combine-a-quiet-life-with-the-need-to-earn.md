**How will people combine a quiet life with earning?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

[Open in the interactive canvas](/static/polotno/en/#q-96enn8) · [Challenge a friend to a duel of worlds](/static/polotno/en/#new-96enn8)

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**By buying quiet: services take over routine.**

- **How it develops:** Quiet can partly be bought: hand routine to services and AI, and keep your earning where you're visible.
- **The price:** Hand over too much and you'll depend on the services and their price.
- **Signal:** Smart lighting is being linked to a digital health assistant.
- **Heard at the game:** “You don't have to delegate 100%”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one routine you could hand to a service or AI, and estimate how much quiet that would give you.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**By choosing your own scale: working at 80%.**

- **How it develops:** The new norm is choosing your own scale: working at 80%, four days, without the race. People are already abandoning the constant race en masse.
- **The price:** A smaller scale may also mean a smaller income.
- **Signal:** A job-search service records people turning away from the constant race.
- **Heard at the game:** “How much is enough, specifically for you?”
- **Step for this week:** Define your size: how much money and work is enough, specifically for you.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**By earning from skills and dosing visibility themselves.**

- **How it develops:** Income comes from several small sources, all based on skills rather than constant visibility. I set the boundary: a couple of days a month on stage (talks, posts, meetings), and the rest of the time I work quietly. I know exactly how much is enough for me, so I don't chase growth.
- **The price:** There's no insurance: if your skill stops being needed, you'll have to rebuild on your own.
- **Signal:** We misjudge forecasts of our own emotions.
- **Heard at the game:** “I want to quietly tend my garden, but have everyone know me and pay a lot”
- **Step for this week:** Work out how much money a month is enough for you and how many days of visibility it takes to earn it.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Through people: quietly, but not alone.**

- **How it develops:** Income comes through people. A quiet life doesn't mean a lonely one: you can choose neighbours and a community where you're known without noise.
- **The price:** Without exchange with people you definitely lose something.
- **Signal:** House hunting is starting to be combined with finding the right neighbours.
- **Heard at the game:** “Everything in life comes to a person through interaction”
- **Step for this week:** Think about which three people your work comes through, and keep in touch with at least one.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Quiet happiness or visibility** (ДС + ЦЗ). Happiness loves quiet, but to earn money you have to be visible.

- **Paradox:** I want to quietly tend my garden, and have everyone know me and pay a lot
- **Experiment for a week:** For a week, separate where you must be visible and where you can disappear.

**Focus on yourself or on connections** (ДС + ДЗ). Focusing on your body and state brings happiness, but weakens the ties through which everything else comes.

- **Paradox:** How do you stay part of the world while focusing on yourself?
- **Experiment for a week:** Do one thing for yourself and one for someone else on the same day, and compare how it feels.
