**What will school and university turn into now that AI exists?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Into a factory of tests: evidence matters, not the diploma.**

- **How it develops:** A diploma means almost nothing. Employers look at evidence of what you can actually do. University has become a factory of tests, and an AI tutor gives more learning in less time.
- **The price:** Learning turns into a race for metrics.
- **Signal:** An alternative to grades is launched: instead of a final score, concrete evidence of work (Mastery Transcript).
- **Heard at the game:** “The exam exists so that you don't get the place”
- **Step for this week:** Collect three pieces of evidence of what you can do and show them instead of the “education” line on your CV.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**Into keepers of what is ours: learning as a lifelong right.**

- **How it develops:** School and university remain, but their purpose has changed: preserve what is ours, our country, our bloc. AI here is not for creativity but for digesting accumulated knowledge. Learning is your lifelong right.
- **The price:** You get a narrow niche next to the robots.
- **Signal:** A new social contract for education is proposed: education as a public good and a lifelong right.
- **Heard at the game:** “AI is for digesting”
- **Step for this week:** Find out where you're entitled to free training, from work, your city or the state, and sign up.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Into a place that lays the foundation; after that, your own AI and your own effort.**

- **How it develops:** I have my own AI that nobody can switch off, and my own basic skills. School is there to lay the foundation and discipline; after that, it's down to me.
- **The price:** Without an inner drive, no AI will help.
- **Signal:** A design academy graduate learned everything from YouTube, built a printing press and posted the process back online.
- **Heard at the game:** “AI is just the ballpoint pen of the 21st century”
- **Step for this week:** Find out which AI can run on your computer without the internet, and move one of your tasks there.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Into a network of labs serving its region.**

- **How it develops:** Our university is a network of labs serving its region: it studies the river, the roads, the forest and learns on those tasks. AI helps, but we learn from the environment and from each other.
- **The price:** There's no diploma saying “I've learned it”; it shows only in what you do.
- **Signal:** The university serves not its own faculties but the bioregion and its needs.
- **Heard at the game:** “The university as a network of real labs”
- **Step for this week:** Find one live lab, project or volunteer effort nearby where people learn on a real task.
