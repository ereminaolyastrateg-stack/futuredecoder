**How will life and work be organised if everything rests on AI?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**AI becomes invisible, like a lift, and you can no longer do without it.**

- **How it develops:** Agent negotiates with agent; my watch reschedules meetings. My main asset is a subscription to a service that holds all my counterparties. I don't understand how it works, and I pay again for every change. Like the first foreign cars: you can't change the brake pads yourself any more, only at the service centre.
- **The price:** You pay almost your whole budget, and you don't always like the result. No alternative is visible; the system stands before you like a wall. Thinking doesn't change: “I'll think as I always have”.
- **Signal:** One person listed all their digital services, assessed which corporations they depend on, and spent a year finding alternatives: a “transition plan”.
- **Heard at the game:** “Now they can't change the brake pads themselves”
- **Step for this week:** List the services without which your work would stop, and find a backup for one of them.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**One person with agents is a whole company, and access to computing power is like land.**

- **How it develops:** For the first two years I deploy agents like crazy, spend money and burn out. Then I'll learn, and become better value than big companies' teams. A manager hires one contractor instead of thirty-five. Taking my neural network away is like taking a plot from a peasant: all the business models are already rebuilt.
- **The price:** The strong clip your wings: platform commissions grow, the AI tax is high. Run short of computing power and everything stops. Businesses that used to help (accountants, assistants) die out.
- **Signal:** Consulting is gradually dying; those with their own product or methodology survive (the host's observation in her own business).
- **Heard at the game:** “Five clients and not one more”
- **Step for this week:** Work out how many clients you need to live, and don't scale beyond that without need.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**The technology isn't yours; only your competence is.**

- **How it develops:** I do a lot myself with AI, without a team. But the tool doesn't belong to me: access may end, be limited or get more expensive. So I hold on to what stays with me: my core competence. It isn't tied to a profession and transfers anywhere.
- **The price:** It's easy to mistake self-reliance for independence, while dependence on the tool remains.
- **Signal:** Access to intelligence becomes a question of geography: AI infrastructure can't always handle the load.
- **Heard at the game:** “The core stays with you”
- **Step for this week:** Look at your past jobs and find what you did everywhere: that's your core.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**AI is a tool for human meanings, not a goal.**

- **How it develops:** People have barely changed in a couple of thousand years, so everything comes back to the human. AI is used in a limited way, with a sense of measure, where it helps. Development isn't banned; it becomes a value. The values are the same: family, friendship, attention, just realised in new ways.
- **The price:** Not to dissolve, you need discipline and an honest question to yourself: could I switch AI off for a few months?
- **Signal:** People start protecting themselves from AI platforms: conscious control over their own lives.
- **Heard at the game:** “Never push it to the limit”
- **Step for this week:** Spend one day without AI and write down where it was hard.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**What goes in comes out** (Все). AI amplifies the signal: if you could think, it amplifies that; if not, you'll be posting cats. Good context gives a good answer, bad context gives a lot of bad. Everyone expects improvement, but it can make things worse.

- **Paradox:** The tool is the same, the results diverge, and stratification grows.
- **Experiment for a week:** Before prompting AI, formulate for yourself what you want to get and why.
