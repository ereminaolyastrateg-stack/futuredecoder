**Who will do what's needed if everyone chooses work they love?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

[Open in the interactive canvas](/static/polotno/en/#q-1rpt873) · [Challenge a friend to a duel of worlds](/static/polotno/en/#new-1rpt873)

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**An algorithm will tweak quotas and exams.**

- **How it develops:** The algorithm sees a shortage and tweaks exams and quotas: fewer university places, more on the tracks that are needed.
- **The price:** The system teaches you to work for the salary it offers.
- **Signal:** The public exam becomes a technology of mass selection.
- **Heard at the game:** “They tweak the state exam so that statistically fewer people pass”
- **Step for this week:** Look at which quotas and exams currently steer people in your field.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**The centre will hand down quotas and give a big goal.**

- **How it develops:** The centre sets what's needed: quotas come down from above for how many doctors and fitters are needed. A big shared goal makes needed work honourable.
- **The price:** “Work you love” is a luxury.
- **Signal:** The image of the right person and family is set from above: for example, so that characters in every cartoon have three or more children.
- **Heard at the game:** “Set a great goal and nobody will be bored”
- **Step for this week:** Find out what big goal your country or company sets now and what it promises those who take part.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Everyone balances their own books; it's not society's job.**

- **How it develops:** Here everyone keeps their own balance of resources. If the work you love doesn't feed you, that's your problem, not society's.
- **The price:** Freedom without insurance.
- **Signal:** Moscow State University started without a mathematics faculty: mathematics was part of philosophy.
- **Heard at the game:** “Autonomy needs the skill of balance: debit and credit”
- **Step for this week:** Work out your balance: what feeds you, what makes you happy, and where they could meet.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**People will agree from below, seeing what the district needs.**

- **How it develops:** What's needed is visible from below, through feedback from the environment. If all work is decently paid and the district's needs are visible, people distribute themselves, or agree.
- **The price:** You have to negotiate constantly.
- **Signal:** The university serves not its own faculties but the bioregion and its needs.
- **Heard at the game:** “Will all hundred choose so that society's needs are covered? Or will everyone want to be a baker?”
- **Step for this week:** Find what your district lacks and ask two acquaintances who would take it on together.
