**How will communication work when AI writes the emails?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

[Open in the interactive canvas](/static/polotno/en/#q-1n3li0z) · [Challenge a friend to a duel of worlds](/static/polotno/en/#new-1n3li0z)

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**A pretty mask instead of a person: language becomes uniform.**

- **How it develops:** A toxic leader writes emails through AI: “AI replaces my swearing with nice communication.” It's unclear who stands behind the text. You swap the mask for decent words, but you don't change. Language becomes uniform: see the word “honestly” in a report and you know AI wrote it.
- **The price:** People unlearn finding common ground and making the effort to understand each other.
- **Signal:** AI polishing of text quietly changes the author's culture; style is lost.
- **Heard at the game:** “AI replaces my swearing with nice communication”
- **Step for this week:** Write one important email yourself, without AI editing.

## Rules world (CE)

Nobody has designed this world for this question yet.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Let AI churn out officialese, while live contact gets more expensive.**

- **How it develops:** There's more and more automation, so offline and live services become more expensive and valuable. People come first; AI is for bureaucracy: “let it churn out official language”. Nobody wants commitments: renting, sharing, an easy way out.
- **The price:** Live interaction feels “very expensive”: it's hard to go out or spend time with someone.
- **Signal:** A participant writes to colleagues in different countries and asks AI to adapt the text to their culture.
- **Heard at the game:** “Real interaction has become expensive”
- **Step for this week:** Give AI one piece of official correspondence and spend the freed half hour on a live meeting.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**AI creates a safe space so people can agree themselves.**

- **How it develops:** The key skill is talking. AI helps formulate the request and builds a game board where you can discuss anything and find a win-win solution rather than a compromise. Live contact takes tension and energy: a “poetic meeting” instead of presentations.
- **The price:** Mediators and some managers lose their jobs. Envy and pride don't go anywhere.
- **Signal:** The host builds a game board with her own GPT: request, macrotrends, signals; an acquaintance used it with her husband to decide where to go on holiday.
- **Heard at the game:** “Sometimes it's useful to travel with a backpack”
- **Step for this week:** At your next meeting, suggest working through a contested issue via four versions of the future instead of an argument.
