## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Legal crypto and automatic bookkeeping: more control and several income sources.**

- **How it develops:** Through the “Fire” lens: crypto is legalised, bookkeeping is automated, control is higher. You can keep several sources of income.
- **The price:** The risk that ideas lose their protection.
- **Signal:** Legalisation of cryptocurrencies
- **Step for this week:** Automate the tracking of at least one spending item.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**Saving the drowning is the drowning's own job: optimise everything.**

- **How it develops:** Through the “Earth” lens: the economic situation of the majority is declining. According to the host, Russians' share of spending on food is a 16-year record: 15–20% in developed countries, 35–36% in Russia before, around 40% now. The routine is “optimising everything”: a multi-purpose kit on a minimal budget, selling a ready solution rather than a product.
- **The price:** Choice, range and quality shrink, more time goes into hunting for deals; you answer to buyers rather than the state. The pluses are transparency, a loyal audience and financial literacy: “when the trend changes, there'll be no one to match you”.
- **Signal:** The share of spending on food is at a 16-year high (according to the host)
- **Heard at the game:** When the trend changes, there'll be no one to match you.
- **Step for this week:** Put together one “ready solution” for your family: a kit that solves a task completely with minimal money.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Half at the factory, half your own small business.**

- **How it develops:** Point B: 50% stays at the factory, plus her own small business (natural food). She tracks expenses, saves, and checks her balance wheel every day. Cards: disciplined growth, the economy of the mighty middle, self-custody infrastructure.
- **The price:** It takes energy and discipline to run two things at once.
- **Signal:** The economy of the mighty middle
- **Step for this week:** Draw your balance wheel and mark one area you'll raise this week.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Autopilot plus ownership** (CS + DS). Accounting and payments are automated, but the person holds their assets themselves. Several income streams.

- **Paradox:** The more you delegate, the more you depend, even if you own.
- **Experiment for a week:** Automate one expense category and move one sum to where you own it yourself.

**Discipline as a weapon** (CE + DS). The world is harsh and the rules are unpleasant, but whoever has learned to count is “hard to con”.

- **Paradox:** Discipline is imposed from above, but the benefit goes to whoever makes it their own.
- **Experiment for a week:** For a week, record every expense, and at the end find one you didn't need.
