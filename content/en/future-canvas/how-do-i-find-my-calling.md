**How will people find their life's work?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Your work is issued from a menu, like a level in a game.**

- **How it develops:** Career guidance, tests, algorithms: the system suggests who I should be. Calling looks like a dream of levelling up. A person here is an object, but also the main resource, and can say: “We're not playing any more.”
- **The price:** You play by someone else's rules and may not notice it's not your game.
- **Signal:** The games industry grows without expanding its audience: it retains those already inside.
- **Heard at the game:** “A level in a game, a level-80 orc”
- **Step for this week:** Take one career test and honestly mark what in the result you disagree with.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**The instructions are given, and you build your own kit.**

- **How it develops:** You don't need one profession for life; you can have several at once: one for money, another for the soul. I try things in parallel and see what works. Places where you get everything at once (money, recognition, self-realisation) are long taken.
- **The price:** It takes many iterations: quantitative motivation won't carry you, only enjoying the process.
- **Signal:** Shanghai punk wellness: people combine traditional practices with modern life.
- **Heard at the game:** “Putting the puzzle together”
- **Step for this week:** Write down three things you'd take on even without money, and give one of them an hour a week.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Your calling forms as you bump into others.**

- **How it develops:** I don't look for myself in a vacuum; I try things and run into people, tasks, reactions. A third skill can be born from two. I decide myself, but understanding comes through others.
- **The price:** The search is long and on your own; nobody will tell you you're in the right place.
- **Signal:** Oil workers: “we know how to drill the earth; find fields where drilling is needed”, looking at what you already know from a different angle.
- **Heard at the game:** “Your calling forms by bumping into others”
- **Step for this week:** Tell three people about your idea and write down what struck you in their reactions.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**It isn't searched for; it finds the person itself.**

- **How it develops:** A calling isn't searched for but tried: “you can do this, let's try”. From each according to ability; everyone will be placed. The environment suggests what you do.
- **The price:** You depend on the environment: if it's the wrong one, nobody will notice you.
- **Signal:** A straight-A daughter was heading to medical school, took a gap year and realised she likes working with people (a participant's example, details changed).
- **Heard at the game:** “They'll find you, they'll place you”
- **Step for this week:** Say out loud in your circle what you can do, and accept the first offer to try.
