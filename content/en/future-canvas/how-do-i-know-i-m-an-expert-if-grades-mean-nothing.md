**Who will decide that I'm an expert?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**An algorithm, by results.**

- **How it develops:** I'm assessed by an algorithm, on results, not knowledge. What matters is not what you learned but what changed after you.
- **The price:** Ratings everywhere, and sometimes you want to go where you're at zero.
- **Signal:** Public procurement reports are no longer about delivering equipment to a hospital, but about doctors having learned to use it so many times.
- **Heard at the game:** “The result matters more than process statistics”
- **Step for this week:** Count the result your work gave others this month, not how many hours it took.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**A community, through an exam and recognition.**

- **How it develops:** An expert is someone the community has recognised: passed the exam, completed the track, got the mentor's approval. The rules are clear to everyone.
- **The price:** Recognition doesn't depend on you.
- **Signal:** Since 1870 the British civil service has recruited through competitive exams.
- **Heard at the game:** “The norm is the exam, it's the pass”
- **Step for this week:** Find a community or guild for your profession and learn what counts as recognition there.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**You yourself, by your own criteria.**

- **How it develops:** I set my own criteria: feedback, income, fit with my values and a sense of happiness. And I show publicly what I can do.
- **The price:** If the world doesn't share your criteria, you'll be an expert only for yourself.
- **Signal:** One after another, experts are building their own sites: knowledge bases with their methods and thoughts on the future.
- **Heard at the game:** “If he invented his own grading system and meets it, does that mean the world also thinks he's an expert?”
- **Step for this week:** Write down five criteria of expertise for yourself and check yourself against them right now.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**The people around you, by what has changed.**

- **How it develops:** You can tell an expert by how they handle the live tasks of people around them. It's not you being assessed, but what changed nearby.
- **The price:** Recognition comes slowly and quietly.
- **Signal:** Metaculus: a site where people forecast the future and everyone's accuracy is visible to all.
- **Heard at the game:** “The environment tells us whom it needs”
- **Step for this week:** Ask two people you work with what changed thanks to you.
