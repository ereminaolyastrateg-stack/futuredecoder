## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**Money has detached from the real and become a product in itself.**

- **How it develops:** Money was decoupled from gold and became an abstraction. The cycle of decoupling and re-anchoring breeds crises: rational decisions give irrational results. Finance has turned from a means into a product.
- **The price:** Dehumanisation: the cards say “population”, not people. According to the host, GDP originally excluded military spending and marketing, and real wellbeing has barely grown in 70 years: “more money, less wellbeing”.
- **Signal:** Finance has stopped being a service institution
- **Heard at the game:** We are a service institution.
- **Step for this week:** Recall what you paid for last week, and mark what of it actually made life better.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Money is being re-anchored to the real; the next anchor may be people or nature.**

- **How it develops:** According to the host, money is being re-anchored to real assets. Attempts to anchor come from below, attempts to decouple from above. Stablecoins are used about four times more actively in Africa than in Russia, for cross-border trade.
- **The price:** It's slow and from below: you have to invent the rules yourselves.
- **Signal:** Stablecoins in Africa, used for trade between countries (according to the host)
- **Step for this week:** Look at what your savings are anchored to: a currency, things, a business or people.

## Mixes and paradoxes from this game

**Finances in four quadrants** (Все). Part on autopilot, part by the rules, part owned, part in a shared cause. Plus a stress scenario at the minimum.

- **Paradox:** Leaning into one quadrant is convenient until a crisis hits.
- **Experiment for a week:** Lay out your money across the four quadrants and see which is empty.

**Money: a means or a product** (CS / DE). Finance has become a product, though it should be a service. Rational decisions produce an irrational result.

- **Paradox:** More money, less wellbeing (according to the host).
- **Experiment for a week:** Over a week, mark which expenses served your life and which served someone's product.
