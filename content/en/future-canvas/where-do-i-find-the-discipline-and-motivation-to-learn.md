**Who will keep me disciplined?** Four answers from four worlds of the Future Decoder method. They come from participants of the scenario games, not from experts.

## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**An AI agent with reminders.**

- **How it develops:** I'm helped by an AI agent that asks every hour, “Have you done it?”, as persistent as a three-year-old. Annoying reminders, it turns out, are what very smart people need.
- **The price:** Turn off the reminder and everything stops.
- **Signal:** To stop slacking, one person built an AI agent that asks every hour: “Have you written the script? Really?”
- **Heard at the game:** “These annoying reminders turn out to be what very smart people need”
- **Step for this week:** Set a reminder that nags you every day until you've done one thing.

## Rules world (CE)

*you take part by shared rules*

**A mentor, lovingly but firmly.**

- **How it develops:** A mentor keeps the discipline, lovingly but firmly. An external limiter is the norm here, and there's no shame in it.
- **The price:** You depend on whoever is holding you.
- **Signal:** The owner of a learning space insisted the group sit two classes with a teacher nobody wanted. Afterwards everyone was grateful.
- **Heard at the game:** “The mentor doesn't let us drop off the path; they motivate in advance, but lovingly”
- **Step for this week:** Find someone you'll allow to nudge you, and agree on a weekly check-in.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**You yourself are your own carrot.**

- **How it develops:** I'm my own carrot. In the first year the result isn't the outcome but the number of sessions: five workouts a week, not a perfect body.
- **The price:** Laziness and the pull of comfort. Many can't survive this world.
- **Signal:** As a child, one participant spent weeks in the library reading everything: inner drive instead of clubs.
- **Heard at the game:** “The pull of comfort will ruin us all”
- **Step for this week:** Set a goal for the month in number of sessions, not in results.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**A community you pass something on to.**

- **How it develops:** A community keeps me going. When you teach others what you're only just learning, discipline appears by itself. And people appear around you.
- **The price:** You have to negotiate and invest yourself.
- **Signal:** Learned something? Teach someone else, without waiting to become an expert. That's how a community grows around a person.
- **Heard at the game:** “When you teach, a community appears around you”
- **Step for this week:** Tell one person what you're learning this week.
