## Autopilot world (CS)

*the system decides, the result is not yours*

**The global pulls resources out of the local.**

- **How it develops:** Things are made in one place, sold in another, and the money collects in a third. A neighbouring city with tasty avocados appears, and three months later your own city has no money left; nobody feeds the internal market. An authentic place is found, bought up and turned into a picture.
- **The price:** Locals can't afford the prices, the sense of place disappears, and the other chains become donors.
- **Signal:** A surf town in Portugal: after COVID, northern Europeans arrived, bought up property, and locals can't afford the prices (a video a participant watched).
- **Heard at the game:** “They found the feeling, bought it up, turned it into a picture”
- **Step for this week:** Work out what share of your monthly money stays in your neighbourhood or city.

## Autonomy world (DS)

*you decide and you own it*

**Global tools work for small groups.**

- **How it develops:** People unite around a problem and build their own system: crowdsourcing, anonymity, alerts. Once it works, active participation is no longer needed. The global doesn't crush the local but helps it: a tsunami warning is useful to everyone.
- **The price:** Hand power to a built system and it may start serving another group. In one case the result belongs to the people, in another to the platform.
- **Signal:** Community early-warning networks run in parallel with state ones.
- **Heard at the game:** “The fire alarm is installed, and then it runs itself”
- **Step for this week:** Find one global tool you could use for your neighbourhood or your group.

## Community world (DE)

*you create together with others*

**Global knowledge for local life.**

- **How it develops:** You don't have to grow wheat if buckwheat has historically grown where you live: world knowledge helps you grow buckwheat better. Local and seasonal is more useful. If the global collapses, you can rebuild from yourself: your own communications, feeding yourself, your own news.
- **The price:** You can retreat into localism and Luddism; rules are still needed, or there's chaos.
- **Signal:** In Nizhny Novgorod a local restaurant launches a snack and writes “the trend was launched in New York” (the host's observation).
- **Heard at the game:** “We can rebuild from ourselves, in our own home”
- **Step for this week:** Pick one thing you buy from far away and find a local alternative.
