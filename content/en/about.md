## What I believe

Our actions are guided by the image of the future in our heads. Most often it is unconscious and gloomy: "things will get worse, that's obvious", and so we drift towards outcomes that are far from the best. The world is made of large, complex systems, and so is a human life. I want to make these systems visible, so that everyone can design their own future instead of guessing it. Strategy is not only for corporations.

## What I do

- **The Future Decoder method.** I have been developing it since 2020. It offers four logics of the future (autopilot, rules, autonomy, community) through which you can look at any question: work, money, education, health, a city, an industry.
- **Scenario games.** Held regularly, on attention, finance, AI, globalisation, longevity and happiness. Participants design the worlds themselves, and everything they come up with goes into this garden and into the [Future Canvas](/static/polotno/en/).
- **The Architecture of a Person lab.** Regular cohorts of four meetings. A person breaks their life down into spheres, tests the future they want against the four worlds and finds a real first step with the resources they have. The method stays with them.
- **Futures design for companies.** Industry and product scenarios, signal scanning, strategy sessions, and future literacy training for teams.
- **Personal strategy.** One-to-one work when you need to make a big decision or rebuild your life.

## What else I do

## Projects

*Most of my work is under NDA, so instead of company names I describe the industry and the task.*

**How projects reveal the baseline scenarios.** In every project I collect signals from the outside world, connect them to macrotrends and lay them out across the four Decoder worlds. A company sees not one forecast but four logics its market may follow, and finds decisions that work in several of them at once.

**The future of real estate and cities** *(developer, 2023–2024).* Research into the future of housing, cities, technology and buyer behaviour. Four real estate market scenarios and a project roadmap. The line continued with brand and digital marketing research for another developer (2023–2026).

**The future of travel** *(banking ecosystem, travel service, 2024).* A tourism research database and a report connecting macrotrends, four tourism scenarios, signals and emerging trends.

**Media and ecosystems** *(telecom ecosystem, 2023–2026).* Research bases and monitoring for an online cinema and media business, macrotrends for a startup hub, and a three-horizons session. For a major media group (2022): trends in media, crisis communications, attention and trust. For a social network: generational research.

**Resale and marketplaces** *(classifieds platform, 2022–2026).* A series of trend and competitor studies. The largest project built a base of 311 cases across 49 platforms worldwide. It produced four models of the future platform: show, care, tool and connections. The key finding: buyers are short of money, attention, emotion and trust all at once, so a mix of models wins. Deliverables: a report, cards and a team workshop.

**Technology foresight for energy** *(oil and gas company, 2024).* Lithium, drones and small-scale mechanisation, geothermal energy, quantum computing, uranium. Each direction examined across three time horizons, with a scientific research base.

**Finance and banking** *(2024–2026).* AI assistant market research for a brokerage. Trend research for an investment company. Digital transformation roadmaps for banks: payments, digital banking, cybersecurity.

**Trading in African markets** *(2026).* Six sessions with the team: selecting and tagging signals, a trend map, hypotheses. Clients are laid out by income level, with products in four logics for each level.

**The exhibition industry to 2030** *(exhibition stand builder).* A digital twin of the company connects its internal data with external signals, with hypotheses, a 90-day strategy and a monthly snapshot. For the industry, four worlds: meetings as a platform service, the exhibition as a state instrument, a network of precise meetings, and a fair of one's own.

**People and society.**

- The 50+ market: over 200 signals, niches nobody serves, and four community models.
- Parenting and family (2023): research and a startup database.
- Technology and society: quarterly research cycles (2023–2024).
- Pet food and tele-veterinary care (2022–2023): market and consumer research in Kazakhstan and Azerbaijan, with interviews, surveys and CJM.
- Alternative worlds 2040–2070 for a sleep products manufacturer (2022).

**Teaching and strategy sessions.**

- Trendwatching course for MBA and Executive MBA students at Synergy University (since 2023).
- A three-day programme for a major bank's team, "from guessing to designing": signals and trends, scenarios, systems and decisions, backcasting, an experiment.
- A one-day programme for entrepreneurs.
- Three-horizons sessions for a marketplace.
- A workshop with four alternative futures for a home improvement retailer.
- The "Future Urban Environment" programme: six groups of nine people.
- A masterclass on choosing digital technologies for the Leaders of Productivity programme (Russian Foreign Trade Academy, 2022).

**Open Decoder research.** [The Future of Work and Education](https://futuredecoder.ru/future_of_work) (in Russian) is an open study. Other research lines: health, demography, urbanisation, AI, network society and changes in thinking.
