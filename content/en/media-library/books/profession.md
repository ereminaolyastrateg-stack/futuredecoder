**Record:** Science-fiction novella · Isaac Asimov · first published in July 1957.

## About the work

In the novella’s world, professional knowledge is acquired through a special technology, while external procedures determine a person’s suitability for a profession. The story places rapid acquisition of existing knowledge beside the ability to create what no educational recording yet contains.

## In Future Decoder discussions

The novella became an image of people being allocated to professional roles and of the difference between mass training and creating something new. The education discussion asked who assigns a person’s path, what entrenches inequality and how an assigned role can be changed.

## Our reading

The key questions are whether an allocation can be reversed and what enables self-directed learning. A system that recognises only established forms of ability may overlook someone who does not yet fit its procedures. Suitability for a role therefore depends partly on access to other ways of learning and demonstrating a result.

Differences in resources, pace and outcomes need careful examination. They do not by themselves settle what opportunities for development are available to someone.

## Questions for discussion

- Who is entitled to decide what work a person is suited for?
- Which conditions allow someone to revise an assigned path and learn to create something new?
