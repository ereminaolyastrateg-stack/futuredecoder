**Record:** Science-fiction novella · Ted Chiang · 1998, in Starlight 2.

## About the work

A linguist studies the language of beings that have arrived on Earth. Learning its unfamiliar structure is connected, within the story, with an unusual experience of time and personal life. The novella became the literary basis for Arrival.

## Why read

Reading the source reveals how the story worked before its screen adaptation: the relationships between knowledge, memory and choice emerge through the form of the narration itself. The two works can be compared without reducing either to a summary of the other.

## In Future Decoder discussions

The novella was named alongside Arrival during a conversation about time and how the future takes shape. The media library led to reading the literary source — a route from one way of telling a story to another.

## Our reading

What does someone learn when the way events are connected changes, rather than simply the amount of information? This broadens the discussion of learning: we acquire ways of making sense of information as well as new facts. The fictional experience of time opens questions about how an image of the future changes our relationship with the present.

## Questions for discussion

- Which relationships between events are established by a familiar way of telling a story?
- What changes between the novella and the film?
