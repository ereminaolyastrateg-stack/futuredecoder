**Record:** Novel · Aldous Huxley · 1932.

## About the work

Society maintains stability through pre-assigned social roles, conditioning, consumption and soma, a substance used to avoid unpleasant experiences. Life is organised so that people enjoy the place allocated to them.

## Why read

The novel examines a loss of freedom experienced as convenience and wellbeing. The question is not only what people are forbidden to do, but who shapes their desires and their idea of a good life.

## Education and happiness

If learning prepares someone to be content with an assigned role, how can they notice another possible path? Is freedom from anxiety and pain enough to call a life happy? Huxley imagines a society where an answer is built into upbringing itself.

## Our reading

Centralized — Synthetic appears as the design of a society where allocation and the management of emotional states make behaviour predictable. Centralized — Earth appears in accepting the order as the only natural one. A personal path begins to change when someone asks where a desire came from and whether it can be reconsidered.

## Questions for discussion

- How does my own desire differ from a habit of wanting what is approved?
- Can someone be content with life while lacking meaningful choice?
- What should education offer beyond adaptation to an existing role?
