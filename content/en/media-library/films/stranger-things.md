**Record:** Series · created by Matt and Ross Duffer · premiered 15 July 2016.

## About the work

After a boy disappears, people in a small town confront secret experiments and an unfamiliar threat. His friends try to understand what is happening; Dungeons & Dragons forms part of their shared cultural language.

## Why watch

The interesting question is how a group describes the unknown together. A shared imagined world supplies words, roles and hypotheses for action. An analogy still needs testing when a situation differs from the familiar game.

## In Future Decoder discussions

The education game introduced the series alongside D&D to explore shared storytelling and experiments with different world logics.

## Our reading

A shared cultural image helps a group form hypotheses and make participation possible. It matters both what the image enables and what it hides. Shared imagination becomes useful when people can test their account and revise it through action.

## Questions for discussion

- Which shared image helps a group begin investigating?
- What might we miss if we trust a familiar analogy too much?
