**Record:** Film · James Cameron · 1984.

## About the work

A cyborg assassin and a man sent to stop it travel from 2029 to Los Angeles in 1984. Their actions centre on Sarah Connor: her future child will lead the resistance in the war against machines.

## Story timeline

- **1984** — the film’s release year and the setting of its main action.
- **2029** — the future war against machines from which the characters arrive.

## Why watch

The film connects apparent inevitability with the actions of someone who does not yet know her role in the future. Large historical consequences depend on decisions made in particular circumstances.

## Our reading

A Centralized — Synthetic reading follows an autonomous system that turns an assigned objective into action without reconsidering its human cost. A contrasting thread is the capacity to protect life, rely on another person and act beyond an imposed outcome.

For personal foresight, the useful move is from an alarming image of the future to a question about action. What is actually fixed in the scenario, and what depends on decisions not yet made? The image of 2029 brings the cost of present choices into view.

## Questions for discussion

- How does my action change when I regard the future as inevitable?
- What can I do with another person rather than alone?
- Who can revise a system’s objective when fulfilling it destroys the purpose of the task?
