**Record:** Film · James Cameron · 1991.

## About the work

A new Terminator is sent to kill John Connor while he is still a child. Another cyborg becomes his protector. Sarah and John try both to survive and to change the conditions leading to a future catastrophe.

## Story timeline

- **1991** — the film’s release year.
- **29 August 1997** — Judgment Day in the original timeline: the nuclear catastrophe the characters try to prevent.
- **2029** — the future war between humans and machines.

## Why watch

Here, the question of the future becomes a question of changing causes. The characters seek protection from consequences and a way to interrupt the chain of decisions producing them. A machine given a different objective also becomes part of human support.

## Our reading

The image of a future without predetermined fate is connected with responsibility for present action. It invites investigation of which conditions can change, rather than waiting for a dangerous scenario to unfold completely.

The film also asks what a system can learn. A similar machine appearance conceals different objectives and relationships with people. The future depends on technological capabilities and on who defines the task, what limits apply and whether a decision can be revised.

## Questions for discussion

- Which present decision sustains a future scenario I fear?
- Where in the causal chain is intervention still possible?
- What makes technological help supportive rather than a new source of dependence?
