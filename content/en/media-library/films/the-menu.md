**Record:** Film · Mark Mylod · 2022.

## About the work

A couple travels to an island to dine at an exclusive restaurant, where the chef prepares an unusual meal. The promise of an exceptional experience becomes part of an unsettling situation.

## Why watch

The film can be read through the relationships between a creator, service workers and the recipient of a service. The question is what happens to craft when its value is increasingly defined by status, consumption rituals and the expectations of a select audience.

## In Future Decoder discussions

The Menu was mentioned alongside Triangle of Sadness to explain who might enjoy Saltburn.

## Our reading

Demonstrable quality and recognition within a particular social world can diverge. The expectation of exclusivity may obscure a simple question: whom does the result serve, and how? The film allows us to examine when commitment to craft turns into serving status and other people’s expectations.

## Questions for discussion

- Who defines the value of craft: its maker, its recipient, or a circle of recognition?
- When does the symbol of exclusivity displace the purpose of a result?
