**Record:** Anthology series · created by Charlie Brooker · first released in 2011.

## About the work

An anthology of stories about human relationships, technology and the consequences of new possibilities. Different stories offer different circumstances and points of view.

## Why watch

The series offers questions pushed to their limits: when does a convenient service become an unavoidable environment, who defines good behaviour, and what does leaving the system cost?

## In Future Decoder discussions

The series was recalled as an image of universal rating. In the education discussion, this opened a question about freedom from continuous assessment: what is lost when every action becomes an occasion for judgement?

## Our reading

Feedback can help someone choose a next step, while a rating may determine access to opportunities. The greater the consequences, the more the criteria, room for error and ability to challenge a decision matter. Visibility can reveal a contribution, but it can also encourage conformity to an assessor’s expectations.

## Questions for discussion

- Which decision does this assessment actually improve?
- Can people learn, make mistakes and experiment without a continuous public rating?
