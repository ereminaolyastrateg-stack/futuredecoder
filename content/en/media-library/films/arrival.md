**Record:** Film · Denis Villeneuve · 2016.

## About the work

Linguist Louise Banks is recruited to establish contact with beings that have arrived on Earth. Learning to communicate becomes connected with her experience of time and the choices she makes.

## Why watch

The film invites us to examine the limits of familiar descriptions: can we notice that an unfamiliar situation works differently from our first model? It also raises the question of choice when a person can imagine both a desired outcome and its cost.

## Our reading

New knowledge can change the frame of a question as well as its answer. Comparing the film and novella reveals what is lost and gained when the form of a story changes. The fictional experience of time invites us to ask how an image of the future influences a decision made today.

## Questions for discussion

- When is revising the frame more useful than finding another answer within it?
- Does knowing a possible cost change what a person chooses?
