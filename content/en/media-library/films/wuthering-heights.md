**Record:** Film · Emerald Fennell · 2026.

## About the work

Emerald Fennell’s adaptation of Emily Brontë’s novel, starring Margot Robbie and Jacob Elordi. It is a distinct screen interpretation of familiar literary material.

## Why watch

The question is how a cultural canon is reworked: what a contemporary production makes visible, what it changes, and which audience expectations it brings to the story. Comparison with the novel can go beyond checking individual plot events.

## In Future Decoder discussions

The adaptation was compared with the novel and Saltburn. The conversation connected Jacob Elordi’s screen persona, an association with K-pop aesthetics and an image of inner anger capable of destroying relationships.

## Our reading

A Decentralized — Synthetic reading follows people trying to deal with their desires and resentments alone, unable to ask for help or relate their choices to another person. An attractive appearance intensifies the pull but does not remove anger’s destructive force.

Comparison with the novel and Saltburn reveals changes in the cultural image of desire. What does a viewer recognise as beauty, strength and independence — and when does the isolation behind them become visible?
