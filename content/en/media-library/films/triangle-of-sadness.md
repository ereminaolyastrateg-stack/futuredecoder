**Record:** Film · Ruben Östlund · 2022.

## About the work

A model couple joins a luxury cruise; a disaster leaves the survivors on a deserted island. Established forms of status confront circumstances that demand different abilities.

## Why watch

The film helps distinguish a place in a hierarchy from the ability to sustain shared life. Under changed conditions, previously recognised value may lose its force while overlooked work becomes essential.

## In Future Decoder discussions

Triangle of Sadness was mentioned alongside The Menu as a reference point for recommending Saltburn: uncomfortable social relations and a familiar order in crisis.

## Our reading

Centralized — Synthetic appears here as the habit of treating hierarchy as a ready-made scheme: roles are allocated, rules established and a life path apparently predictable. When conditions change, following the scheme stops helping. A gap becomes visible between familiar status and the ability to understand a situation independently.

The film raises a question about the cost of complete predictability: what happens to judgement and responsibility when someone expects the system to have decided everything? A new resource holder does not automatically create new rules either. What matters is whether other people can influence the conditions of shared life.

## Questions for discussion

- What creates power: recognised status, a resource, or a useful ability?
- How can rules change without reproducing the same dependence under a new resource holder?
