Books and films help us explore different ways of living, learning and acting. The catalogue brings together works, questions and readings through Future Decoder scenarios.

## Images of worlds

### Dune: the price of a shared future

In the discussion of education and agency, the Dune cycle became an image of a decision taken for a shared future that affects many people. The Golden Path raises a question: who is entitled to determine the price of that future for everyone else? A collective goal needs to be considered alongside a person’s right to refuse or choose another path.

Related works: [[en/media-library/books/dune|Dune, novel]]; [[en/media-library/films/dune|Dune, film]].

### Fallout: who sets an environment’s rules

Vaults from Fallout help distinguish diversity in the organisation of environments from a person’s agency within them. Different specialisations do not by themselves answer who assigned them, who can change the rules or whether moving between them is possible. The analogy opens questions about educational systems where choosing between existing options is not yet the same as being able to create one’s own.
