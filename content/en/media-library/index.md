## Films and series

- [[en/media-library/films/1984|Nineteen Eighty-Four]] (1984) — helps explore how language, surveillance, and rewritten facts become an infrastructure of power.
- [[en/media-library/films/2001-a-space-odyssey|2001: A Space Odyssey]] (1968) — helps explore the limits of trust in an autonomous machine and the human place beside it.
- [[en/media-library/films/avatar|Avatar]] (2009) — helps explore the conflict between technological expansion, living ecosystems, and local knowledge.
- [[en/media-library/films/wall-e|WALL-E]] (2008) — helps explore what happens when consumption and automation displace care for the environment and the body.
- [[en/media-library/films/enemy-of-the-state|Enemy of the State]] (1998) — helps explore how convenient surveillance infrastructure shifts the balance between security and freedom.
- [[en/media-library/films/in-time|In Time]] (2011) — helps explore a world where lifespan becomes currency and the main mechanism of inequality.
- [[en/media-library/films/total-recall|Total Recall]] (1990) — helps explore whether experience can remain one's own when memory is engineered by a system.
- [[en/media-library/films/gattaca|Gattaca]] (1997) — helps explore how biological data becomes a social verdict and what can break it.
- [[en/media-library/films/the-hunger-games|The Hunger Games]] (2012) — helps explore how a powerful center turns violence at the periphery into mass entertainment.
- [[en/media-library/films/wuthering-heights|Wuthering Heights]] (2026) — helps explore how a new adaptation repackages a cultural canon for today's attention economy.
- [[en/media-library/films/johnny-mnemonic|Johnny Mnemonic]] (1995) — helps explore the human as data carrier and the bodily cost of the information economy.
- [[en/media-library/films/brave-new-world|Brave New World]] (2020) — helps explore how comfort, entertainment, and managed emotion voluntarily suppress autonomy.
- [[en/media-library/films/dune|Dune]] (2021) — helps explore resource power, colonial vision, and the dependence of large systems on place.
- [[en/media-library/films/a-clockwork-orange|A Clockwork Orange]] (1971) — helps explore where public safety ends and coercive correction of the individual begins.
- [[en/media-library/films/cybervillage|Cybervillage]] (2023) — helps explore how advanced technology can enter a local way of life without destroying it.
- [[en/media-library/films/the-congress|The Congress]] (2013) — helps explore the fading boundary between reality, human image, and a marketable digital identity.
- [[en/media-library/films/the-beauty|The Beauty]] (2026) — helps explore the price of standardized beauty and the body becoming a managed product.
- [[en/media-library/films/ice-age-dawn-of-the-dinosaurs|Ice Age: Dawn of the Dinosaurs]] (2009) — helps explore how a group adapts to an unknown environment and benefits from different roles.
- [[en/media-library/films/the-matrix|The Matrix]] (1999) — helps explore a system that extracts value from people while constructing their reality.
- [[en/media-library/films/back-to-the-future|Back to the Future]] (1985) — helps explore how a small action changes a system's trajectory and its image of the possible future.
- [[en/media-library/films/never-let-me-go|Never Let Me Go]] (2010) — helps explore what remains of humanism when some lives are institutionally designed to serve others.
- [[en/media-library/films/niu-lai|Niu Lai / Here Comes the Cow]] (2026) — helps explore how an imperfect handmade work became a viral answer to polished mass and AI-generated content.
- [[en/media-library/films/dead-poets-society|Dead Poets Society]] (1989) — helps explore education as a space for voice, choice, and resistance to a prewritten life.
- [[en/media-library/films/the-island|The Island]] (2005) — helps explore the ethical limit of biotechnology and a created person's right to self-determination.
- [[en/media-library/films/wag-the-dog|Wag the Dog]] (1997) — helps explore how political reality can be manufactured through spectacle and collective attention.
- [[en/media-library/films/arrival|Arrival]] (2016) — helps explore how language changes the perception of time, choice, and the ability to understand the other.
- [[en/media-library/films/the-handmaids-tale|The Handmaid's Tale]] (2017) — helps explore how crisis is used to normalize control over body, family, and choice.
- [[en/media-library/films/the-rum-diary|The Rum Diary]] (2011) — helps explore how personal choice collides with capital, colonial profit, and professional integrity.
- [[en/media-library/films/sexmission|Sexmission]] (1984) — helps explore how a closed system rewrites history and stabilizes a convenient model of gender and power.
- [[en/media-library/films/surrogates|Surrogates]] (2009) — helps explore what people lose by transferring bodily risk and social presence to ideal avatars.
- [[en/media-library/films/triangle-of-sadness|Triangle of Sadness]] (2022) — helps explore why breaking an old hierarchy does not by itself create a fair system.
- [[en/media-library/films/f1|F1]] (2025) — helps explore how experience, feedback, and a team system turn individual mastery into results.
- [[en/media-library/films/what-women-want|What Women Want]] (2000) — helps explore the temptation to obtain empathy technologically without the long practice of listening.
- [[en/media-library/films/black-mirror|Black Mirror]] (2011) — helps explore how separate conveniences accumulate into new norms of attention, reputation, and control.
- [[en/media-library/films/fahrenheit-451|Fahrenheit 451]] (1966) — helps explore a society voluntarily trading difficult reading for screens, speed, and emotional comfort.
- [[en/media-library/films/i-robot|I, Robot]] (2004) — helps explore why formally safe optimization can come into conflict with human freedom.
- [[en/media-library/films/three-body-problem|3 Body Problem]] (2024) — helps explore how civilizations make decisions when a threat exceeds the scale of a human lifetime.
- [[en/media-library/films/dont-look-up|Don't Look Up]] (2021) — helps explore why knowledge of a threat does not become action when politics, markets, and crowds set the agenda.
- [[en/media-library/films/leave-the-world-behind|Leave the World Behind]] (2023) — helps explore the fragility of digital infrastructure, trust, and household autonomy during systemic failure.

- [[en/media-library/books/we|We]] (1924) — helps explore a mathematically ordered society without privacy, chance, or personal choice.
- [[en/media-library/books/brave-new-world|Brave New World]] (1932) — helps explore voluntary unfreedom sustained by comfort, entertainment, and managed emotion.
- [[en/media-library/books/fahrenheit-451|Fahrenheit 451]] (1953) — helps explore how short emotional content displaces reading and the capacity for difficult thought.
- [[en/media-library/books/a-clockwork-orange|A Clockwork Orange]] (1962) — helps explore the price of safety achieved through coercive alteration of personality.
- [[en/media-library/books/the-handmaids-tale|The Handmaid's Tale]] (1985) — helps explore how an emergency becomes a durable order controlling body and family.

- [[en/media-library/books/the-three-body-problem|The Three-Body Problem]] (2006) — helps explore long-horizon decisions and conflicting human strategies in the face of a civilizational threat.

- [[en/media-library/books/hyperion|Hyperion]] (1989) — helps explore multiple human perspectives on a shared technological and civilizational crisis.
- [[en/media-library/books/the-futurological-congress|The Futurological Congress]] (1971) — helps explore how chemically and medially produced reality destroys confidence in one's own perception.

- [[en/media-library/books/i-robot|I, Robot]] (1950) — helps explore the paradoxes of safety rules and human responsibility for goals given to machines.
- [[en/media-library/books/the-gods-themselves|The Gods Themselves]] (1972) — helps explore second-order consequences of a profitable solution that a system prefers not to see.
- [[en/media-library/books/klara-and-the-sun|Klara and the Sun]] (2021) — helps explore care, replaceability, and human dignity through the eyes of an artificial friend.
- [[en/media-library/books/snuff|S.N.U.F.F.]] (2011) — helps explore media as a weapon for manufacturing war, enemies, and audience consent.
- [[en/media-library/books/the-rum-diary|The Rum Diary]] (1998) — helps explore professional integrity beside capital, tourism, and colonial profit.
- [[en/media-library/books/around-the-world-in-eighty-days|Around the World in Eighty Days]] (1872) — helps explore how infrastructure and standardized time changed the sense of an achievable world.
- [[en/media-library/books/future-shock|Future Shock]] (1970) — helps explore why the speed of change overloads the mind and how to prepare for repeated transitions.
- [[en/media-library/books/homo-prospectus|Homo Prospectus]] (2016) — helps explore the ability to imagine futures as a basic human function shaping decisions.
- [[en/media-library/books/power-and-progress|Power and Progress]] (2023) — helps explore why technological progress does not guarantee shared prosperity without institutions and distributional struggle.

- [[en/media-library/books/cultural-codes-of-economics|The Cultural Codes of Economics]] (2022) — helps explore how values and informal norms shape institutions, motivation, and economic choices.

- [[en/media-library/books/atomic-habits|Atomic Habits]] (2018) — helps explore small repeatable changes as a practical bridge between a desired future and the present.
- [[en/media-library/books/the-jedi-path|The Jedi Path]] (2021) — helps explore managing limited cognitive resources without the illusion of endless productivity.
- [[en/media-library/books/the-wealth-of-nations|The Wealth of Nations]] (1776) — helps explore the roots of modern thinking about markets, specialization, and collective wealth.
- [[en/media-library/books/thinking-fast-and-slow|Thinking, Fast and Slow]] (2011) — helps explore how fast reactions and slow thinking build different decisions under uncertainty.
- [[en/media-library/books/the-culture-map|The Culture Map]] (2014) — helps explore why the same behavior is interpreted differently across cultural systems.
- [[en/media-library/books/risk-uncertainty-and-profit|Risk, Uncertainty and Profit]] (1921) — helps explore the difference between measurable risk and uncertainty where the map of possibilities is incomplete.
