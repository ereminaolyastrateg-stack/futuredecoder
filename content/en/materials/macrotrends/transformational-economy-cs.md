*[[en/materials/macrotrends/transformational-economy|Transformational Economy]] is a macrotrend about how the idea of value changes when growth is no longer the norm. This is its version in the **[[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]]** world, where a platform counts price, rating and status.*

## 2035. A valuation in one minute

Marina wants a loan for her workshop. The bank doesn't ask for a business plan: in one minute an agent gathers her purchase history, the valuation of her flat and a painting she bought five years ago, and offers a rate. The painting, it turns out, has gone up in value — the gallery's AI adviser once suggested she buy it. Marina is pleased: everything is fast and clear. In the evening she opens the app where her "level" has gone up one step, and notices that her favourite café no longer appears for her — it "doesn't fit her profile". Her friend Oleg, who sells wooden spoons he carves, has been turned down: handmade work has no price history, and the system doesn't know what it is worth.

## Where we come from

Value has always been counted by intermediaries: appraisers, banks, critics, shops. Digital platforms took over this role and made it instant. When growth stops being the norm and money gets more expensive, people more readily hand the choice to whoever counts it reliably. [[en/materials/macrotrends/transformational-economy#where-we-are-now|More — history and numbers]].

## What the world becomes

In CS, value is what can be counted. The platform builds "value ladders": segments, levels, subscriptions, ratings. Trust and predictability become a premium product, and steady mid-sized companies win over the race for hypergrowth. Anything with a data history — a flat, a painting, a reputation — turns into an asset and collateral. Anything without a data history risks being left with no price at all.

## What it gives and what it costs

**What it gives.** Clarity: you know what your things are worth, where to grow and what you get at the next level. Decisions are fast, there are fewer mistakes, and quality is held by a standard.

**What it costs.** You are rated rather than doing the rating. Value that cannot be counted — handmade work, friendship, the local café — drops out of the results. Synthetic goods are made for everyone, handmade ones for the few.

## What was said at the lecture

### Synthetic for everyone, handmade for the few

The CS answer to a constrained economy is scale: make things cheap and in large numbers. The flip side is an inverted pyramid of value.

> everything synthetic is produced in huge quantities for the masses, and handmade work counts as luxury and is made for the few

<!-- Lecture «The Future Has Changed Hands», 11.11.2026, answer to a question from the audience. Translated from Russian. -->

### The pyramid can be turned over

The same technology can work the other way: helping make things of handmade quality for many. Then CS value is not a person's rating but access to good things.

> This pyramid can be turned the other way round.

<!-- Same source. -->

### Whoever counts, decides

If an agent counts the price, the rating and the recommendation, power belongs to whoever sets it up. The CS question is not "what is this worth" but "who decided it is worth that".

<!-- Author's framing of the lecture point "whoever routes demand decides what a person will see". -->

## What you can do now

1. Check which of your things an algorithm already rates: credit score, ratings in services, purchase profile.
2. Before buying on an AI's advice, ask who set up the adviser and how it makes money.
3. Keep a record of what cannot be counted: handmade work, reviews, your reputation with people.
4. Don't hand an agent decisions that change your life for a long time without a second look of your own.

## Transformational Economy in other worlds

The worlds differ in who decides what things are worth:

- **CS — the algorithm.** Price, rating and asset value.
- **[[en/materials/macrotrends/transformational-economy-ce|CE]] — the system.** What counts as enough and what to protect.
- **[[en/materials/macrotrends/transformational-economy-ds|DS]] — you yourself.** Your hand, your name and a direct link to the buyer.
- **[[en/materials/macrotrends/transformational-economy-de|DE]] — your own people.** Exchange, gifts and a life that is "enough".
