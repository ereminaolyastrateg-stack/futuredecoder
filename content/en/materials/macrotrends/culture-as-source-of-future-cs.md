*[[en/materials/macrotrends/culture-as-source-of-future|Culture as a Source of the Future]] is a macrotrend about who writes the images of the future people live by. This is its version in the **[[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]]** world, where they are written by platforms, agencies and recommendation algorithms.*

## 2035. A film for everyone

Director Kamila gets a brief from the studio: a film for two hundred countries, a 16-year-old hero, the theme "accept your dark side". The studio's AI has already worked out from viewing data which twists keep viewers to the end and has made a storyboard. Kamila rewrites the dialogue so it sounds alive and fights for one scene the algorithm doesn't like. The scene stays. Six months later the film is in the global top; children in Lima, Seoul and Kazan sing the same song. Kamila is glad everyone saw the film. But the studio turns down her next idea — about the village where she grew up: "no data that people will watch it".

## Where we come from

Culture was always expensive, and it was produced by those with the means: the court, the church, the publisher, the film studio. Streaming and platforms made culture instantly available worldwide, and recommendation algorithms began deciding what people see. Now the platform is producer, distributor and critic at once. [[en/materials/macrotrends/culture-as-source-of-future#where-we-are-now|More — history and figures]].

## What the world becomes

In CS culture scales through platforms and data-driven curation. "Big culture" becomes more accessible, global stories gather fandoms in every country, and AI helps with production, restoration and explanation. Art becomes an asset: it is valued, borrowed against, and people ask AI what to buy. The image of the future for millions is chosen by whoever owns the story world and the recommendations.

## What it gives and what it costs

**What it gives.** Global hits and access to everything: any story, museum or concert is on your phone. A shared cultural language for teenagers all over the world.

**What it costs.** Uniformity: what isn't in the data doesn't get made. Culture becomes an asset and a dataset, and the image of the future is written by platform owners.

## What you can do now

1. Look at what stories about the future you have seen in the past month, and who chose them — you or the recommendations.
2. Once a week watch or read something the algorithm didn't suggest.
3. Talk with your children about what their favourite films and bands say about the future and about them.
4. If you work in culture, keep the scenes and ideas "not supported by data" and look for another way to the audience.

## Culture as a Source of the Future in other worlds

The worlds differ in who writes the image of the future:

- **CS — platforms.** Streaming, agencies and recommendations.
- **[[en/materials/macrotrends/culture-as-source-of-future-ce|CE]] — the state.** Museums, school, big cultural projects and values.
- **[[en/materials/macrotrends/culture-as-source-of-future-ds|DS]] — the author.** A personal hand and a direct link to the audience.
- **[[en/materials/macrotrends/culture-as-source-of-future-de|DE]] — the community.** Fandoms, memes and local scenes.
