*TR-2026-11-01 · AI as the go-between for people and the world*

**In brief:** AI moves into the physical world and becomes an agent that searches, buys and decides for us.

**Period:** November 2026.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-047 — Advanced AI systems and cognitive computing; MT-002 — Human–machine interaction; MT-081 — Ecosystems, super-apps and the platform economy.

**Life domains:** [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/everyday-life|Everyday life, consumption and leisure]]; [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance|Finance]].

### Description

AI moves into the physical world and becomes an agent that searches, buys and decides for us. The question is whose agent it is and what values it was trained on.
