*TR-2026-11-02 · The entrance to work is closing*

**In brief:** AI is not yet destroying professions, but it is changing who gets hired into them.

**Period:** November 2026.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-066 — Labour-market transformation and talent management; MT-007 — Automation of labour; MT-014 — Gig economy.

**Life domains:** [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/work|Work]]; [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education|Education]].  
**Thematic tags:** first job.

### Description

AI is not yet destroying professions, but it is changing who gets hired into them. The main fear is not a machine uprising but becoming unnecessary.
