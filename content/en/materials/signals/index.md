These overviews bring together concrete examples of change and possible futures: educational practices, new formats, research proposals and speculative projects. Each example has a source and an explanation of what it helps us discuss and which conclusions it does not establish.

The overviews preserve the context of games: how an example was read, and which questions and objections arose. Scenario hypotheses remain separate from descriptions of the projects.
