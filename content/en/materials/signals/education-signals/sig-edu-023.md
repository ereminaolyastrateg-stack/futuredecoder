*SIG-EDU-023 · Critical AI Literacy 2035*

**In brief:** Working with AI includes examining bias, ethics and one’s own judgement.

**Date / period:** 2025 — project; 2035 — scenario horizon.  
**Geography:** United Kingdom — RCA; Brazil — the education system explored.  
**Type and stage:** Speculative design research and learning activities.  
**Scenario:** DS — Decentralized — Synthetic.

**Why this scenario:** The learner is asked to retain their own judgement: notice AI bias, examine an answer and decide what to accept or change. Using the tool rests on an independent intellectual position.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-047 — Advanced AI systems and cognitive computing; MT-044 — Social trust and digital ethics.

**Life domains:** Education; Work; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** Higher education; design; AI literacy.  
**Thematic tags:** AI literacy, bias, authorship.

### Description

Critical AI Literacy 2035 explores possible futures for Brazilian design education. Participatory workshops, future prototyping and backcasting connect a preferred 2035 situation to potential earlier steps. Educators support critical AI use and discussion of bias, ethics and local authorship.

Three teaching briefs address idea generation, prototyping and AI-mediated collaboration. A roadmap moves from experimentation in 2025–2027 towards institutional development and changes in how knowledge is understood by 2035. It proposes communities of practice, guidelines and leadership development. This is a designed pathway for change rather than a report that all these measures have already been implemented in Brazil.

### Why this is a signal

Being able to use a tool and being able to assess its limitations become distinct educational tasks.

### Limits of the conclusion

2035 is the research horizon, not the date of an already completed transformation of Brazilian higher education.
