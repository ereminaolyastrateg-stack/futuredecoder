*SIG-EDU-036 · Learning Planet Youth Design Challenge*

**In brief:** Young project authors receive support to develop their own initiative.

**Date / period:** 2026–2027 edition; launch on 22 September 2026.  
**Geography:** France; international programme.  
**Type and stage:** Challenge and support programme for youth-led social projects.  
**Scenario:** DE — Decentralized — Earth.

**Why this scenario:** An author develops a social project and learns from emerging tasks, difficulties and responses. Learning is connected to what is discovered while trying to change a real situation.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-054 — Designing futures and strategic vision; MT-031 — Human mastery.

**Life domains:** Education; Work; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** Education; social entrepreneurship.  
**Thematic tags:** #theme/youth-project #theme/learning-through-action #theme/public-benefit

### Description

Learning Planet Youth Design Challenge supports social-impact projects by people aged 15–26. Online proposals address peace and democracy, mental health and wellbeing, or environment and climate. Participants' own ideas provide the basis for learning.

The 2026–2027 edition describes online training, mentoring, masterclasses and practical tools for 60 shortlisted projects. A jury then selects 12 projects whose representatives receive grants and further networking support. These benefits follow selection rather than going to every applicant. The signal connects education with action: participants develop a real proposal, receive feedback and learn to explain it to others.

### Why this is a signal

The need for new knowledge emerges within a real attempt to change a situation. Programme support does not guarantee a project’s outcome.

### Limits of the conclusion

The programme supports selected projects. Support and stated intentions are not the same as achieved social impact.
