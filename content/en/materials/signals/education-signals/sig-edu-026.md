*SIG-EDU-026 · Bredagora*

**In brief:** The learning day follows a student’s questions, with a coach’s support.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** Netherlands, Breda.  
**Type and stage:** Agora-school practice with personal coaches.  
**Scenario:** DS — Decentralized — Synthetic.

**Why this scenario:** The learner starts from their own questions and interests and chooses what and how to study. A coach helps them follow that route and notice gaps.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-031 — Human mastery; MT-038 — Inequality and resource distribution.

**Life domains:** Education; Close relationships and family.  
**Industries:** School education.  
**Thematic tags:** choice, coach, independence.

### Description

Bredagora describes learning through students' own questions and challenges. Each learner has a coach and considerable control over the day. Individual and group projects replace a fixed sequence of subject lessons.

The published school-day model still provides structure: a coach-group morning meeting, project time, visiting experts, a quiet screen-free period and a shared closing. Parents and students can offer inspiration sessions; subject specialists and coach feedback support the work. The format combines choice with rhythm and community support. The absence of a conventional subject timetable does not mean an absence of structure, adults or expectations for learning outcomes.

### Why this is a signal

Self-directed choice and support can be combined. Freedom over the timetable raises the question of how a person notices gaps and obtains help.

### Game discussions

**Centralized — Earth.** A flexible timetable was read as an opportunity to organise one’s route and find a suitable environment. The coach does not disappear.

### Limits of the conclusion
