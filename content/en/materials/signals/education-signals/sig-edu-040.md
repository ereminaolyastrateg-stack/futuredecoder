*SIG-EDU-040 · Sora Schools*

**In brief:** A student’s own project is connected to demonstrated curricular units.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** United States; online school.  
**Type and stage:** School model with projects, mentors and academic requirements.  
**Scenario:** [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|DE — Decentralized — Earth]].

**Why this scenario:** A learner develops a project, and acquired knowledge and abilities are linked to what the work demonstrates. The next step can be refined through the result and mentors’ feedback.

**Macrotrend drivers:** [[en/materials/macrotrends/education-knowledge-literacy-de|MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy]]; [[en/materials/macrotrends/human-mastery-de|MT-031 — Human mastery]]; MT-044 — Social trust and digital ethics.

**Life domains:** Education; Close relationships and family.  
**Industries:** School education; online education.  
**Thematic tags:** student project, academic credit, mentor.

### Description

Sora Schools organises learning in twelve-week cycles. Students choose interdisciplinary “expeditions” and work on projects with subject experts. Initially, staff design the projects; as students learn the process, they are encouraged to develop their own topics and proposals.

Each cycle requires a final deliverable demonstrating curricular units and abilities. Experts help find resources, give feedback and maintain breadth. Students can see completed requirements and areas needing more work. Independent activity combines with live sessions and community meetings. Personal projects shape much of the journey while required subject outcomes and graduation standards remain in place.

### Why this is a signal

A student’s interests become an entry into learning, while credit, breadth of preparation and required content still need assessment.

### Limits of the conclusion

The school has required curricular units, support and checkpoints. A project does not remove the criteria for academic credit.
