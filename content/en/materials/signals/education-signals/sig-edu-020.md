*SIG-EDU-020 · University of the Underground*

**In brief:** An independent initiative challenges familiar boundaries of the university.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** Netherlands; United Kingdom; transnational programme.  
**Type and stage:** Independent educational and cultural initiative.  
**Scenario:** [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|CE — Centralized — Earth]].

**Why this scenario:** The educational community states shared principles: free provision for students, pluralism and transnationality. These define which forms of learning and participation it values.

**Macrotrend drivers:** [[en/materials/macrotrends/education-knowledge-literacy-ce|MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy]]; MT-029 — Crisis of social institutions; MT-030 — Culture as a source of futures.

### Description

University of the Underground is an educational and cultural initiative associated with urban spaces and nightclub basements. Its mission combines education free to students, pluralistic thinking and work across national borders. Design, film, music, theatre and political thought help investigate institutions and power.

Its archive includes programmes on Afrofuturisms and new politics, music initiatives and research residencies between cities. Learning involves creating situations, events and artistic work that invite public engagement with social change. It also experiments with funding: long-term support and donations are intended to widen access. Qualification status and content depend on the particular programme and academic partners.

### Why this is a signal

The legitimacy of a learning environment is connected to critical practice and community. Free provision is a stated principle, not a property of all learning.

### Game discussions

**Centralized — Earth.** The possibility of learning in unfamiliar places expanded the idea of an educational environment. Questions remained about the quality of support, the process of learning and recognition of the result. An independent initiative does not imply that institutions disappear or that anyone can teach anything to anyone.

### Limits of the conclusion

Free education and pluralism are stated principles of the initiative. They do not establish equal access to every educational route.
