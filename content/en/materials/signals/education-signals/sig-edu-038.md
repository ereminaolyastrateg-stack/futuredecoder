**In brief:** A group repeats and changes a workshop format to explore how learning happens.

### Description

Figuring Things Out Together is a Hackers & Designers publication about collective learning and workshop formats. Instructions, conversations and accounts of re-enactments make practices transferable: another group can perform a script, annotate it and adapt it.

One approach examines familiar workshop props, such as sticky notes, boards and chair arrangements. Participants replace them with self-made alternatives and observe the consequences; another exercise physically rehearses a workshop at high speed. The aim is to notice how spaces and actions shape collaboration. Different versions and open questions present learning as shared work that can continue and change in another context.

### Why this is a signal

The collaborative learning process becomes an object of experimentation: instructions, materials and roles can be changed to explore their effects.

### Game discussions

**Decentralized — Earth.** Making things together was discussed as a way to clarify a solution that is not yet contained in a finished plan. Feedback comes from materials and other people. An unknown solution does not mean an absence of goals, skills or constraints; project uncertainty alone is not a new social rule.

### Limits of the conclusion

Repeating and altering a workshop provides material for investigating a particular setting. It does not establish a universal recipe for learning.
