*SIG-EDU-027 · College Unbound*

**In brief:** An adult’s educational plan is connected to their life and work.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** United States.  
**Type and stage:** Higher-education model for adults.  
**Scenario:** DS — Decentralized — Synthetic.

**Why this scenario:** An adult develops a personal plan from their own life and professional challenges. Their experience and chosen goal become the basis of the educational route.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-031 — Human mastery; MT-066 — Transformation of work and talent management.

**Life domains:** Education; Work; Close relationships and family.  
**Industries:** Higher education; adult learning.  
**Thematic tags:** #theme/life-experience #theme/personal-plan #theme/portfolio

### Description

At College Unbound, adults create a Personal Learning Plan from day one, connecting education to work, experience and future goals. Each student develops a project and presents progress publicly for feedback. A cohort and accompanying faculty provide continuing support.

Assessment includes portfolios and reflection on learning. Ten competencies include collaboration, critical thinking, accountability and agency. Experience from employment, caregiving or community participation can support academic credit when learning is documented and assessed by faculty. The college recognises knowledge gained in life through an explicit process that retains requirements for evidence.

### Why this is a signal

Previous experience becomes material for learning and assessment. Criteria, support and degree requirements remain in place.

### Limits of the conclusion

A personal plan operates within an educational model and assessment procedures, rather than replacing all requirements.
