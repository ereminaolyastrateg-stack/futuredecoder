*SIG-EDU-034 · Ficta Ordo*

**In brief:** Possible future orders are explored through rituals, film and space.

**Date / period:** October 2025 — June 2026.  
**Geography:** United Kingdom, RCA.  
**Type and stage:** Speculative educational framework and student films.  
**Scenario:** DE — Decentralized — Earth.

**Why this scenario:** A possible rule is tried through ritual, action and an artistic situation. Its consequences become something to experience and discuss, after which the proposal can be reconsidered.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-054 — Designing futures and strategic vision; MT-030 — Culture as a source of futures.

**Life domains:** Education; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** Design education; film; cultural research.  
**Thematic tags:** rituals, future norms, rehearsal.

### Description

Ficta Ordo was a 2025–2026 superFUTURES project about future rituals, habits and everyday order. Students worked alongside a network of anthropologists, ethnographers and cultural researchers. Fiction, film, space and world-building made alternative ways of living available for examination.

Three strands addressed human relationships with other species; intangible life, including time, faith and meaning; and communities' relationships with digital systems. Repeated actions reveal arrangements of power, permission, responsibility and belonging. Student films present the resulting worlds. The educational task is to give a proposed order a concrete form and investigate it through events and actions within the fiction.

### Why this is a signal

A rule can be considered through imagined action and lived experience. Rehearsal helps raise questions about its consequences.

### Game discussions

**Decentralized — Earth.** A ritual action was seen as a way to try a possible future rule. A suggestion was to rehearse a rule through a small shared action and then discuss the experience. Designing a norm, rehearsing it and having society adopt it need to remain distinct: an experiment does not establish that the rule is lasting or desirable outside it.

### Limits of the conclusion

A film or ritual rehearsal does not establish public acceptance of the proposed order.
