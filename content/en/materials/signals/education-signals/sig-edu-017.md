*SIG-EDU-017 · Kaospilot*

**In brief:** Real projects and teamwork form the basis of learning.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** Denmark.  
**Type and stage:** Three-year educational programme.  
**Scenario:** [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|DE — Decentralized — Earth]].

**Why this scenario:** Participants work in teams on real challenges, receive feedback from people and organisations, and revise their actions along the way. The knowledge needed and the next step emerge through the work itself.

**Thematic tags:** projects, team, change.

### Description

Kaospilot is a three-year Danish programme built around real challenges, teamwork and leading change. It integrates business design, process design, project design and leadership. Participants work individually, in smaller groups and across their team, including with real organisations.

Learning connects theory, action and reflection. Participants encounter tools through practical workshops, apply them in projects and reflect on their experience and next steps. Over time they build a portfolio and experience of working with different people. The continuous connection between action and its consequences matters: the ability to act develops through repeated encounters with a real task, its consequences and the need to revise one's approach.

### Why this is a signal

Professional preparation combines action, theory and reflection. Results need to be considered in relation to the programme’s tasks and conditions.

### Game discussions

**Centralized — Synthetic: a game reading.** Project-based teamwork was connected to a hypothesis that higher levels of qualification bring greater freedom. A counterpoint was that such opportunities already exist. The relationship between level and freedom is a discussion hypothesis, not an established rule of the programme; the original deck assigned Kaospilot to Centralized — Earth.
