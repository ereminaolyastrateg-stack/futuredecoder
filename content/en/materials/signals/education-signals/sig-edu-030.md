*SIG-EDU-030 · Agile Learning Centers*

**In brief:** Learners choose activities and revise their community’s tools.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** International network of learning communities.  
**Type and stage:** Self-directed learning model.  
**Scenario:** DS — Decentralized — Synthetic.

**Why this scenario:** Children determine much of their day, state intentions and participate in choosing community rules. The emphasis is on their right to direct their own learning with support from the environment.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-076 — Digital communities and virtual interaction; [[en/materials/macrotrends/human-mastery-ds|MT-031 — Human mastery]].

**Life domains:** Education; Close relationships and family; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** School and informal education.  
**Thematic tags:** intention, reflection, shared rules.

### Description

Agile Learning Centers is a self-directed education model used by different learning communities. Its core cycle is intention, action, reflection and sharing. Children choose much of their activity, while adults help connect people and resources, address obstacles and support collaboration.

Regular written, drawn or recorded reflections help reveal connections between choices and outcomes. Participants can revise tools and community agreements, try small changes and change back when needed. Independence coexists with responsibility to others and safety boundaries. Practices vary between centres; the common foundation is learning through decisions, their consequences and the shared adaptation of the environment.

### Why this is a signal

Managing the learning day can include cycles of intention, action and reflection. A supportive environment remains a substantial part of the model.

### Limits of the conclusion

Self-direction is built within a community with practices of support and reflection. It is not an absence of an environment or responsibility.
