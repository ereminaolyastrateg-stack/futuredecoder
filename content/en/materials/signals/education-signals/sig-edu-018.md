*SIG-EDU-018 · A School of Schools*

**In brief:** A biennial provides a setting for different ways of learning.

**Date / period:** 2018 — biennial; 22 January 2018 — the organiser’s publication.  
**Geography:** Türkiye, Istanbul.  
**Type and stage:** Biennial exhibition and educational programme.  
**Scenario:** CE — Centralized — Earth.

**Why this scenario:** The biennial makes public the question of what counts as a school and who can organise learning. The focus is on reconsidering the recognised form of education through a cultural institution.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-030 — Culture as a source of futures; MT-029 — Crisis of social institutions.

**Life domains:** Education; Place of living and public environment; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** Design; exhibitions; cultural education.  
**Thematic tags:** #theme/biennial #theme/school #theme/educational-experiment

### Description

A School of Schools was the concept of the fourth Istanbul Design Biennial in 2018. The biennial became a setting for testing ways to learn through urban inquiry, exchanging experience, making and examining education itself. Its framing brought academic and amateur knowledge, generations and disciplines together.

The concept proposed a flexible year-long programme extending beyond the exhibition. Both outcomes and process mattered: how collaboration works, who holds knowledge and what helps people share experience. Museum, laboratory, studio and academy formats became resources for rethinking school. This was a curatorial educational platform rather than a single school awarding a shared qualification to all participants.

### Why this is a signal

A cultural venue can organise learning. The boundary of school becomes a subject of public experimentation.

### Limits of the conclusion

This concerns a particular biennial and its educational programme. An exhibition experiment does not describe the education system as a whole.
