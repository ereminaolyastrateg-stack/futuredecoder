*SIG-EDU-022 · Deepself*

**In brief:** AI is imagined as an intermediary serving the individual’s own interests.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** Netherlands.  
**Type and stage:** Speculative Design Academy Eindhoven installation.  
**Scenario:** [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|DS — Decentralized — Synthetic]].

**Why this scenario:** In this speculative vision, information selection should serve the individual’s own values. The main question is how to give a person influence over what shapes their knowledge and view of the world.

**Macrotrend drivers:** [[en/materials/macrotrends/education-knowledge-literacy-ds|MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy]]; MT-044 — Social trust and digital ethics; MT-081 — Ecosystems, super-apps and the platform economy.

### Description

Deepself is a speculative installation about who shapes the information through which people understand the world. It imagines a technology-rich democracy where AI serves users' interests. Its starting concern is dependence on major technology companies and their commercial priorities.

Mirrors invite visitors to consider their core values and how technology shapes them. This experience raises questions about distributing digital power and retaining the ability to choose a preferred future. For education, it concerns independent judgement and the selection of information sources. Deepself presents an imagined relationship with AI rather than an operational service already guaranteeing users' interests.

### Why this is a signal

Personalisation is considered alongside power over the information environment: whose interests shape selection, and what can the user change?

### Game discussions

**Centralized — Synthetic: a game reading.** Participants imagined a system configurable around their own values, whose recommendations could be checked against the person’s interests. The discussion distinguishes a personal recommendation from the right to control its criteria. Setting preferences does not itself grant control over a platform; Deepself remains a speculative installation.

### Limits of the conclusion

Deepself is an image and an installation, not an available independent AI service with demonstrated protection of user interests.
