*SIG-EDU-032 · FOUNDING LAB*

**In brief:** New university formats are tried with students and visiting researchers.

**Date / period:** 2023–2024.  
**Geography:** Austria, Linz; international participants.  
**Type and stage:** Summer school and experimental semester.  
**Scenario:** DE — Decentralized — Earth.

**Why this scenario:** Future university formats are tested through a summer school and experimental semester. Participants learn inside the prototype while also discovering how learning itself should be organised.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-054 — Designing futures and strategic vision; MT-029 — Crisis of social institutions.

**Life domains:** Education; Work; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** Higher education; digital transformation.  
**Thematic tags:** university, co creation, prototyping.

### Description

FOUNDING LAB was a joint experiment by Ars Electronica and the emerging IT:U university in Linz. A 2023 summer school, public forum and 2023–2024 fall term explored content and formats for interdisciplinary education. Students and visiting researchers tested them through shared work.

The fall term combined six four-day onsite chapters with online mentoring and discussions. Topics covered infrastructure, data and code, machines, interfaces, media and digital society. Participants developed their projects between chapters and presented results at the university's open day and Ars Electronica Center. Learning and institutional design developed together: participants' experience could inform the design of the educational environment itself.

### Why this is a signal

The organisation of an educational institution becomes an object of collaborative design and experimentation.

### Limits of the conclusion

Prototyping formats does not mean that every participant had the sole right to determine how the university would operate.
