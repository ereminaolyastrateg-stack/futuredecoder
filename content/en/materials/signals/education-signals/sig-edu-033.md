*SIG-EDU-033 · Citizen Science in Education*

**In brief:** School students can contribute to real scientific research.

**Date / period:** 4 September 2025 — panel discussion.  
**Geography:** Austria, Linz; international research practices.  
**Type and stage:** Conference discussion of citizen science in education.  
**Scenario:** [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|DE — Decentralized — Earth]].

**Why this scenario:** Learners participate in research whose outcome is not known in advance. Working with methods, data and uncertainty provides feedback that may change the next research step.

**Macrotrend drivers:** [[en/materials/macrotrends/education-knowledge-literacy-de|MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy]]; [[en/materials/macrotrends/human-mastery-de|MT-031 — Human mastery]]; MT-054 — Designing futures and strategic vision.

**Life domains:** [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education-de|Education]]; [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/meaning-de|Values, meaning and community]].  
**Industries:** Science; school education; citizen research.  
**Thematic tags:** citizen science, student researcher, uncertainty.

### Description

Citizen Science in Education was an Ars Electronica 2025 panel about students contributing to real research with scientists and communities. Its framing connects scientific knowledge to issues in learners' surroundings, making methods and uncertainty part of the educational experience.

The panel also examines practical tensions: limited school time and resources, scientific rigour and educational needs. These must be negotiated for participation to serve both research and learners. The signal concerns a public discussion of this model and its constraints. The programme does not describe one implemented course or establish identical educational outcomes across citizen-science projects.

### Why this is a signal

The learner’s role expands to participation in producing knowledge. Scientific rigour, available time and educational goals need to be coordinated.

### Game discussions

**Decentralized — Synthetic.** The learner was considered a researcher who could contribute to how education is organised. This extends the role beyond receiving a ready-made programme. Participation in research does not confer the right to change a standard unilaterally or control the whole learning system.

### Limits of the conclusion

This is a discussion of different practices. Real research requires time, resources, methodological care and scientific rigour.
