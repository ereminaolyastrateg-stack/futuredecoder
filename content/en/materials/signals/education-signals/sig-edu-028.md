*SIG-EDU-028 · School of Machines*

**In brief:** Art, technology and a personal question come together in a practical project.

**Date / period:** n.d.  
**Geography:** Germany, Berlin; international programmes.  
**Type and stage:** Independent hands-on educational programmes.  
**Scenario:** DS — Decentralized — Synthetic.

**Why this scenario:** Technical skills are connected to the participant’s own artistic or research question. Their chosen topic and the work through which they want to express it are central.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-026 — Creative economy; MT-031 — Human mastery.

**Life domains:** Education; Work; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** Art; creative technology; design.  
**Thematic tags:** #theme/art #theme/technology #theme/personal-inquiry

### Description

School of Machines, Making & Make-Believe creates small practical programmes across art, technology and design. Teaching artists and technologists work with the tools they introduce. Participants develop individual and collaborative pieces towards a public exhibition.

The school welcomes people without previous experience and treats personal questions, beliefs and life stories as resources for creative work. Technical learning accompanies developing an expressive purpose and critically examining relationships with technology. Themes, instructors, duration and even the host country vary between programmes. The school takes shape around a particular period of shared practice and work participants can present to others.

### Why this is a signal

A development route can cross professional disciplines. A personal topic, work with tools and discussion of the result become significant.

### Limits of the conclusion

Conditions, duration and the composition of individual programmes vary. Interdisciplinarity alone does not establish the quality of an outcome.
