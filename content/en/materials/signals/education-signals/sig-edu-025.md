*SIG-EDU-025 · P2PU Learning Circles*

**In brief:** A group studies open materials with a facilitator’s support.

**Date / period:** Since 2015 — learning circles with libraries, according to P2PU’s history.  
**Geography:** United States; international library and community spaces.  
**Type and stage:** Facilitated peer-learning methodology.  
**Scenario:** DS — Decentralized — Synthetic.

**Why this scenario:** Participants gather around a chosen topic and study open resources together. A facilitator supports their work, while the group retains agency over its learning direction and participation.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-043 — Education, knowledge and literacy; MT-076 — Digital communities and virtual interaction; MT-038 — Inequality and resource distribution.

**Life domains:** Education; Values, meaning and community.  
**Industries:** Libraries; adult education; open resources.  
**Thematic tags:** #theme/learning-circle #theme/peer-learning #theme/facilitation

### Description

P2PU helps organise free learning circles: small groups meet regularly in libraries, other public spaces or online to study a chosen topic. They may use an open online course, adapt another group's materials or create their own collection.

A facilitator organises meetings and supports collaboration without needing subject expertise. Participants work through materials, share questions and support one another's progress. P2PU supplies tools, guides and a facilitator community. Access to content is thus accompanied by social support: regular time, a meeting place and people to learn with. Groups can reproduce the model locally while choosing topics and materials suited to their interests.

### Why this is a signal

Organising a learning process and providing subject expertise can be distributed between different people and resources.

### Limits of the conclusion

A facilitator supports the process, while the quality of open resources and access to additional expertise remain important.
