*SIG-2026-003 · Open Chinese models*

**In brief:** Chinese labs release open-weight models that can be downloaded, run locally and fine-tuned for one’s own task.

**Date / period:** 2026.  
**Geography:** China; global.  
**Type and stage:** Example from the lecture.  
**Scenario:** [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|DS — Decentralized — Synthetic]].

**Why this scenario:** The person chooses and assembles the tools and is responsible for the outcome.

**Collection trend:** [[en/materials/signals/2026-november-december#trend-1|1. AI as the go-between for people and the world]] — AI moves into the physical world and becomes an agent that searches, buys and decides for us. The question is whose agent it is and what values it was trained on.

**Macrotrend drivers:** MT-047 — Advanced AI systems and cognitive computing; MT-002 — Human–machine interaction; MT-081 — Ecosystems, super-apps and the platform economy.

**Life domains:** [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/everyday-life-ds|Everyday life, consumption and leisure]]; [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance-ds|Finance]].  
**Thematic tags:** ai agents.

### Description

Chinese labs release open-weight models that can be downloaded, run locally and fine-tuned for one’s own task. MIT Technology Review reports that much of the Global South sees this as a path to AI of its own.

### Why this is a signal

An agent stops being only a platform service: you can own one.

### Where it came up

Lecture “The Future Has Changed Hands”, 11 November 2026.

### Limits of the conclusion

The example was used in the lecture to illustrate the trend; figures come from the source given.
