### Description

In August 2025 China’s State Council adopted the “AI+” plan: penetration of smart devices and AI agents above 70% by 2027 and above 90% by 2030. The stated aim is broad access to technology and shared benefits.

### Why this is a signal

AI as infrastructure, like electricity, rather than a product.
