### Description

According to the Art Basel and UBS survey, 69% of collectors buy directly from artists: in studios, on commission, via Instagram. Direct sales account for 19% of spending, more than fairs or auctions.

### Why this is a signal

The showroom middleman fades; a direct link to the maker remains.
