### Description

In its Convergence Outlook 2026, FTSG describes agents that search, negotiate and pay on their own; payment networks are already building protocols for them, such as Mastercard Agent Pay and Visa Trusted Agent Protocol. A purchase becomes a result the person simply receives.

### Why this is a signal

Whoever owns the agent decides what a person gets to see at all.
