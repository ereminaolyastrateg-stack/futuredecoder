### Description

ADP data through June 2026 show that employment of young workers in highly AI-exposed occupations is about 19% below where it would be had it kept pace with peers. The drop comes through reduced hiring; experienced workers show no such gap.

### Why this is a signal

The first rung of a career disappears before the professions do.
