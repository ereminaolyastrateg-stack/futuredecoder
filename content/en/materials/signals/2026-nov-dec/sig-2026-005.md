*SIG-2026-005 · Robots as the workforce*

**In brief:** In its “New Labor Equation” convergence, FTSG writes that companies increasingly own work in the form of agents and robots instead of hiring people.

**Date / period:** 2026.  
**Geography:** global.  
**Type and stage:** Example from the lecture.  
**Scenario:** [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|CS — Centralized — Synthetic]].

**Why this scenario:** A system, platform or infrastructure makes the decision, and the person receives a ready result.

**Collection trend:** [[en/materials/signals/2026-november-december#trend-2|2. The entrance to work is closing]] — AI is not yet destroying professions, but it is changing who gets hired into them. The main fear is not a machine uprising but becoming unnecessary.

### Description

In its “New Labor Equation” convergence, FTSG writes that companies increasingly own work in the form of agents and robots instead of hiring people. Economic growth stops creating jobs.

### Why this is a signal

Power shifts to those who own the automation.

### Where it came up

Lecture “The Future Has Changed Hands”, 11 November 2026.
