### Description

Fiverr’s active buyers fell from 3.4 to 2.7 million in a year (−21.9%), and Upwork’s by 4% to 763,000. On 10 November 2026 OpenAI and Upwork showed that in 43 of 63 work categories, tasks people already do in ChatGPT are losing share of orders, while AI-related projects get larger budgets.

### Why this is a signal

Freelancing stops being a fallback: AI takes the bottom of the market, and complex, expensive work remains.
