### Description

RAND moved from geopolitics and defence topics to AGI in the 2030s as a working hypothesis. Eight scenarios, from a “coalition of democracies” and “Cold War 2” to “The Wild Frontier” and “The AGI Coup”, are built on the axis of how centralised development is.

### Why this is a signal

The centre–decentre axis becomes central to the conversation about AI’s future.
