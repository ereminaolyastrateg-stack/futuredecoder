**In brief:** CIFS’s 2022 megatrends included “Individualisation and empowerment”; the 2026 edition has “Individualised everyday life”: loneliness and small families instead of liberation.

**Date / period:** 2026.  
**Geography:** Denmark; global.  
**Type and stage:** Example from the lecture.

### Description

### Why this is a signal

Institutes describe individualism as a condition, not a promise.
