**Type and stage:** News or event.

### Description

Fiverr’s active buyers fell from 3.4 to 2.7 million. Customers increasingly hand simple tasks such as copywriting, basic design and entry-level coding to AI; the company is turning to higher-value projects.

### Why this is a signal

The bottom of freelance work shrinks first.

### Where the signal comes from

News or event — fiverr.com.

### Limits of the conclusion

This is a single event, not an established practice. The signal shows a direction, not a scale.
