### Description

The kleinkleinklein META 2026 review combined 70+ reports and 400+ trends: the words of the year are “anxiety” and “overload”, and 74% of strategists say there are too many empty trends. Spectacle and goosebumps are overrated; the verification crisis, retraining and loneliness are underrated.

### Why this is a signal

The trend industry has begun to criticise itself: verification with data is valued over flashiness.
