### Description

Agents were expected to bypass Booking and Expedia, yet these became partners of Google’s agentic booking. OpenAI wound down its instant checkout in ChatGPT.

### Why this is a signal

The layer does not vanish; its function shifts from passing on to guaranteeing.

### Where the signal comes from

Case — hospitalitynet.org.

### Limits of the conclusion

This is one case. It shows that it can already be done, not that everyone does it.
