### Description

On 24 September 2026 the Copenhagen Institute for Futures Studies released a new report: 11 megatrends instead of 15 in the previous edition. “Economic Growth” gave way to “The Constrained Economy”, “Globalisation” to “Shifting World Order”, “AI & Automation” to “The Rise of Embedded AI”, “Network Economy” to “Power Through Networks” and “Focus on Health” to “Health Beyond the Clinic”. Individualism is no longer described as liberation. The institute also tightened its definition of a megatrend.

### Why this is a signal

One of the oldest futures institutes no longer treats growth and an open world as the norm, which changes the baseline assumptions of any strategy.
