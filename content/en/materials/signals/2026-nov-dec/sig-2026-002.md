### Description

According to the Art Basel and UBS survey (3,100 wealthy collectors, October 2026), 22% use apps and AI for advice, up from 4% in 2024. Two-thirds see AI as useful for finding artists and checking authenticity.

### Why this is a signal

Even taste and choice in art are starting to be delegated to machines.
