## For people

### Decoder scenario games

Regular open games. In one game the group designs four worlds of the future on a single topic: attention, money, AI, longevity, happiness, work. You see that your question can play out in very different ways, and leave with ideas that had not occurred to you before. Everything participants come up with goes into the [Future Canvas](/static/polotno/en/).

*For:* anyone who wants to think about the future without anxiety. No preparation needed. Games run in English and Russian.

### The Architecture of a Person lab

Regular cohorts of four meetings. You break your life down into eight spheres, choose what you want in each, test it against the four worlds and find a first step with the resources you have now. The method stays with you to keep using. Personal maps are never discussed in public.

*For:* people who say "I don't like what I have, and I don't know what I want". Often it is a time of choosing a path or rebuilding a life.

### Personal strategy

One-to-one work when you face a big decision: changing profession, moving country, starting your own business, rebuilding your life. We look at your question through the four worlds and external signals, not only from the inside.

## For companies

### Futures scenarios for your industry and product

I collect signals from the outside world, connect them to macrotrends and lay them out as four scenarios. You see which logics your market may follow and find decisions that work in several worlds at once. Deliverables: a report, scenario cards, hypotheses and product directions.

### Trend and signal research

Research into trends, markets and the "user of the future". A base of cases and signals with sources, a trend map and conclusions for your task. One-off or as regular monitoring.

### Scenario game or strategy session for your team

A Decoder game on your topic, or a strategy session: three horizons, alternative futures, backcasting. The team designs the worlds itself and agrees on decisions. Works for 6–60 people, in person and online.

### Future literacy training

Programmes of one to three days, "from guessing to designing": signals and trends, scenarios, systems and decisions, an experiment. Afterwards people notice signals themselves and think in options rather than forecasts.

### A digital twin of your company

A system that connects the company's internal data (projects, clients, money) with external signals. It produces hypotheses, decisions, a 90-day strategy and a monthly snapshot. Useful when you need to check your course regularly against a changing world.

## Talks and lectures

Talks on chaos and uncertainty, images of the future, global trends, the future of business and of people. Topics, formats and recordings are on the [[en/talks|Talks and Lectures]] page.

## How we work

1. **Introduction.** A short conversation about your task: what you want to understand or decide, and by when.
2. **Proposal.** I suggest a format, stages and the result you will get.
3. **Work.** Research, games or sessions. I share interim results along the way.
4. **Outcome.** Scenarios, decisions and first steps. The conclusions and decisions remain yours: I provide content and a framework, not a ready-made answer.

Most of my work for companies is under NDA. Anonymised project examples are on the [[en/about|About the Author]] page.
