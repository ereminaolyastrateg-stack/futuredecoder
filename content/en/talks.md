## Topics for talks

I adapt any topic to the audience and the time available, from 40 minutes to a full day.

### People and their futures

- **The image of the future.** Why our actions follow the picture in our heads, and how to make it conscious.
- **Chaos and uncertainty.** How to act when nothing is clear, and why uncertainty is freedom.
- **The architecture of a person.** How to design your life as a system: eight spheres, four worlds, a first step with limited resources.
- **The automation ladder.** Machines took our muscles, the camera took copying, and now we are automating thinking. Which floor do humans move to next?
- **Longevity.** Why live to 150, and will we have the strength, money and meaning for it? Based on scenario games.
- **Attention.** We have become so good at capturing attention that there is none left. Where it goes and whether we can get it back.

### The world and trends

- **What changed in global trends.** How leading futures institutes redrew their maps by 2026, what they miss, and what it means for us. Based on a comparison of 82 macrotrends.
- **The point in the cycle and the bipolarity of trends.** Where we are now, why every trend has its opposite, and where to look for ways out.
- **Painting as a radar of the future.** Artists are not prophets, but they are honest in their perception. What the avant-garde of 1905–1945 tells us about the 2030s.
- **Attitudes to the human being.** From humanism to transhumanism: who is responsible for a person — themselves, society, the system or no one.
- **People and AI.** What we hand over to the machine and what we keep. Based on scenario games.

### Business and teams

- **Why a business exists.** New business models in a changing economy, with shrinking incomes and growing uncertainty. Which scenarios exist, and which model survives all of them.
- **From guessing to designing.** Future literacy for teams: how to stop guessing the future and start designing it.
- **Trendwatching without the hype.** How a trend differs from a fad, how to work with signals, and why following trends is risky.
- **The future of industries.** Scenarios for your industry: real estate, travel, media, retail, finance, exhibitions and business events.
