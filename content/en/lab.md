## The path: down to a step and back up to the system

<img src="/static/lab/ladder-en.svg" alt="The lab ladder: from four worlds down to your life, one day, contradictions and a first step, then a pause for the experiment and back up to the system" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:16px">

We go down from big pictures of the future to one day and one step, and then climb back up: what this step changes in the whole life. The ladder was invented by futurist Stuart Candy. John Willshire, who teaches it at IED Barcelona, added the key point: you need not only to get down to the thing, but also to climb back up.

## How it works

- Five online meetings of 2 hours, on Sundays at 10:00, from 10 January. Six weeks in total.
- Before the last meeting there is a two-week pause: you try the first step in real life.
- Between meetings you work on your own in the [online lab](/static/opros/en/) — it is free and you can try it in advance.
- Up to 10 people per cohort. We work through everything on a fictional hero; personal maps are never discussed in public.

## Programme

| Stage | What we do | What you get | What it rests on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start** | Talk: eight spheres of life and four logics of the future | A language to describe any life | The Future Decoder scenario method |
| **1. Look** | How things are now and what I want in each sphere, and what for | A map of two pictures: now and desired | Kahneman: when we look at one sphere we overrate its weight — so we look at all eight |
| **2. Expand** | Options from the four worlds, examples from the games, Day B | Wants cleared of other people's, and a desired day lived through | Candy and Willshire: the future should be lived, not read. Burnett and Evans: several options instead of one plan |
| **3. Find** | Contradictions ("I want money but don't want to work"), beliefs, the field of levers | An untangled cloud and several levers | Goldratt: a contradiction rests on a false assumption. Meadows: levers of different levels |
| **4. Assemble** | System: what each lever changes in life, loops, a year map. An experiment for the week | A lever built into life and a first step with a date | Meadows: loops and delays. Michie: capability, opportunity, motivation. Gollwitzer: "when…, I…". Klein: premortem |
| **5. Check** | What worked, what changed in the world, the next circle | An updated map and the next step | Prochaska: change goes in stages, a relapse is part of the path |


## What you get

- Your map: eight spheres, levers, links and a year map by quarters.
- One lever built into your life and an experiment with a date.
- The method: each new circle starts with "how things are now", and you can keep using it.
- A group chat for the cohort.
- Coming soon: a "future self" bot and monthly meetings with new signals.
