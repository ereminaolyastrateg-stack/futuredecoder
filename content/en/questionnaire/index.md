The questionnaire shows two pictures of your life: how it is now and how you want it to be. You choose the spheres: one of them or all eight.

<a class="polotno-hero" href="/static/opros/en/"><span class="polotno-hero-t">Take the questionnaire →</span><span class="polotno-hero-s">8 spheres, 33 subspheres, four ways of living in each</span></a>

## How it works

Each subsphere offers four ways of living from the method's [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/index|life spheres]]. Every option belongs to one of the four Decoder worlds, but the world labels are hidden so they don't steer you. You can answer each option in one of four ways:

- **I want this.** Then note what you take from the option and what it will give you.
- **Not for me.**
- **I have it, keep it.**
- **I have it, drop it.**

You end up with two pictures. "Now" is built from what you already have. "Wanted future" combines what you want with what you keep. If you like, you can also see which worlds pull you.

## What happens to your answers

We collect and store nothing. A draft is kept only in your browser and can be erased with one button. You can download the result as a Markdown note (for Obsidian or AI) or a file for the bot, or print it.
