*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/place|Place of living and public environment]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Place of living and mobility

**A portable life and free moves**

> I can move to another city for a few months while keeping my work and usual services.

**What it gives.** You can change place faster to suit tasks and circumstances.

**What it costs.** Moves take money and energy; harder to keep lasting local ties.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I use several services for housing, transport and mobile work. [source](https://europass.europa.eu/en/europass-employment-and-recruitment)
- Experiment: I build a portable set of services for living between two cities. [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation)

## Infrastructure, rights and safety

**My own backup access to services**

> I keep backup internet and service contacts in case of outages.

**What it gives.** Less vulnerable to a single way of access; easier to act during a failure.

**What it costs.** Backups need checking and money; they don't replace public infrastructure.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I keep my own archive of documents and track my applications. [source](https://europass.europa.eu/en/europass-employment-and-recruitment)
- Experiment: I set up deadline reminders and document exchange in one storage. [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation)

## Nature and environmental quality

**Technical protection of my own conditions**

> At home I monitor air quality myself with a sensor and a purifier.

**What it gives.** You can improve available conditions by your own criteria.

**What it costs.** Technology doesn't remove all sources of pollution; false precision of readings and the cost of consumables.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose devices to save energy and control the climate at home. [source](https://citizens-energy.ec.europa.eu/start-your-energy-journey/households_en)
- Experiment: I set up a home energy system around my own production and consumption. [source](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/markets-and-consumers/energy-consumers-and-prosumers/citizens-energy-package/setting-energy-community_en?prefLang=et)

## Taking part in shared decisions

**Direct participation in individual decisions**

> I vote online on improving my courtyard and check the result.

**What it gives.** You can have targeted influence without constant participation in meetings.

**What it costs.** A narrow agenda and digital exclusion; voting doesn't replace understanding the consequences.

*Spends: Time, Intellect · Gives: Freedom of choice, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I submit projects and vote on a city participation platform. [source](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/digital/en/digital-empowerment/democracy-and-digital-rights/decidim-barcelona)
- Experiment: I use an open platform to discuss and track public initiatives. [source](https://docs.decidim.org/en/develop/understand/about.html)
