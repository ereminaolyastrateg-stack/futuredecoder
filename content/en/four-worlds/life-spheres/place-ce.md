*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/place|Place of living and public environment]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Place of living and mobility

**Becoming a local in the chosen place**

> After moving, I get to know my neighbours through the library and local events.

**What it gives.** Familiar routes and people; easier to build local knowledge and trust.

**What it costs.** Changing place can be painful; local habits don't suit everyone.

*Spends: Time, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: Together with the community I restore a meeting place to keep ties with the neighbourhood. [source](https://www.who.int/activities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities)

## Infrastructure, rights and safety

**A professional who handles my case**

> If there's a dispute over a service, I trust a lawyer to handle my case.

**What it gives.** The solution takes circumstances into account; the consequences of actions are clearer.

**What it costs.** Cost and finding a competent specialist; the result also depends on outside institutions.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Intellect, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

<!-- C2-ЦЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Nature and environmental quality

**Green habits together with those around**

> Together with my neighbours I sort waste and clean up the shared area.

**What it gives.** Easier to join a clear shared practice; less to organise from scratch.

**What it costs.** Without support from those around the habit may fade; an accepted practice isn't always ecologically effective.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

<!-- C3-ЦЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Taking part in shared decisions

**A shared voice through a trusted leader**

> Through a trusted representative I take part in solving my neighbourhood's problems.

**What it gives.** A shared voice can be stronger than separate requests; easier to agree a direction.

**What it costs.** Dependence on the leader; the minority's view can get lost.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I pass agreed wishes on to the district representative. [source](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/digital/en/digital-empowerment/democracy-and-digital-rights/decidim-barcelona)
