*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/place|Place of living and public environment]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Place of living and mobility

**My own base with the option to return**

> There is a home place I return to with my children and pass on family stories to them.

**What it gives.** A place to return to and an accumulated connection with people and history.

**What it costs.** Keeping the connection takes time; the next generations' interests may differ.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

## Infrastructure, rights and safety

**The ability to act and defend interests**

> When there are utility problems, I study the rules and push for a solution together with my neighbours.

**What it gives.** Knowledge and the ability to act grow; joint efforts can strengthen a request.

**What it costs.** Takes time and skills; personal activity doesn't guarantee an institution will change its decision.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Freedom of choice, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I join neighbours to jointly monitor how a city outage is fixed. [source](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/digital/en/digital-empowerment/democracy-and-digital-rights/decidim-barcelona)

## Nature and environmental quality

**Ecology as a condition of the way of life**

> I live closer to nature and look after my plot, even if the trip to the city is longer.

**What it gives.** Ecological requirements are built into the way of life itself.

**What it costs.** Fewer conveniences and possibly long trips; a natural setting doesn't guarantee cleanliness and safety.

*Spends: Time, Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Energy and health, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: Together with my neighbours I create rules for sharing resources and repairs. [source](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/markets-and-consumers/energy-consumers-and-prosumers/citizens-energy-package/setting-energy-community_en)

## Taking part in shared decisions

**Setting the agenda and rules together**

> Together with my neighbours I decide what to spend money on and how to arrange the courtyard.

**What it gives.** Participants influence how the problem is framed and what the solution is.

**What it costs.** High time costs; an active minority may decide more than the rest.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I take part in allocating part of the local budget together with others. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-for-citizen-participation-processes_f765caf6-en.html)
- Experiment: I take part in a citizens' assembly that prepares recommendations for the authorities. [source](https://docs.decidim.org/en/develop/features/general-description.html)
