*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/everyday-life|Everyday life, consumption and leisure]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Housing and home setup

**A flat in the heart of city life**

> I live in the city centre so I can walk to meetings and exhibitions.

**What it gives.** Access to cultural events, architecture and meeting places.

**What it costs.** Price, noise and the limits of old buildings; living in the geographic centre doesn't make it CE.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose to live near people with shared values and traditions. [source](https://urban-types.de/de/cases/wohnen-hoch-drei)

## Household and home services

**A cook and a regular helper**

> We have regular household helpers who know the family's habits.

**What it gives.** A familiar person takes tastes into account and notices needs without detailed instructions.

**What it costs.** Dependence on a particular helper; trust, pay and clear boundaries of work are needed.

*Spends: Time, Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Soul.*

<!-- B2-ЦЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Clothing, personal items and grooming

**Enough good, familiar things**

> I buy well-made things and repair them instead of replacing them often.

**What it gives.** Less attention goes to shopping; predictable quality.

**What it costs.** A habitual choice may stop fitting; the price of quality isn't always justified by durability.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Time, Money, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I buy shoes from a craftsman I know. [source](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/17/7180)
- Today: I wear things from a local maker and take them for repair. [source](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/faq/)
- Experiment: A craftsman makes an item for me and keeps servicing it. [source](https://wornwear.patagonia.com/)

## Leisure, travel and experiences

**Travel with a guide and company**

> I travel with a small club and a guide who knows the place well.

**What it gives.** Meaningful context and company; less need to build the route yourself.

**What it costs.** The shared pace and programme may not suit you; strong dependence on the leader.

*Spends: Time, Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Intellect, Soul.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose a small trip with a guide matched to my interests. [source](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/mexican-maya-communities-strengthen-sustainable-cultural-and-community-based-tourism-unesco)

## Digital services, data and security

**Personal security guidance**

> A specialist helps me protect the family's devices and accounts.

**What it gives.** Individual review and a contact for help; less self-study.

**What it costs.** Cost and trust in the specialist; their mistake or excessive access also creates risk.

*Spends: Time, Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*
