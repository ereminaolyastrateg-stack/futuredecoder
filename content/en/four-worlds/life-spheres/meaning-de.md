*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/meaning|Values, meaning and community]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## My role, values and meaning

**Creating and preserving what is valuable**

> Together with the residents I collect and preserve the stories of my neighbourhood.

**What it gives.** A sense of significance is tied to something concrete you created or preserved.

**What it costs.** You can overload yourself with a mission; the value of a contribution doesn't always get outside confirmation.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I create a product I consider useful beyond my own life. [source](https://urban-types.de/de/cases/wohnen-hoch-drei)
- Experiment: I pass on knowledge through an open archive and co-creation. [source](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/concepts/personal-profile)

## Comfort and attitude to consumption

**Enough in the basics, deep in what matters**

> I don't replace a working phone, but I put money and time into a favourite hobby.

**What it gives.** Fewer unnecessary purchases; more resources for what matters.

**What it costs.** Needs your own criterion of enough; possible conflicts with those around and expensive exceptions.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I create consumption rules that free up time and attention. [source](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/17/7180)

## Relationships with people, friendship and communities

**Relationships where we can understand each other**

> I often meet two close friends for long conversations.

**What it gives.** You can be understood without having to be the same; trust survives change.

**What it costs.** Such ties take time and mutual wish; they can't be secured by one-sided effort.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: Every week I meet a close friend with no agenda. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries_6df2d6a0-en/full-report/executive-summary_7f72ab4a.html)
- Experiment: With friends I create a space for deep conversations and mutual help. [source](https://timebanking.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/WisbechTimeCreditsEvaluation-Workingpaper1-Introductiontotimebankingandtimecredits.pdf)

## Creativity and self-expression

**Finding my own way of expression**

> I write stories in my own style, without aiming for views and likes.

**What it gives.** Your own voice and a deep connection with the material gradually form.

**What it costs.** A long path and an uncertain result; there may be no outside recognition.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I develop a recognisable authorial language and my own theme. [source](https://www.arts.gov/impact/research/publications/arts-participation-2022-technical-summary-report)

## Contribution and public action

**I can start the change myself**

> Together with my neighbours I collect data about a dangerous crossing and push for change.

**What it gives.** The link between participation and concrete benefit is visible; the approach can change with the situation.

**What it costs.** A high personal load and limited scale; the result may depend on one initiator.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I organise a shared initiative with people based on discussion. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-for-citizen-participation-processes_f765caf6-en.html)
- Experiment: I help participants set rules together and evaluate changes. [source](https://swvr2022.unv.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/UNV_SWVR_2022.pdf)
