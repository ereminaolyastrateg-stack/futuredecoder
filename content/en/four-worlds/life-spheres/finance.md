*Finance is one of the eight life spheres through which the Future Decoder method looks at a person. The sphere has 4 subspheres; each looks different in the four worlds. Below are all the options in a filterable table and links to the four cards.*

[[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance-cs|Finance in CS]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance-ce|Finance in CE]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance-ds|Finance in DS]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance-de|Finance in DE]]

## Subsphere questions

**Income and inflows.** Where from and for what do I want to receive money? How far should income depend on time, results, capital or personal mastery? Which conditions suit me? Which inflows can I receive without working — help from others, benefits or money from selling property? Which are regular and which one-off?

*Boundary: Sources of inflow, including regular and one-off inflows without earnings. Selling property turns an asset into money; the whole proceeds are not profit. Career role and growth are W1; building assets is F3.*

**Spending and obligations.** How do I want to allocate current money and pay obligations? Which spending decisions do I keep for myself? How do I keep payments within my means?

*Boundary: Managing cash flow. The motive for consumption is V5, choosing goods is B3, protection from shocks is F4.*

**Capital and savings.** What do I want to accumulate and why? Which assets and abilities do I consider my capital? From what means and for whom am I building it?

*Boundary: Composition and creation of capital. Income from it is F1; reserves and protection horizon are F4. Education is not a liquid asset.*

**Financial resilience.** What will I rely on if I lose income or face a big expense? For how long do I want to be prepared? Which obligations and dependencies am I ready to accept?

*Boundary: The way to withstand shocks and the planning horizon. Not a list of savings, which is F3.*
