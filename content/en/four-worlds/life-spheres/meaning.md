## Subsphere questions

**My role, values and meaning.** What do I live for, and what role do I give myself in the world? What do I want to receive, continue, discover, create or preserve? What will make me consider my life meaningful? What place do worldview, faith and spiritual practices have in my life?

*Boundary: Role and meaning outwards, including worldview, faith and choosing between values. The criterion of enough comfort is V5; concrete public action is V4.*

**Comfort and attitude to consumption.** What does consumption give me: convenience, status, emotions or support for what matters? Where is enough for me? For what am I ready to spend more, or to skip a purchase?

*Boundary: The general motive and the line of sufficiency. Concrete purchases are B3, leisure B4, budgeting F2.*

**Relationships with people, friendship and communities.** What place do other people have in my life? Do I need company, belonging, shared interests, deep understanding or a mix? What am I ready to give to these relationships myself?

*Boundary: The social environment: friends, acquaintances, communities and different circles. K1 is the closest circle. The number of people isn't tied to a scenario; combinations are possible.*

**Creativity and self-expression.** What do I want to express or create — even if it brings no money and nobody needs it? How do I want to bring my idea to life? Do I want to share what I've made, and on what terms?

*Boundary: Self-expression and realising a personal idea. A finished product and publication are optional, and you may not give this a place in your life at all.*

**Contribution and public action.** Beyond my personal life, what do I want to support or change — and what am I ready to do for it? Do I believe my contribution can change something? What exactly?

*Boundary: Change for others through support. V3 is connections and belonging; C4 is rules, priorities and distributing resources.*
