*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/place|Place of living and public environment]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]] world: order through system, standard and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Place of living and mobility

**Everything nearby and travel without hassle**

> I live near the metro, shops and a doctor, so I rarely need a car.

**What it gives.** Less time on the road and organising trips; owning a vehicle is optional.

**What it costs.** Convenience depends on density of services, timetables and fares; not every need will be close by.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I use public transport and city routes. [source](https://www.who.int/activities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities)

## Infrastructure, rights and safety

**Handled requests and repairs**

> When something breaks at home, I call a service that organises the repair itself.

**What it gives.** Less bureaucratic and organisational load.

**What it costs.** A fee and dependence on the intermediary; they can't always influence the service's own timing.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I submit an application at an office window and get a ticket number. [source](https://www.who.int/activities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities)
- Experiment: A service automatically routes a request between agencies for a life event. [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation)

## Nature and environmental quality

**Urban comfort with serviced protection**

> I live in a building where the management company services ventilation and water filtration.

**What it gives.** You can get comfort without rebuilding your whole urban lifestyle.

**What it costs.** Protection covers a limited space; nice design can hide unsolved environmental problems.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I live in a district with waste sorting and public transport. [source](https://www.who.int/activities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities)

## Taking part in shared decisions

**Electing representatives and monitoring delivery**

> The building council decides on repairs, and I read its reports.

**What it gives.** No need to discuss every decision all the time; there is a defined person in charge.

**What it costs.** Representatives can drift away from participants' interests; monitoring also takes attention.

*Spends: Time, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I vote for a representative in municipal elections. [source](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/digital/en/digital-empowerment/democracy-and-digital-rights/decidim-barcelona)
- Today: I follow the decisions and reports of elected bodies. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-for-citizen-participation-processes_f765caf6-en.html)
