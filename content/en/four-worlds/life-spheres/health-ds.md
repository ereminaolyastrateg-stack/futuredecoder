*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/health|Health]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Body and physical abilities

**Personal tech support**

> I choose my own watch or sensors for training and decide what to track.

**What it gives.** Support can be fitted to a personal goal and changed piece by piece; the chosen metrics are visible.

**What it costs.** Measurement and interpretation errors; costs of compatibility and maintenance; numbers can crowd out sensations.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I compare data from my watch and app and adjust the load myself. [source](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/assistive-technology)
- Experiment: I combine data from several wearables into my own training plan. [source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41305286/)

## Habits, sleep and recovery

**A configured sleep and rest mode**

> At home, lights and notifications automatically switch to a calm evening mode.

**What it gives.** Fewer repeated decisions; conditions can be set to my own schedule.

**What it costs.** Settings need maintenance; not every device metric reflects the quality of recovery.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose my own tracker, alarm and rest schedule. [source](https://mobile.va.gov/app/insomnia-coach)

<!-- H2-ДС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Mental health

**Support at hand**

> I keep a journal and the contacts of people I can turn to for support on my phone.

**What it gives.** Support is collected in a convenient form; individual tools can be swapped.

**What it costs.** Needs setting up and judging quality; an app doesn't provide professional help on its own.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose an app with exercises and decide myself when to open it. [source](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927)
- Experiment: I combine exercises, audio practices and mood notes to suit my needs. [source](https://www.who.int/news/item/17-06-2026-who-step-by-step-programme-recognized-as-a-promising-innovation-for-mental-health-in-humanitarian-settings)

## Cognitive health

**My own external memory**

> I keep notes, decisions and my calendar in my own system where everything is easy to find.

**What it gives.** Needed information is easier to recover; the person sets the rules for access and reminders.

**What it costs.** The archive needs care; search errors or data loss create a new burden.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Intellect, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I build a local assistant to search my own notes. [source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42350222/)

<!-- H4-ДС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Prevention, treatment, rehabilitation and care

**My own medical history and choice of care**

> I keep my test results and reports and decide myself whom to show them to.

**What it gives.** More freedom in choosing care; less history lost between institutions.

**What it costs.** Coordination stays with the person; information may be incomplete and prescriptions uncoordinated.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I get my results myself and manage my records in an app. [source](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/setting-up/get-full-access/)
- Experiment: I gather my treatment history from several institutions into one record I can access. [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation)
