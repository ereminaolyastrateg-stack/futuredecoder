*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/family|Close relationships and family]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]] world: order through system, standard and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Closeness, agreements and life together

**Closeness compatible with usual life**

> My partner and I live separately and agree on meetings and shared expenses in advance.

**What it gives.** Clear expectations and less organisational friction.

**What it costs.** Predictability can make it harder to discuss changed needs; services don't create mutuality.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I see my relatives on family holidays. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries_6df2d6a0-en/full-report/executive-summary_7f72ab4a.html)

## Raising and developing children

**Conditions for the child while keeping my own life**

> Kindergarten, after-school care and a nanny help me combine children, work and personal life.

**What it gives.** Easier to combine parenthood with work and other parts of life.

**What it costs.** High cost and dependence on available help; delegating doesn't remove parental responsibilities.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I use clubs, after-school care and available family services. [source](https://www.unicef.org/support-parenting)
- Experiment: I get help from several services through one family centre. [source](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/support-and-services/services-and-support-for-parents/)

## Care and help for loved ones

**Help through a ready service**

> A home help service visits an elderly relative on a schedule.

**What it gives.** Help depends less on one relative being constantly present.

**What it costs.** Cost and dependence on the service; unfamiliar workers may keep changing.

*Spends: Time, Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Soul.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I order regular visits from a home helper. [source](https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/WHO-EURO-2025-12081-51853-79478)
- Experiment: A service coordinates several kinds of home help in one care plan. [source](https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/WHO-EURO-2025-12081-51853-79478)
