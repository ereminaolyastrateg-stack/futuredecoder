*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/work|Work]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Direction, role and professional growth

**A master of my craft and product**

> For years I've been developing my own product and working under my own name.

**What it gives.** Building deep mastery and a personal signature.

**What it costs.** A slow path to recognition; a high bar of quality can limit speed and volume.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

## Getting tasks done

**Deep work on quality**

> I work several days in a row without meetings to do a complex task well.

**What it gives.** The chance to deepen mastery and solve non-standard problems.

**What it costs.** High time costs; hard to promise an exact deadline for exploratory work.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

<!-- W2-ДЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Time, place and working conditions

**Place and time for deep focus**

> I agree on meeting-free days and work in a quiet place.

**What it gives.** Conditions support attentive work and an individual rhythm.

**What it costs.** Needs space, discipline and agreements; not every profession allows this mode.

*Spends: Time, Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

<!-- W3-ДЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Collaboration and management

**A self-managing team for the task**

> In our small team we distribute roles ourselves and revisit decisions.

**What it gives.** Participants influence the rules and can use different strengths.

**What it costs.** Discussions take time; without clear authority, decisions can stall.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: The team itself discusses what to improve after a project. [source](https://www.ilo.org/publications/platform-labour-search-value-study-workers%E2%80%99-organizing-practices-and)
