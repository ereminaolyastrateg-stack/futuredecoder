*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/family|Close relationships and family]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Closeness, agreements and life together

**Family as a solid shared support**

> We gather as a whole family for holidays and support each other in hard times.

**What it gives.** Belonging and stable mutual obligations.

**What it costs.** Pressure of expectations; personal wishes can get lost behind habitual roles.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I keep up regular family rituals and make decisions around my loved ones. [source](https://www.caretrialog.de/natuerlich-gemeinsam-mehr-miteinander-als-nebeneinander-leben)

## Raising and developing children

**Life built around a family with children**

> I fit my work around the children and organise family days together with relatives.

**What it gives.** There is an image of the desired family and support from relatives' experience.

**What it costs.** Traditional expectations may differ from the child's needs or the adults' possibilities.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: Older relatives take part in raising the children. [source](https://www.unicef.org/support-parenting)
- Today: I build my work schedule around important stages in my children's lives. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/paid-leave-for-fathers_07442bed-en.html)

## Care and help for loved ones

**Family care and regular helpers**

> Relatives agree on days to help an elderly family member, and there is a regular helper.

**What it gives.** Familiar people know the habits better and notice changes.

**What it costs.** Relatives get overloaded and duties are shared unequally; the limits of help are hard to discuss.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I invite a regular helper whom my relative trusts. [source](https://www.nhs.uk/social-care-and-support/money-work-and-benefits/personal-budgets/)
- Experiment: I choose a small circle of rotating helpers for stable care. [source](https://www.who.int/europe/news/item/06-11-2025-telemedicine-shows-promise-in-improving-dementia-care--who-study-finds)
