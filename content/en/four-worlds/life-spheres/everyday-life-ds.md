*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/everyday-life|Everyday life, consumption and leisure]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Housing and home setup

**Rented housing to suit the situation**

> I rent where it's convenient for me now and move easily.

**What it gives.** Housing is easier to change with circumstances; fewer lasting investments in setup.

**What it costs.** Uncertainty about renewals and rules; repeated moves and limited influence over the space.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I use a modular layout and local automation of my home space. [source](https://urban-types.de/de/cases/wohnen-hoch-drei)

## Household and home services

**Machines do the routine**

> A robot vacuum and other appliances do part of the housework on a schedule.

**What it gives.** Less repetitive manual work; the mode can be set to suit you.

**What it costs.** Cost of equipment, space, repairs and maintenance; machines don't do all the housework.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose appliances and apps to automate household tasks. [source](https://citizens-energy.ec.europa.eu/start-your-energy-journey/households_en)
- Experiment: I link lighting, heating and sensors into a local home system. [source](https://www.home-assistant.io/)

## Clothing, personal items and grooming

**Picked to fit my exact requirements**

> A service picks purchases by my rules and budget when they're really needed.

**What it gives.** Less searching with an exact match to the request.

**What it costs.** You need to formulate requirements; the assistant may promote what's good for the seller, not for you.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I compare reviews, prices and specs in several shops. [source](https://start.libraryofthings.co.uk/2025-london-impact-report)

## Leisure, travel and experiences

**Experiences made for me, including virtual**

> I build my own travel route with digital assistants and choose alternative experiences.

**What it gives.** You can get the right impressions without necessarily travelling far.

**What it costs.** Preparation and choosing take attention; possible dependence on a platform and loss of access.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice.*

<!-- B4-ДС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Digital services, data and security

**My own data and independence from platforms**

> I keep the family archive myself and make backups without depending on a platform.

**What it gives.** More control over storage and the ability to switch providers.

**What it costs.** Administration and backups fall on the person; storing it yourself isn't the same as protecting it.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I set up a password manager and personal cloud storage. [source](https://docs.decidim.org/en/develop/understand/about.html)
- Experiment: I build a portable data archive with locally managed access. [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation)
