## Subsphere questions

**Housing and home setup.** What kind of home do I want: a flat or a house, its size, surroundings, equipment? Do I need ownership, or is access enough? Which cares of setting up a home am I ready to take on?

*Boundary: The desired home and space. Geographic ties and moves are C1; household routine is B2.*

**Household and home services.** Which household tasks do I want to do, and which not? Who cooks, cleans and looks after things? How do we share the load, and how much will I pay for freed-up time? How do I want to supply the home with food and household items: buy, grow, make or share?

*Boundary: Housework and maintenance, including supplying, making, storing, repairing and sharing household items. Food as health is H2; care when someone loses independence is K3.*

**Clothing, personal items and grooming.** Which things and care do I need? What matters more: novelty, quality, exact fit or sufficiency? Where am I ready to dig into details, and what share of the budget will I allocate?

*Boundary: Concrete consumption of clothing, personal items and non-medical care. The general motive for consumption is V5.*

**Leisure, travel and experiences.** What do I want from free time: a change of events, cultural experience, personal impressions or depth and quiet? Do I need to travel for that?

*Boundary: Concrete leisure. Why I consume experiences at all is V5; recovery as a routine is H2.*

**Digital services, data and security.** How do I want to use communications, accounts and digital services? What am I ready to share for convenience? Who stores the data and is responsible for settings, recovering access and protection? What should happen to my accounts and files after my death?

*Boundary: Personal digital life, including access devices, data transfer, ending subscriptions and digital legacy. Network availability as infrastructure is C2. Security, privacy and control are different outcomes.*
