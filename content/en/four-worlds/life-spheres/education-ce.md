*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education|Education]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Purpose, route and access to knowledge

**Systematic knowledge through a chosen programme**

> I study a long programme with a teacher and learn the subject systematically.

**What it gives.** Consistency and reliance on the experience of teachers and authors.

**What it costs.** Someone else's programme may lag behind your needs; considerable time and cost.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I take a long programme with a teacher and discussion of the material. [source](https://news.mit.edu/2025/bringing-manufacturing-back-america-fab-labs-0413)

## Practice and applying knowledge

**Experience under a master's guidance**

> I learn a craft from a master and make a real object with their hints.

**What it gives.** Execution mistakes are noticed quickly; practical experience builds up.

**What it costs.** Quality depends on the master and on available tasks; possible dependence on one way of doing things.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I practise a skill with regular feedback from a mentor. [source](https://news.mit.edu/2025/bringing-manufacturing-back-america-fab-labs-0413)

<!-- E2-ЦЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Feedback and recognition

**Assessment by an experienced teacher**

> A teacher goes through my work and shows what to improve.

**What it gives.** Strengths and gaps are visible in detail; professional trust builds up.

**What it costs.** Subjectivity and dependence on the chosen tradition of assessment.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: A professional community assesses a specific project, not just a diploma. [source](https://europass.europa.eu/en/european-digital-credentials-learning)
