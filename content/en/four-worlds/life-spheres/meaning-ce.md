*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/meaning|Values, meaning and community]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## My role, values and meaning

**Being part of something bigger**

> I take part in a big public project with a shared goal and rules.

**What it gives.** A sense of continuity and of personal effort linked to a larger result.

**What it costs.** If the shared cause is in crisis, personal footing can shake; one's own and accepted values may conflict.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose to take part in a big mission with a strong team. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries_6df2d6a0-en/full-report/executive-summary_7f72ab4a.html)

## Comfort and attitude to consumption

**Confirming status**

> I belong to a prestigious professional club and feel I belong to my circle.

**What it gives.** Choices help express belonging and the desired position.

**What it costs.** Dependence on outside judgement; overpaying for a symbol and having to maintain status.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I invest in services and things that my circle recognises. [source](https://europass.europa.eu/en/european-digital-credentials-learning)

## Relationships with people, friendship and communities

**Belonging and being needed in a community**

> I help run local community meetings and feel needed.

**What it gives.** A sense of belonging and being needed; a familiar circle of mutual support.

**What it costs.** The community's expectations can become excessive; hard to step out of a habitual role.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I support the regular meetings of a stable community. [source](https://www.who.int/activities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities)

## Creativity and self-expression

**Expressing myself through mastery and tradition**

> For several years I've been studying singing with a teacher and performing at small concerts.

**What it gives.** You can reach expressiveness by relying on a developed tradition and a master.

**What it costs.** Dependence on the teacher's aesthetics; a personal idea may not fit the chosen approach.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I learn drawing in an artist's studio. [source](https://www.arts.gov/news/press-releases/2023/new-data-reveal-how-adults-participated-arts-during-covid-19)

## Contribution and public action

**Together with others my contribution gains strength**

> I help a civic organisation and do my part of a shared project.

**What it gives.** Scale and division of labour increase what a personal contribution can do.

**What it costs.** Dependence on the organisation's goals and quality; its interests may diverge from those of the people it helps.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I take part in an association I trust to represent our interests. [source](https://knowledge.unv.org/evidence-library/2022-state-of-the-worlds-volunteerism-report-building-equal-and-inclusive-societies)
