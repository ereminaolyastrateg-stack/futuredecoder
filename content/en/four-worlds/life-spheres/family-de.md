*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/family|Close relationships and family]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Closeness, agreements and life together

**Our own culture of closeness and shared life**

> My loved ones and I invent our own family traditions and rules of communication.

**What it gives.** The format can reflect the participants and change with them.

**What it costs.** Takes conversations and emotional work; a common language may fail in a conflict.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: Together we agree on boundaries, responsibilities and time for each other. [source](https://www.caretrialog.de/natuerlich-gemeinsam-mehr-miteinander-als-nebeneinander-leben)

## Raising and developing children

**Life together and the child's independence**

> Together with my child we choose what to explore and learn at home.

**What it gives.** Close contact and the chance to follow the child's changing interests.

**What it costs.** Needs a lot of time and attention; adults find it hard to tolerate an uncertain result.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: We discuss rules with the children and take their opinion into account. [source](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/baby-basics/bonding-with-your-baby/)

## Care and help for loved ones

**A circle of mutual support**

> My neighbours and I take turns helping someone who finds it hard to cope alone.

**What it gives.** Care takes into account the person's wishes and the possibilities of several participants.

**What it costs.** Help isn't always available on time; complex needs can exceed what the circle can do.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: We agree with friends and family on mutual help. [source](https://www.caretrialog.de/natuerlich-gemeinsam-mehr-miteinander-als-nebeneinander-leben)
- Experiment: We set up a local network exchanging hours of help for people who need it. [source](https://timebanking.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/WisbechTimeCreditsEvaluation-Workingpaper1-Introductiontotimebankingandtimecredits.pdf)
