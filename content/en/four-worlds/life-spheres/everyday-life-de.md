*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/everyday-life|Everyday life, consumption and leisure]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Housing and home setup

**A country house and life my own way**

> I live in a country house with a garden and a workshop and arrange my household myself.

**What it gives.** More space and more ways to arrange everyday life to suit you.

**What it costs.** Cares about the house and grounds, costs and long trips; possible isolation.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: Together with my family I design a space for different rhythms and shared activities. [source](https://www.cohousing-berlin.de/projekte/gutleben-buettnershof-ein-werkdorf-der-altmark)

## Household and home services

**Favourite tasks and shared care for the home**

> I cook Sunday dinner with pleasure, and we share the rest of the housework.

**What it gives.** The joy of household tasks is kept; the load can be shared meaningfully.

**What it costs.** Agreements need revisiting; one person's favourite task can create work for another.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Energy and health, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I keep some household tasks as time for being together. [source](https://start.libraryofthings.co.uk/2025-london-impact-report)

## Clothing, personal items and grooming

**Basic sufficiency and things I love**

> I wear a few simple things but choose the ones I love with special care.

**What it gives.** Money and attention are focused on what really matters.

**What it costs.** Careful choosing takes time; a favourite category can also become an expensive collection.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I buy second-hand and repair things. [source](https://start.libraryofthings.co.uk/2025-london-impact-report)
- Experiment: I take part in local exchange and repair of things with neighbours. [source](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/faq/)

## Leisure, travel and experiences

**My own pace and deep experience**

> I spend my holiday in one city, walking unhurriedly and getting to know the place deeply.

**What it gives.** More time for experiencing, attention and rest at your own pace.

**What it costs.** Less variety in the same time; quiet and long free time can be expensive.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Energy and health, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I choose a trip that involves local life and learning a craft. [source](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/mayan-and-mexican-community-based-tourism-model-displays-cultural-power-sustainable-development?hub=800)

## Digital services, data and security

**A smaller digital footprint**

> I don't post my personal life online and agree on privacy with my loved ones.

**What it gives.** Less unnecessary information ends up with strangers.

**What it costs.** Some conveniences and public opportunities are unavailable; you can't fully control your footprint alone.

*Spends: Time, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I remove unnecessary apps and limit notifications. [source](https://europass.europa.eu/en/europass-employment-and-recruitment)
- Experiment: I choose services with encryption and data minimisation. [source](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9932632052378-How-to-protect-yourself-on-Signal)
