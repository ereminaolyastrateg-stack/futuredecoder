*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/everyday-life|Everyday life, consumption and leisure]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]] world: order through system, standard and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Housing and home setup

**A modern flat in a convenient area**

> I live in a convenient modern flat with services and delivery nearby.

**What it gives.** Ready conveniences and fewer worries about equipment; services are close by.

**What it costs.** Standard solutions and the complex's rules; regular fees and dependence on management.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I live in a complex with ready infrastructure and services. [source](https://www.who.int/activities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities/creating-age-friendly-cities-and-communities)

## Household and home services

**Household as a ready service**

> I order food delivery, cleaning and laundry so I don't have to do them myself.

**What it gives.** A significant part of the routine is handed over; you get a ready result.

**What it costs.** Constant costs, providers' schedules and fluctuating quality.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

<!-- B2-ЦС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Clothing, personal items and grooming

**The joy of shopping without long choosing**

> I buy pre-selected outfits and don't spend time searching.

**What it gives.** Quick choice and the pleasure of novelty; fewer comparisons.

**What it costs.** Unnecessary purchases, dependence on recommendations and rising repeat costs.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Soul.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose items through an online shop's recommendations and delivery. [source](https://start.libraryofthings.co.uk/2025-london-impact-report)

<!-- B3-ЦС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Leisure, travel and experiences

**Frequent change of ready-made experiences**

> I buy ready-made tours and often choose new short trips.

**What it gives.** Lots of new events with little preparation.

**What it costs.** Costs and fatigue from intensity; less time to get to know a place deeply.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I book a package trip with transport and hotel. [source](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20250402-1)

<!-- B4-ЦС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Digital services, data and security

**The convenience of familiar platforms**

> I keep photos, documents and chats in one cloud account.

**What it gives.** Fewer settings; convenient access and ready ways to recover it.

**What it costs.** Dependence on the platform and its rules; handing over data doesn't guarantee security.

*Spends: Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: A platform links several services into one interface and account. [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation)
