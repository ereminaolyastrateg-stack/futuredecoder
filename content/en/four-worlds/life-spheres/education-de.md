*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education|Education]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Purpose, route and access to knowledge

**Curiosity and my own research**

> I explore a topic that interests me through books and archives, without exams or a diploma.

**What it gives.** Depth and freedom to explore without immediate gain.

**What it costs.** It's unclear when you've studied enough; gaps and dead ends are possible.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: Instead of a fixed programme, I gather a circle of people for joint research. [source](https://news.mit.edu/2025/bringing-manufacturing-back-america-fab-labs-0413)

## Practice and applying knowledge

**My own project with an unknown solution**

> I try to build something I don't yet know how to make and learn from my mistakes.

**What it gives.** Develops the ability to act without ready instructions.

**What it costs.** Lots of trial and error; hard to estimate the time and completeness of learning in advance.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I learn skills in my own project with a living result. [source](https://news.mit.edu/2025/bringing-manufacturing-back-america-fab-labs-0413)

## Feedback and recognition

**Recognition of an independent contribution**

> I publish my own research and improve it after critical reviews.

**What it gives.** Feedback concerns the substantive value of your own contribution.

**What it costs.** Recognition may come late or not at all; a competent audience is hard to find.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I prove my qualification with published work and a project result. [source](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/concepts/personal-profile)
