*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance|Finance]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Income and inflows

**Income from assets I own**

> I get income from my own flat or another asset, taking the costs into account.

**What it gives.** Part of the inflow doesn't require selling personal time for each payment.

**What it costs.** Requires capital; vacancies, losses, management costs and falling payouts are possible.

*Spends: Time, Money, Capital · Gives: Money, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I combine income from rent, a digital product and an asset portfolio. [source](https://www.ilo.org/publications/summary-role-digital-labour-platforms-transforming-world-work)

## Spending and obligations

**My own limits and regular payments**

> I set spending and payment limits in an app and review them.

**What it gives.** Routine payments follow my own rules; deviations are visible.

**What it costs.** Settings and balances need checking; an error in one rule can affect several payments.

*Spends: Time, Intellect · Gives: Time, Money, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I record spending in a notebook by my own categories. [source](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/)
- Today: I pick an app and create my own limits by category. [source](https://www.fidelity.com/managed-accounts/fidelity-go/overview/)
- Experiment: I combine several accounts into a personal system for tracking regular payments. [source](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/articles/how-america-saves-2025-key-trends-insights.html)

## Capital and savings

**Automatic topping up of assets**

> Every month I automatically top up my savings or investments.

**What it gives.** Regularity depends less on monthly effort; the choice of assets stays with you.

**What it costs.** Auto-saving doesn't remove market risk; a wrong initial choice is repeated too.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Capital, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I split my savings between deposits and diversified funds myself. [source](https://advisors.vanguard.com/investments/products/vttvx/vanguard-target-retirement-2025-fund)

## Financial resilience

**A medium-term reserve by my own rules**

> I keep a reserve for several months of living on an accessible account.

**What it gives.** More time to rebuild after losing income; your own limits of protection are visible.

**What it costs.** A reserve takes resources and discipline; the estimate may underrate how long hard times last.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Capital, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I build a cash reserve and insurance policies myself. [source](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/save-and-invest/save-rainy-day)
- Experiment: I test scenarios of losing my main income in my own cash-flow spreadsheet. [source](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/articles/how-america-saves-2025-key-trends-insights.html)
