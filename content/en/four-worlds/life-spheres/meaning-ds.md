*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/meaning|Values, meaning and community]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## My role, values and meaning

**Expanding my own possibilities**

> I try new tools to do what I couldn't do before.

**What it gives.** Many paths for trying things and expanding personal experience.

**What it costs.** Constantly searching for the next opportunity can get in the way of depth and finishing things.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Intellect, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I master new possibilities through open technologies and practice. [source](https://news.mit.edu/2025/bringing-manufacturing-back-america-fab-labs-0413)

## Comfort and attitude to consumption

**Emotions and new experiences**

> Instead of new things I buy interesting trips and experiences.

**What it gives.** Money brings experiences without having to accumulate things.

**What it costs.** Novelty quickly becomes ordinary; spending on access can be hard to notice.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose trips and hobbies to my own taste. [source](https://start.libraryofthings.co.uk/2025-london-impact-report)

## Relationships with people, friendship and communities

**Different people for different interests**

> I have different communities for books, travel and other interests.

**What it gives.** Different interests find their company; the intensity of contact can vary.

**What it costs.** Fragmented attention; few people see the whole person or notice their absence.

*Spends: Time, Intellect · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I connect circles of friends with different interests myself. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries_6df2d6a0-en/full-report/social-connections-across-oecd-countries_4e46a241.html)

## Creativity and self-expression

**Realising my ideas with technology**

> I invent a story and use software to make pictures and music for it.

**What it gives.** You can quickly realise ideas beyond the technique you've mastered.

**What it costs.** Much time goes into selecting; limits of platforms, rights and control over the result.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I combine different creative tools to experiment with a new format. [source](https://news.mit.edu/2025/bringing-manufacturing-back-america-fab-labs-0413)

## Contribution and public action

**My everyday choice also changes the world**

> I choose a help project myself and switch if I don't see results.

**What it gives.** A contribution can be made regular and its direction changed independently.

**What it costs.** Convenient regularity can keep ineffective support going; reliable information about results is needed.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Freedom of choice, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I use digital tools to organise my own campaign. [source](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/digital/en/digital-empowerment/democracy-and-digital-rights/decidim-barcelona)
- Experiment: I collect data on a problem and build an open tool to solve it. [source](https://docs.decidim.org/en/develop/understand/about.html)
