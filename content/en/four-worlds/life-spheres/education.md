*Education is one of the eight life spheres through which the Future Decoder method looks at a person. The sphere has 3 subspheres; each looks different in the four worlds. Below are all the options in a filterable table and links to the four cards.*

[[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education-cs|Education in CS]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education-ce|Education in CE]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education-ds|Education in DS]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education-de|Education in DE]]

## Subsphere questions

**Purpose, route and access to knowledge.** Why do I need knowledge? What do I want to understand myself, and where is an answer enough? Who chooses topics, sequence and sources?

*Boundary: Choice of content and path. Practice is E2; diplomas and recognition are E3.*

**Practice and applying knowledge.** How will I turn familiarity with a topic into the ability to act? On which tasks will I try? What should work without the help I had before?

*Boundary: Changing skill through practice. Producing a work product is W2; assessment and documents are E3.*

**Feedback and recognition.** How will I know what I've learned and what I can't do yet? Whose assessment matters? Why do I need confirmation: licensing, professional trust or recognition of my contribution?

*Boundary: Assessment of training and evidence. Learning content is E1; career status is W1.*
