*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/health|Health]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Body and physical abilities

**Feeling the body and mastering movement**

> I go to dance or movement classes to feel my body better.

**What it gives.** A finer sense of movement and independence in choosing a manageable load.

**What it costs.** Takes time and regularity; subjective sensation doesn't replace assessment of an injury or illness.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Soul, Energy and health, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I train my balance with yoga and physical exercise. [source](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults)

## Habits, sleep and recovery

**My own rhythm and sense of measure**

> I watch when I get tired and adjust sleep and rest to how I feel.

**What it gives.** My own needs become clearer; the routine is easier to adjust when life changes.

**What it costs.** Observation takes time and can be inaccurate; persistent problems need separate attention.

*Spends: Time, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I go to bed guided by tiredness rather than a norm. [source](https://veteranshealthlibrary.va.gov/LivingWith/Insomnia/Treatment/142%2C41434_VA)
- Today: I avoid overload and leave time in my day for recovery. [source](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/stress)

## Mental health

**Understanding myself and changing reactions**

> I write down what I feel and learn to understand why I react the way I do.

**What it gives.** More understanding of my own feelings and more say in choosing changes.

**What it costs.** You can get stuck in self-analysis or wrong explanations; it takes time and willingness to ask for help.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I work through feelings by talking and writing. [source](https://www.who.int/publications/b/82448)
- Today: I practise skills for handling difficult thoughts from a WHO guide. [source](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927)

## Cognitive health

**Keeping clarity and focus**

> Every day I read or think for an hour without my phone and notifications.

**What it gives.** Attention stays one's own support; fatigue is easier to notice.

**What it costs.** Results are hard to measure quickly; habits depend on life circumstances and don't explain every difficulty.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I read complex texts and compare the arguments on my own. [source](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults)

## Prevention, treatment, rehabilitation and care

**Shared decisions about health**

> Before treatment I discuss different options with my doctor and how they would affect my life.

**What it gives.** Decisions are better tied to life priorities; the person understands the choice and its limits.

**What it costs.** Discussion takes time and energy; information can be complex and contradictory.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose treatment together with my doctor after comparing benefits and risks. [source](https://www.england.nhs.uk/personalisedcare/shared-decision-making/)
