*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance|Finance]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]] world: order through system, standard and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Income and inflows

**A fixed salary**

> I get a stable salary every month and don't look for clients.

**What it gives.** Easier to plan inflows; no need to chase each payment.

**What it costs.** Dependence on one employer; extra effort has limited link to income.

*Spends: Time, Freedom of choice · Gives: Money, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I get a fixed salary for a permanent job. [source](https://www.ilo.org/publications/summary-role-digital-labour-platforms-transforming-world-work)
- Experiment: My employer offers flexible forms of pay within a shared system. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)

## Spending and obligations

**Bills paid in a set order**

> My phone and utility bills are paid automatically.

**What it gives.** Fewer forgotten bills and manual repetitions.

**What it costs.** Mistakes can repeat automatically; it's easy to stop noticing regular costs growing.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I set money aside for mandatory payments in envelopes. [source](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/)
- Today: I set up automatic payments for utilities and phone. [source](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/articles/how-america-saves-2025-key-trends-insights.html)

## Capital and savings

**Saving without giving up current comfort**

> I put small sums aside and pay for big purchases in instalments.

**What it gives.** You can keep your usual consumption and gradually build a reserve.

**What it costs.** Capital grows more slowly; interest on loans can eat up savings.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Capital, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I put a small sum into the bank every month. [source](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing)
- Today: I take part in a pension scheme with automatic contributions. [source](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/articles/how-america-saves-2025-key-trends-insights.html)
- Experiment: A ready-made investment programme automatically shifts asset shares as the goal date approaches. [source](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/asset-allocation)

## Financial resilience

**The next few months and outside support**

> I know what help or insurance I can count on if I lose my job.

**What it gives.** Less self-organised protection; existing programmes are used.

**What it costs.** A short personal horizon; outside help can be limited or late, and credit creates debt.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Money, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I keep some money for illness or job loss. [source](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/save-and-invest/save-rainy-day)
- Today: I rely on deposit insurance and available social protection. [source](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/articles/how-america-saves-2025-key-trends-insights.html)
