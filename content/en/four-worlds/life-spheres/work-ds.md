*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/work|Work]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Direction, role and professional growth

**An independent project professional**

> I choose my own clients and run several independent projects at once.

**What it gives.** Freedom to choose projects; the value of expertise grows without a mandatory career ladder.

**What it costs.** Uneven workload, the need to sell your work and keep up proof of quality.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Money, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I build a portfolio of project roles instead of a single permanent position. [source](https://www.ilo.org/publications/summary-role-digital-labour-platforms-transforming-world-work)

## Getting tasks done

**My own processes with minimal routine**

> I remove repetitive work routine with templates and configured tools.

**What it gives.** Repetitive work takes less manual effort; you can change the rules yourself.

**What it costs.** An error scales with the process; checking and maintenance don't disappear.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I set up automatic report generation from data. [source](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/02/algorithmic-management-in-the-workplace_3c84ed6d/287c13c4-en.pdf)

## Time, place and working conditions

**Remote work on my own schedule**

> I work from home and agree my availability hours with clients myself.

**What it gives.** Work can be fitted to life, and the place can change.

**What it costs.** Organising conditions falls on the person; the boundaries of working time can blur.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I switch between a home office and a coworking space. [source](https://www.ilo.org/publications/summary-role-digital-labour-platforms-transforming-world-work)
- Experiment: I set up one workflow across several devices and countries. [source](https://autonomy.work/portfolio/uk4dwpilotresults/)

## Collaboration and management

**Independent contractors and clear contracts**

> I assemble a team of independent contractors for a project and agree on the result.

**What it gives.** A flexible line-up for the task and limited contractual obligations.

**What it costs.** Coordinating separate parts can be expensive; shared responsibility can be blurred.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I assemble a project team with shared documents and transparent tasks. [source](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/examples/project-management-activities)
- Experiment: Several specialists work as a temporary network without a permanent hierarchy. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)
