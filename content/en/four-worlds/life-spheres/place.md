## Subsphere questions

**Place of living and mobility.** Where do I want to build everyday life, and how tied to a place do I want to be? How often do I want to and can I move around? What matters more: nearby services, belonging, the option to move, or a base of my own?

*Boundary: Geography, transport and attachment to place. Type and furnishing of the home is B1; environmental conditions are C3.*

**Infrastructure, rights and safety.** How do I want to get basic services and help when things fail? Who protects my interests? What can I really choose, prepare or decide within the existing rules? How will I get water, energy and connectivity — through a provider, my own equipment or together with others?

*Boundary: Access to utility and public infrastructure, rights, physical safety, including own and shared supply and restoring access. Personal accounts and data are B5; the state of the local environment is C3.*

**Nature and environmental quality.** How important are air, water, quiet and living nature to me? Which conditions do I consider enough? What am I ready to change in my life for the environment, and how do I account for my impact? What do I want to preserve and restore, even without direct benefit to myself?

*Boundary: Environmental quality and ecological priority, including natural processes, biodiversity and restoring nature. Not just decorative greenery or gardening.*

**Taking part in shared decisions.** Which shared issues do I want to decide? Do I believe I can have influence, and what powers do I really have? What do I delegate, and where am I ready to take part in choosing rules and priorities?

*Boundary: The right to shape and make shared decisions, including civic duties within real powers. Supporting a public result through action is V4.*
