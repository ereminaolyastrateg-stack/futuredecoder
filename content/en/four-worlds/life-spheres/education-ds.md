*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/education|Education]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Purpose, route and access to knowledge

**The right skill at the right moment**

> I learn a new tool through short lessons right on my own project.

**What it gives.** Specific needs are met quickly; the route is easy to change.

**What it costs.** Fragmented knowledge; constantly chasing skills can crowd out fundamentals.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Intellect, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose courses on different platforms and keep my own learning plan. [source](https://europass.europa.eu/en/europass-employment-and-recruitment)

## Practice and applying knowledge

**My own drills and working templates**

> I create my own exercises to master a skill I need for work.

**What it gives.** Practice is tied to personal tasks; good solutions can be reused.

**What it costs.** You may train only on convenient cases and reinforce your own mistakes.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Intellect, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I create my own practice cycle of tasks and self-checks. [source](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/get-started-openlearn)
- Experiment: I check progress on tasks I choose, without being tied to one course. [source](https://news.mit.edu/2025/bringing-manufacturing-back-america-fab-labs-0413)

## Feedback and recognition

**A portable portfolio and credentials**

> I build a portfolio of real projects and reviews to show my skills.

**What it gives.** You can show concrete achievements to different audiences.

**What it costs.** A portfolio needs updating; not every audience trusts the chosen evidence.

*Spends: Time, Intellect · Gives: Money, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I gather skill credentials from different courses into a digital profile. [source](https://europass.europa.eu/en/european-digital-credentials-learning)
- Experiment: I combine portable digital credentials in a personal wallet. [source](https://www.w3.org/news/2025/the-verifiable-credentials-2-0-family-of-specifications-is-now-a-w3c-recommendation/)
