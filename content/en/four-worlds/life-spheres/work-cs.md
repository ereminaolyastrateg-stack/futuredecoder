*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/work|Work]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]] world: order through system, standard and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Direction, role and professional growth

**A specialist with better conditions**

> I work as a specialist with a good salary and a convenient schedule, without managing people.

**What it gives.** A clear role and a way to improve conditions; you can stay a specialist.

**What it costs.** The ceiling depends on how the organisation is built; higher pay may come with a hidden rise in workload.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Money, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose an employer with transparent pay and conditions for growth. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)

## Getting tasks done

**A clear task and a ready procedure**

> I get clear tasks, do them by the instructions and pass difficult cases to my manager.

**What it gives.** It's clear what to do and when a task is finished; less uncertainty.

**What it costs.** Little influence on the idea; instructions may not cover a difficult case.

*Spends: Intellect, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I do the work by step-by-step instructions. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)
- Today: I receive tasks in a system and mark them as done. [source](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/02/algorithmic-management-in-the-workplace_3c84ed6d/287c13c4-en.pdf)

## Time, place and working conditions

**An office where everything is ready**

> I come to an office where the equipment, internet and workplace are already set up.

**What it gives.** Ready equipment and less organisational hassle.

**What it costs.** Tied to a place and schedule; comforts don't make up for an awkward schedule.

*Spends: Time, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I come to a dedicated office at a set time. [source](https://www.ilo.org/publications/major-publications/working-home-invisibility-decent-work)
- Today: I use a ready workplace and company equipment. [source](https://www.eurofound.europa.eu/en/publications/all/living-and-working-europe-2024)

## Collaboration and management

**Clear roles and measurable rules**

> In my team everyone has a clear role and the rules of work are understood.

**What it gives.** Less uncertainty about duties and accepting results.

**What it costs.** Formal metrics can encourage rivalry and hide mutual help.

*Spends: Time, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: The team is managed by roles, metrics and scheduled meetings. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)
