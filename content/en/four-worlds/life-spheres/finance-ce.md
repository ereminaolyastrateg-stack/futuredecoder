*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance|Finance]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Income and inflows

**Pay for results within an organisation**

> I have a base salary and a bonus for my team's results.

**What it gives.** Contribution and reward are linked; you can earn more inside the organisation.

**What it costs.** Income fluctuates; criteria and assessment can change without the employee's say.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Money, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: My income grows with my manager's trust and the complexity of my assignments. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)

## Spending and obligations

**A budget with a financial adviser**

> Once a month I go through the family budget with a financial adviser.

**What it gives.** Easier to make sense of complex spending and work out a realistic order.

**What it costs.** The cost of advice; possible conflict of interest; the person still has to follow the plan.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Money, Intellect, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: A financial adviser helps me sort out debts and spending. [source](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/)

## Capital and savings

**Housing and education by plan**

> Following a plan with an adviser, I save for housing or education.

**What it gives.** Capital is tied to clear life opportunities; easier to stay consistent.

**What it costs.** Housing can be illiquid, education doesn't guarantee returns; the strategy needs revisiting.

*Spends: Time, Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Intellect, Capital.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: An adviser helps include inheritance questions in the family's financial plan. [source](https://www.fidelity.com/managed-accounts/fidelity-go/overview/)

<!-- F3-ЦЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Financial resilience

**Protection matched to life goals**

> I plan a financial cushion with an adviser to match my obligations.

**What it gives.** Protection is matched to specific deadlines and obligations.

**What it costs.** The plan depends on the quality of its assumptions; a specialist's help doesn't remove responsibility for loans.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Energy and health, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I consult on risks to income, health and property. [source](https://www.fidelity.com/managed-accounts/fidelity-go/overview/)
