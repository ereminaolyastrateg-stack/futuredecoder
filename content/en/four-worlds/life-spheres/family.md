*Close relationships and family is one of the eight life spheres through which the Future Decoder method looks at a person. The sphere has 3 subspheres; each looks different in the four worlds. Below are all the options in a filterable table and links to the four cards.*

## Subsphere questions

**Closeness, agreements and life together.** Whom do I consider closest? Do I need partnership and a shared household? How much shared life, mutual support and independence do I want?

*Boundary: The closest circle and shared life, including without a partner. Wider social life is V3; concrete help in need is K3.*

**Raising and developing children.** Do I want parenthood, and what place should it have in my life? How am I ready to invest time, money and attention? What help do I want to rely on?

*Boundary: The parent's role and the conditions for development. Choosing not to have children, uncertainty and life stage are not scenarios.*

**Care and help for loved ones.** How do I want to help loved ones when they can't cope on their own? What do I take on, and whom do I trust with the rest? How will I get help myself? What help do I want to receive and give after the loss of a loved one, including organising a farewell and supporting those left behind?

*Boundary: Concrete support in need, including help after a loss and organising a farewell. Not ordinary housework (B2), not medical prescriptions (H5), not the definition of closeness (K1).*
