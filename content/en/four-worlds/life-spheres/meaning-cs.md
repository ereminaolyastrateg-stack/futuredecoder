*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/meaning|Values, meaning and community]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic|Centralized — Synthetic]] world: order through system, standard and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## My role, values and meaning

**Enjoying the goods of the world**

> I use conveniences and services without setting myself a public mission.

**What it gives.** You can value everyday life without the duty of having a great mission.

**What it costs.** A sense of a meaningful life can depend heavily on the availability of external goods.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

## Comfort and attitude to consumption

**More accessible comfort**

> I pay for ready household services so I spend less time organising.

**What it gives.** Everyday inconveniences can be steadily reduced.

**What it costs.** The level of "enough" can keep moving away, raising costs and dependence on income.

*Spends: Money · Gives: Time, Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I choose convenient delivery, appliances and familiar services. [source](https://start.libraryofthings.co.uk/2025-london-impact-report)

<!-- V5-ЦС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Relationships with people, friendship and communities

**Pleasant company without complex organising**

> I go to an organised dinner or walk to meet people.

**What it gives.** Easier to find company and start talking.

**What it costs.** Ties may stay superficial; the organiser decides access and format.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I meet people through city clubs and ready-made events. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries_6df2d6a0-en/full-report/executive-summary_7f72ab4a.html)

<!-- V3-ЦС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Creativity and self-expression

**Easily making something beautiful and personal**

> I make a family photo album in a ready-made service and give it to my loved ones.

**What it gives.** A personal result comes quickly without long mastering of technique.

**What it costs.** Expressiveness is limited by the format; works can look alike.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I make an illustration from ready elements in an editor. [source](https://www.arts.gov/news/press-releases/2023/new-data-reveal-how-adults-participated-arts-during-covid-19)

<!-- V2-ЦС · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Contribution and public action

**The main changes don't depend on me**

> I regularly donate to a charity and read its reports.

**What it gives.** You can support a solution without a separate organisational role.

**What it costs.** Little influence on the approach; easy to miss the absence of results.

*Spends: Money, Freedom of choice · Gives: Time, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I rely on municipal services and established rules. [source](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/digital/en/digital-empowerment/democracy-and-digital-rights/decidim-barcelona)
- Today: I use institutional mechanisms for petitions and voting. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-for-citizen-participation-processes_f765caf6-en.html)
