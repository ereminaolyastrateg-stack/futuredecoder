*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/family|Close relationships and family]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic|Decentralized — Synthetic]] world: independence through one’s own tool, method and reproducibility. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Closeness, agreements and life together

**An independent life with chosen ties**

> My partner and I have separate plans and space, and we choose our shared days ourselves.

**What it gives.** More personal space and a flexible format.

**What it costs.** Less spontaneous shared life; support in a hard moment has to be agreed separately.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I build a distributed network of care and regular meetings while living in different countries. [source](https://urban-types.de/de/cases/wohnen-hoch-drei)

## Raising and developing children

**Help I put together myself**

> I put together the schedule of school, clubs and helpers for my child myself.

**What it gives.** Help can be flexibly fitted to the family and its parts changed.

**What it costs.** The parent remains the coordinator; separate helpers may not see the whole picture of the child.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Time, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Experiment: I coordinate the schedules of several helpers and clubs in one calendar. [source](https://www.unicef.org/support-parenting)

## Care and help for loved ones

**I put together the right help myself**

> After a relative's operation I arrange separate services, transport and meal delivery.

**What it gives.** Help is fitted more precisely to the case; parts can be changed.

**What it costs.** A heavy load on the organiser; gaps between providers remain their job.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I coordinate doctors' and helpers' visits in a family calendar. [source](https://www.nhs.uk/social-care-and-support/money-work-and-benefits/personal-budgets/)
