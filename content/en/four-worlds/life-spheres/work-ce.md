*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/work|Work]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Direction, role and professional growth

**A leader of people and large projects**

> I lead a large team and project and am responsible for people and the budget.

**What it gives.** You can influence a large result and people's growth.

**What it costs.** More responsibility for others' work, more conflicts and organisational load.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Money, Intellect, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I lead a team and am responsible for a complex project in the organisation. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)

## Getting tasks done

**A meaningful result with a manager**

> My manager explains the purpose of the project, and I'm responsible for the result of my part.

**What it gives.** The value of the work is visible, and there is support in difficult cases.

**What it costs.** Dependence on the quality of management; approvals can slow work down.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Soul, Self-realisation, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: My manager explains the purpose of the task and helps me fix mistakes. [source](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/examples/open-communication)
- Experiment: At difficult stages I discuss with a mentor not only the result but also the method. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)

## Time, place and working conditions

**A work environment next to colleagues**

> I work next to colleagues and learn from them as I go.

**What it gives.** Quick discussion and a sense of common cause; access to colleagues' experience.

**What it costs.** Noise, interruptions and the need to agree on when to be present.

*Spends: Time, Freedom of choice · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I go to the office for meetings, mentoring and sharing experience. [source](https://www.eurofound.europa.eu/en/surveys-and-data/surveys/living-and-working-in-the-eu-e-survey/e-survey-2024/)

## Collaboration and management

**A team with a respected leader**

> I work with a strong leader whom I trust to choose the direction.

**What it gives.** Disputes are settled quickly, and there is one direction.

**What it costs.** Dependence on one leader; disagreement can be hard to voice.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health, Freedom of choice · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Influence and scale.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: The leader helps the team reconcile conflicting goals. [source](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-widespread-is-algorithmic-management-in-workplaces_cda7a114-en/full-report.html)
