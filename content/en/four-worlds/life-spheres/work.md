## Subsphere questions

**Direction, role and professional growth.** Who do I want to be at work? What do I count as progress: conditions, scope of responsibility, project value, product quality or recognition? How do I want to change, reduce or end my professional role, and do I want other work afterwards?

*Boundary: Role and career criterion, including changing and ending a career. Source of money is F1, daily actions W2, transfer of ownership F3, preparing successors W4. Continuing to work is not required.*

**Getting tasks done.** How do I want to produce a concrete work result? Which decisions am I ready to make myself? What do I do personally, and what do I hand to software, a template or another person?

*Boundary: The way of doing concrete work. Career progress is W1; team authority is W4.*

**Time, place and working conditions.** Where, when and at what pace do I want to work? Which conditions do I need? How important are ready equipment, colleagues nearby, mobility or the chance to go deep?

*Boundary: Space, schedule and conditions. Where to live is C1; how to do the task is W2.*

**Collaboration and management.** How do I want to work with others? Who distributes tasks and decides in disagreements? Who can change the rules of joint work, and how? How do we pass on experience, documentation and unfinished work, and who owns shared results?

*Boundary: Work coordination, including handover of experience and work, rights to joint results, monetary and non-monetary exchange. Public decisions are C4; the need for a social circle is V3.*
