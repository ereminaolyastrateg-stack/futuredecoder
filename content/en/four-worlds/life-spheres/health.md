*Health is one of the eight life spheres through which the Future Decoder method looks at a person. The sphere has 5 subspheres; each looks different in the four worlds. Below are all the options in a filterable table and links to the four cards.*

[[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/health-cs|Health in CS]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/health-ce|Health in CE]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/health-ds|Health in DS]] · [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/health-de|Health in DE]]

## Subsphere questions

**Body and physical abilities.** Which abilities of my body do I want to keep or develop? What do I want to do more easily? What am I ready to rely on: assistive devices, guidance or my own sense of movement?

*Boundary: Body functions and bodily sense. The medical route is H5; routine and recovery are H2.*

**Habits, sleep and recovery.** How do I want to feel after sleep and rest? Which rhythm of eating, activity and recovery suits me? How much daily attention am I ready to give it?

*Boundary: Rhythm and well-being. Delivery, cleaning and cooking as housework are B2.*

**Mental health.** What emotional stability and support do I want? What am I ready to change in how I relate to myself and my feelings? How do I want to get help in hard times?

*Boundary: Mental state and support. The format of close relationships is K1; life role is V1.*

**Cognitive health.** How do I want my attention, memory and clarity of thinking to be? What do I want to offload, and what do I want to keep as my own ability? How will I notice overload?

*Boundary: How memory and attention work. Learning content is E1; proof of training is E3.*

**Prevention, treatment, rehabilitation and care.** How do I want to organise medical care and recovery? Who links my visits into one route? Which decisions do I want to take part in personally?

*Boundary: Choosing and coordinating care, not prescriptions. Practical help for loved ones is K3.*
