*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/health|Health]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth|Centralized — Earth]] world: order through shared meaning, authority and living mastery. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Body and physical abilities

**Strength and endurance with a coach**

> Twice a week I train with a coach who adjusts my load.

**What it gives.** External motivation and timely feedback; a consistent sequence of sessions.

**What it costs.** Cost and dependence on the coach's competence; an awkward schedule can get in the way of regularity.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: A coach builds a strength programme for me and corrects my technique. [source](https://acsm.org/top-fitness-trends-2026/?nocache=1767647640)

## Habits, sleep and recovery

**Habits with personal guidance**

> With a sleep specialist I gradually change my habits and discuss the results.

**What it gives.** Personal review of difficulties and gradual change; support for regularity.

**What it costs.** Cost and meeting time; quality depends on the specialist's skill and how realistic the plan is.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: A sleep consultant helps me keep a diary and change my habits. [source](https://mobile.va.gov/app/cbt-i-coach)

## Mental health

**Working with a psychotherapist**

> I meet a psychotherapist regularly and work through my reactions.

**What it gives.** Stable contact and consistent work with feelings; personal feedback.

**What it costs.** Cost, time and finding the right specialist; change can be slow.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: In a hard period I turn to a psychologist I know. [source](https://www.who.int/publications/b/82448)
- Today: I meet regularly with a psychotherapist I chose. [source](https://www.who.int/news/item/17-06-2026-who-step-by-step-programme-recognized-as-a-promising-innovation-for-mental-health-in-humanitarian-settings)

## Cognitive health

**Maintaining abilities with a specialist**

> When I have trouble with memory or attention, I see a specialist.

**What it gives.** Difficulties are seen in the context of the person's state; there is professional feedback.

**What it costs.** Limited availability and cost; not every plan transfers to everyday life as hoped.

*Spends: Time, Money, Energy and health · Gives: Intellect, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: I train my memory with a teacher or mentor. [source](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults)

<!-- H4-ЦЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Prevention, treatment, rehabilitation and care

**A doctor who knows my history**

> I see one family doctor who knows my history.

**What it gives.** The doctor knows the history and circumstances; easier to link treatment with recovery.

**What it costs.** Dependence on a particular team's availability; a risk of delaying a second opinion.

*Spends: Time, Money · Gives: Soul, Energy and health.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Known practice: For a long time I've been seeing one trusted doctor. [source](https://www.england.nhs.uk/personalisedcare/shared-decision-making/)
