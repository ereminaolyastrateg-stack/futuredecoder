*The [[en/four-worlds/life-spheres/finance|Finance]] sphere in the [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth|Decentralized — Earth]] world: order and knowledge emerging through participation, interaction and revisiting experience. Below is how this logic looks in each subsphere: a life situation, what it gives, what it costs and where it already exists.*

## Income and inflows

**Income from my mastery and product**

> I sell my own course or product that I came up with and made myself.

**What it gives.** Reward is tied to quality and a personal professional signature.

**What it costs.** Income depends on demand, reputation and ability to work; scaling can change quality.

*Spends: Time, Intellect, Energy and health · Gives: Money, Freedom of choice, Self-realisation.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I sell a product I made directly to clients. [source](https://webapps.ilo.org/infostories/en-GB/Campaigns/WESO/World-Employment-Social-Outlook-2021.html)

## Spending and obligations

**Spending by meaningful priorities**

> My family and I decide together what to spend money on and what not to buy.

**What it gives.** Spending is tied to meaningful priorities; the cost of obligations is easier to see.

**What it costs.** Takes attention and conversations; people may judge what's necessary differently.

*Spends: Time, Energy and health · Gives: Money, Soul, Freedom of choice.*

**Where this already exists:**

- Today: I separate spending on what matters to me from purchases out of habit. [source](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/)

## Capital and savings

**Family wealth and passing it on**

> In our family we discuss inheritance and teach the children how to handle money.

**What it gives.** Useful assets and experience can be kept for the long term; continuity grows stronger.

**What it costs.** Possible conflicts between heirs, concentration of wealth and limits on personal choice.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Capital, Influence and scale.*

<!-- F3-ДЗ · Тест · 132 / Справочник v7 -->

## Financial resilience

**Family resilience across generations**

> We agreed in advance who will take care of the bills and documents if I can't.

**What it gives.** A long horizon and several supports; knowledge isn't concentrated in one person.

**What it costs.** Complex agreements and different interests across generations; family support isn't guaranteed.

*Spends: Time, Money, Intellect · Gives: Soul, Capital, Freedom of choice.*
