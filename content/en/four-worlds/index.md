*Every signal, macrotrend and question in this garden is looked at in four worlds. They are not forecasts but four logics by which a society, an organisation or one's own life can be organised. In reality they live side by side, often inside one company or one person.*

The worlds come from two questions. **Where do decisions and rules sit:** in a centre (state, platform, institution, school) or with the participants themselves (decentralized)? **How is the result produced:** through an assembled, reproducible system (synthetic) or through living practice, judgement and relationships (earth)?

| | Synthetic | Earth |
|---|---|---|
| **Centralized** | [[en/four-worlds/centralized-synthetic\|CS]] — order through system and standard | [[en/four-worlds/centralized-earth\|CE]] — order through shared meaning and mastery |
| **Decentralized** | [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-synthetic\|DS]] — independence through one's own tool | [[en/four-worlds/decentralized-earth\|DE]] — order emerging through participation |

Each world's page covers what it looks like in life, its person, its strength and price, which well-known models it echoes (AQAL, Cynefin, Spiral Dynamics, generational archetypes, stages of organisational development), where it appears in history, and what the games added. At the bottom — everything from this world in the garden.

Comparisons with external models show a similarity of logic, not equivalence: each model has its own subject and axes.
